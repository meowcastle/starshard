## short description

## about this game

the moon crosses one of twenty-eight stations each night. four old maps of the sky, Arabic, Indian, Chinese and Japanese, drew those stations over the same stars without meeting. Manzil is a card game played on that road, tonight, against the sky as it stands.

### her hand is the real sky

the five planets stand where they truly stand tonight, open on the table. nothing about her is hidden. saturn locks its ground: the card is never claimed. mars strikes onward: a card it takes fights its far neighbour. venus softens both neighbours by one. mercury plays its numbers either way. jupiter counts as two. the planet standing nearest sets her temper for the night, and her judgment is seeded to the date, so her game is the same for everyone tonight and different tomorrow. nothing else is random.

### one rule

take turns lodging cards on the nine stations ahead of her. where two cards touch, the bigger number claims the smaller, and equal numbers claim too; a card taken by a tie fights on. own most of the nine when the road is full. a card lodged on its own station glows and counts as two, and the ground keeps that weight whoever takes the card.

### the walkers' road

an unclaimed station's night is a climb: eight boards against other walkers, each reading deeper than the last, and the sky herself at the top, wearing the station's own rule. on the veil's night her cards close again. on the thread's night the road wraps. the sky you play under is the only progress bar: the first board stands on warm ground with the stars barely out, the last in open space with no horizon at all. fall off a rung and the night ends there; the ladder waits where it stood for your next visit.

### every card is a receipt of a real night

beat her at the top and the station is yours: your name and the date go on the card. the stations your own chart was born under start truly owned, framed and dated. a card sheds its labels as you learn it. at first it says everything: numbers, name, move. by the end it is nearly all art. its numbers never change.

### what is not here

no packs, no currency, no energy, no streaks, no odds to disclose. all twenty-eight stations play from the first night. nothing that affects play is ever sold.

### tonight

the moon takes about twenty-seven days to walk the whole road and comes round again. every night the game is different because the world is.
