About Deck Bench

A test bench for deck changes, not affiliated with Forge or Wizards of the Coast.

What it does

Deck Bench plays your deck against the field, many games at a time, with every decision made by a rules engine, and shows what changed when you swap a card. Both sides of a change play the same shuffles, so the difference you see is the change and not the draw.

It is a bench for changes, not a prediction of a tournament. A win rate here is one engine's play against another's.

Where things come from

The games
Forge, the open-source rules engine by the Card-Forge project, licensed under the GPL-3.0. Deck Bench runs Forge as a separate program. A small patch and a bridge, both under the GPL-3.0, let Deck Bench's own pilot make some in-game decisions and set the AI's look-ahead; the rest of each game plays as Forge plays it.
Card names, text, legality, prices and images
Scryfall, through its public API. Card images are shown from Scryfall's servers.
Mana symbols
The Mana font by Andrew Gioia.
The field
Metagame lists read from MTGGoldfish and MTGTop8; Commander averages from EDHREC; your own lists from Moxfield, Archidekt or pasted text.
Real results
The open MTGODecklistCache archive of tournament decklists and results.
The coach and sideboard plans
Written by Claude, a model made by Anthropic, from your list and the field. Read them as a strong player's opinion, not a rule.
Shop links
Buy buttons go to TCGplayer and Card Kingdom.
