star shard, and manzil inside it

last updated 1 November 2026

Star Shard is one service with two surfaces: the sky, your chart, your shard and the codex of 28 stations on one side, and Manzil, the card game played against the same sky, on the other. One account covers both. This page describes what that account stores, who else sees any of it, how long it is kept, and how to get a copy of it or delete it. It describes what the software does, not what we intend it to do.

The short version. An account needs an email address, a username, a password and the facts of your birth: the date, the time if you know it, and the place. The birth date is checked against the minimum age where you live. The birth facts compute your chart; the game draws five numbers from the same chart to decide your starting cards. We sell nothing to anyone, run no advertising, and there is no analytics or tracking code anywhere in the product.

who we are

Star Shard is run by Bjur Media LLC. For anything on this page, a question, a copy of your data, a deletion or a complaint, write to privacy@starshard.net. We are the data controller for everything described below.

your birth

Making an account stores your birth date, your birth time and whether you knew it, the place name, its latitude and longitude, and its time zone. The date decides whether you are old enough. The rest is the chart: every reading, the ring of your shard and tonight's sky against it are computed from these facts each time they are shown, not kept in a table. Nothing else is derived and stored.

If you typed a place without choosing one from the list, the name is stored with no coordinates.

Game progress: cards, levels, lives, which nights you have walked, and your settingsSo the save follows you between devices

In a multiplayer match the other player sees your username and the cards you play. Nothing else about you reaches them.

your shard

The ring of your shard and the station segments you have lit on it, with the night each was lit, are stored with the account so they follow you between devices.

what we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not show advertising and we do not work with ad networks.
  • There is no analytics, no tracking pixel and no third-party tracking code anywhere in the product.
  • We do not build profiles and we make no automated decisions with legal effects about you.
  • We do not use your birth facts for anything except the age check and the astrology the product exists to do.

cookies

One cookie, starshard_session. It keeps you signed in for up to 90 days and it is the only cookie we set. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to: it is strictly necessary for a service you asked for. Signing out clears it and revokes it on the server, so a copy taken from another device stops working too.

The game also keeps a copy of your save and your settings in your browser's local storage, on your own device. That copy leaves the device only as the progress already synced to your account.

how old you have to be

At least 13, and older in some countries, because several EU and EEA states set a higher age for consenting to online services on your own: 16 in Croatia, Germany, Hungary, Ireland, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Poland, Romania, Slovakia and Slovenia; 15 in Czechia, France and Greece; 14 in Austria, Bulgaria, Cyprus, Italy, Lithuania and Spain.

Which number applies comes from your device's time zone, and your date of birth is checked against it when you sign up. That check has limits: a time zone is a guess, and a date typed into a box is not proof. It is a reasonable effort, not an identity check. If you are below the age where you live, please do not make an account. If you believe a child has made one, write to us and we will delete it.

your rights

If you are in the UK, EU or EEA, the UK GDPR and GDPR give you the right to see your data, correct it, delete it, take it elsewhere, restrict what we do with it, and object to it. We extend all of these to everyone, wherever you live.

  • A copy of everything. One file, machine-readable JSON, containing every field we hold about you. It leaves out reports other people have filed about you; your own copy must not tell you who reported you. Reset and confirmation links are left out too, since each is a live key to your account. The button is on the account page: "keep a copy of your data".
  • Deletion. Permanent and immediate. It asks for your password first, because it cannot be undone. The button is on the account page: "delete my account".

Both are on the account page, with no email needed. Write to privacy@starshard.net for a correction, a restriction or an objection, or if the account page is out of reach. We answer within a month. If you are unhappy with how we handle it you can complain to your national data protection authority; in the UK that is the ICO.

Our lawful bases: contract for your account, your chart and your saves, because there is no service without them; legal obligation for the age check; and legitimate interests for keeping the multiplayer lobby free of abuse and the service free of spam.

where your data is

Our servers are in the United States. If you are in the UK or EEA, using the service transfers your data there. Our email provider may also process your address outside your country.

changes

If how any of this works changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves. If a change is significant, new data, a new recipient or a new purpose, we tell account holders directly rather than quietly editing the page.
