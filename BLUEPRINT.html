6 · The four laws — cross-cutting, non-negotiable

The Vocabulary Law

Every locked term has a reveal tier. A term above the traveler's tier never appears on their surfaces — UI, cards, notifications, emails. Pre-Tier-1: kindle, gather, walk, cross.

The Register Law

Physics is curriculum, never engine. TRUE = cited fact · RHYME = parable voice only · QUESTION = ends grounded. "Because quantum…" is banned everywhere. Cave-arc pages always end with the floor-holds line.

The Ethics Floor

Count UP, never down · live return-countdowns · ~24h windows + grace · no paid pulls, no currency · foils only for real sky events · notifications opt-in, off by default for minors. The sky is the drop table.

Privacy & Respect

Birth data is computed in-browser and never sent — only api.js may fetch. Coined words on every surface; real traditions credited by name in the glossary; three exclusion zones (no secret-ancient-knowledge, no 2012 register, no starseed lexicon).
