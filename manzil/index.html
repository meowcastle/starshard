the whole game
take turns lodging cards on the nine mansions ahead of her. where cards touch, the bigger number claims the smaller. own most of the nine when the road is full. first to three boards takes the mansion.
a card's two numbers are its two sides: left fights left, right fights right. your ground is parchment; the sky's is amber. her hand is closed: tonight's planets, veiled until she plays them. the lead alternates each round.
levels wear away the labels: a new card says everything, level 2 drops the name, level 3 drops the move, level 4 is nearly all art. hold any card to read it in full. the throne is two-faced; choose on lodge.
dominion: a card lodged on its own mansion glows and counts as two at the count. the ground keeps it, whoever claims the card. her planets hold dominion where they truly stand tonight.
first nights: an unclaimed mansion's night is the walkers' road: five single boards against other walkers' fives, then the sky herself, wearing the mansion's own rule. fall off and the ladder waits where you left it. claimed mansions go straight to her table.
