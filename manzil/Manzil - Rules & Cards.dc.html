five laws under everything

ties flip. equal numbers claim like bigger ones, both ways. and a card taken by a tie fights on: it attacks both its own neighbours the moment it turns. adopted 20 aug 2026, the flip-density pass: 5.1 flips a board, up from 2.4.

tied counts are yours. a level road at the count falls to you: the sky already claims ties in the fight, so the draw does not tax you twice. relocked 20 aug 2026.

dominion. a card lodged on its own mansion glows ✦ and counts as two at the count. the ground keeps that weight whoever claims the card. her planets hold dominion where they truly stand tonight. jupiter counts two everywhere; on his own mansion, three. dominion is never hinted on the deck screen: spotting it is skill.

the seeded sky. her judgment is deterministic; where her arithmetic ties, a date seed breaks it. the same for everyone tonight, different tomorrow. nothing else is random.

the night's temper. one planet stands closest each night and flavors her even choices, printed in one header line, learnable in a board: mars strikes (tied, she picks the biggest fight) · saturn guards (she keeps her ground) · venus keeps company (she lodges beside the crowd) · mercury roams (she lodges apart) · jupiter spends big (she plays her largest, and his mercy is real: jupiter's nights run softer). tempers rest on road nights.

the table

nine mansions, two colors

the board is the road ahead of her: her next nine nights (she crosses one mansion a night; the board never claims otherwise). your ground is parchment; the sky's is amber. a card's two numbers are its two sides: left fights left, right fights right; claims flip the loser's color. no cascades: one comparison, once.

her hand is closed: tonight's real planets, face down until played. she is deterministic, greedy arithmetic that reads your best reply before she commits, so the same night deals the same duel for everyone.

the deck

choose your five

a persistent walking five, changed anytime, carried night to night. all 28 mansions play from day one: unclaimed ones as loaners, grey and frameless at level 1, the sky's loan. winning a mansion's night claims it: your date goes on the card and its levels unlock. every owned card is a receipt of a real night.

levels, earned by the road: L1 vanilla numbers · L2 wakes the signature move · L3–4 sharpen numbers by +1. borders richen with level, TTA-style, and the card face sheds what you no longer need: L2 drops the name, L3 drops the printed move, L4 gives the face to the art with small numbers. hold any card to read it in full. nothing is ever sold.

first nights

the walkers' road

an unclaimed mansion's night is not the sky cold. it is a short ladder: five single boards against other walkers' fives, piloted by her own judgment, then the sky herself, decently harder, wearing the mansion's own rule. the storm's night, ties are hers everywhere. the veil's night, her cards close again. the thread's night, the road wraps. the void's night, a lone card cannot be claimed.

fall off a rung and the night ends there: the road-shard is given anyway, and the ladder waits where it stood for your next visit. beat her at the top and the mansion is claimed. the ladder is lifetime-per-mansion, an introduction, never a toll. claimed nights are the standard best-of-five.

the eclipse fortnight

on the rare night an eclipse stands on the road ahead, the table may stretch: fourteen stations, eclipse to the answering lunation, a fortnight of road. seven cards each fill it. you bring seven; her hand is the five planets and two of the slow sky, standing where they truly stand tonight.

the outers play as themselves: uranus swaps two lodged cards · neptune turns her numbers to mist until played against · pluto removes one card from the road. best of three boards; the road is longer, the match is not.

fourteen is even, so a count can level. a level road falls to whoever holds the eclipse's own station. that slot is the board's anchor; hold it and ties are yours.

designed, not yet dealt: the nine-station road is the whole game. this returns only on true event nights, and only if you ask for it.

flagged 19 aug 2026, from the length sim: a longer road adds turns, not decisions (unique best moves fall 38% to 31%, flips per turn stay ~0.5, and her extra planets outgrow your extra cards). before this is ever dealt, the fortnight must earn its length through rules pressure per placement: the outers, the level count at the anchor, not stations for their own sake. re-sim at fourteen first.
