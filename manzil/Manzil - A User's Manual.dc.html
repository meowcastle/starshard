manzil

the moon crosses one of 28 mansions each night. manzil is played on the nine nights ahead of her: how to play, and every kind of night you will meet.

the whole game

you and the sky take turns lodging cards on the nine mansions ahead of her. where cards touch, the bigger number claims the smaller. own most of the nine when the road is full.

your hand: the walking five

five cards chosen from your collection. the five carries night to night and can be changed anytime. a card has two numbers, its two sides: left fights left, right fights right. your ground is parchment, hers is amber.

her hand

tonight's five planets, closed: face down until she plays them. her judgment is deterministic and seeded by the date, so her game is the same for everyone tonight and different tomorrow. each planet keeps its signature:

saturn · its ground is locked: the card is never claimed
mars · its claim strikes onward: a claimed card fights its far neighbor
venus · softens both neighbors' facing numbers by one
mercury · picks its better face
jupiter · counts as two at the count

the night's temper

one planet stands closest each night and flavors how she breaks her even choices. the header prints it in one line, and it is learnable in a single board:

mars strikes · tied, she picks the biggest fight, lodging beside your strongest numbers
saturn guards · tied, she keeps her ground
venus keeps company · tied, she lodges beside the crowd
mercury roams · tied, she lodges apart
jupiter spends big · she plays her largest, and his mercy is real: jupiter's nights run softer

the count

when the ninth card lodges, count the road. most of nine wins the board. tied counts are hers: the sky keeps what you do not take. dominion: a card lodged on its own mansion glows ✦ and counts as two, and the ground keeps that weight whoever claims the card. her planets hold dominion where they truly stand tonight. jupiter counts two everywhere; on his own mansion, three.

levels

cards level by walking the road: wins level fastest, visits count too. a card sheds its labels as you learn it.

level 1 · says everything: numbers, name, move
level 2 · drops the name. the signature move wakes
level 3 · drops the printed move. one number sharpens
level 4 · nearly all art. both numbers sharpened

first signatures, waking at level 2: the storm claims ties · the heart fights +1 while the sky holds more of the road · the blaze's ground stays yours · the throne is two-faced · the jewel cannot be softened · the crown counts two on edge mansions · the hideaway's mansion counts for nobody. all 28 earn a move eventually.

the nights

a claimed mansion's night

the standard match: best of five boards against the sky, the lead alternating each round. first to three boards takes the night. win, and a game-shard is yours. lose, and nothing is lost. play as many matches as the night holds.

a first night: the walkers' road

an unclaimed mansion's night is not the sky cold. it is a short ladder: five single boards against other walkers' fives, piloted by her own judgment, ending at the sky herself, decently harder, wearing the mansion's own rule. the storm's night, ties are hers. the veil's night, her cards close again. the thread's night, the road wraps. the void's night, a lone card cannot be claimed.

fall off a rung and the night ends there: the road-shard is given anyway, and the ladder waits where it stood for your next visit. beat her at the top and the mansion is claimed: your name and the date go on the card, its levels unlock, and the ladder never returns. tempers rest on these nights; her rule is the whole lesson.

shards

two currencies, never mixed. the road-shard comes every night the moon crosses, win or lose: the 28 are never skill-gated. game-shards come only from match wins and buy nothing that affects the collection.
