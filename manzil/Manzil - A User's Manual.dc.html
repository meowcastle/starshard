manzil

the moon crosses one of 28 mansions each night. manzil is played on the nine nights ahead of her: how to play, and every kind of night you will meet.

the whole game

you and the sky take turns lodging cards on the nine mansions ahead of her. where cards touch, the bigger number claims the smaller. own most of the nine when the road is full.

your hand: the walking twelve

you carry twelve mansions, changed anytime, and five are dealt from them at the start of every board, face up. the deal is seeded to the night: the same for everyone tonight, different tomorrow. tonight's mansion always walks with you when you carry it. a card has two numbers, its two sides: left fights left, right fights right. your ground is parchment, hers is amber.

your chart's own cards start truly owned: framed, dated, able to level. every other mansion is a loaner, grey and frameless at level 1, the sky's loan, playable from day one. the throne is two-faced: you choose which number faces which way when you lodge it.

her hand

tonight's five planets, open: five lights you can tell apart, so her hand is never a secret. her judgment is deterministic and seeded by the date, so her game is the same for everyone tonight and different tomorrow. each planet keeps its signature:

saturn · its ground is locked: the card is never claimed
mars · its claim strikes onward: a claimed card fights its far neighbor
venus · softens both neighbors' facing numbers by one
mercury · picks its better face
jupiter · counts as two at the count

the night's temper

one planet stands closest each night and flavors how she breaks her even choices. the header prints it in one line, and it is learnable in a single board:

mars strikes · tied, she picks the biggest fight, lodging beside your strongest numbers
saturn guards · tied, she keeps her ground
venus keeps company · tied, she lodges beside the crowd
mercury roams · tied, she lodges apart
jupiter spends big · she plays her largest, and his mercy is real: jupiter's nights run softer

the count

when the ninth card lodges, count the road. most of nine wins the board. tied counts are hers: the sky keeps what you do not take. dominion: a card lodged on its own mansion glows ✦ and counts as two, and the ground keeps that weight whoever claims the card. her planets hold dominion where they truly stand tonight. jupiter counts two everywhere; on his own mansion, three.

levels

cards level by walking the road: wins level fastest, visits count too. a card sheds its labels as you learn it. its numbers never change: the card is the card from night one.

level 1 · says everything: numbers, name, move
level 2 · drops the name. the signature move wakes
level 3 · drops the printed move
level 4 · nearly all art

every mansion carries a move, waking at level 2. a few: the storm cannot be tied on its own mansion · the heart, while it stands, lets nothing of yours be softened · the blaze's ground stays yours · the throne is two-faced · the jewel cannot be softened · the crown counts two on edge mansions · the hideaway closes the loudest mansion beside it. the full slate is on the rules sheet.

the nights

a claimed mansion's night

the mansion is yours, and its nights go back to the walkers: a best of five against one of the road's own, the same face each time you come here, the lead alternating each round. first to three boards takes the night. win, and a game-shard is yours. lose, and nothing is lost. play as many matches as the night holds.

the sky herself returns only when the moon crosses one of your own chart's mansions. then she sits for the whole match, and her temperament sits with her.

a first night: the walkers' road

an unclaimed mansion's night is not the sky cold. it is a climb: eight single boards against other walkers' hands, piloted by her own judgment and reading deeper at every rung, ending at the sky herself, a best of five, wearing the mansion's own rule. the storm's night, ties are hers. the veil's night, her cards close again. the thread's night, the road wraps. the void's night, a lone card cannot be claimed.

nothing counts the climb for you. the first board stands on the ground, warm and low, the stars barely out; from there the low air, the dusk, the cloud line, the high air, the cold air, the thin air, the edge of the air. she waits in open space. the sky you are playing under is how far you have come.

fall off a rung and the night ends there: the road-shard is given anyway, and the ladder waits where it stood for your next visit. beat her at the top and the mansion is claimed: your name and the date go on the card, its levels unlock, and the ladder never returns. tempers rest on these nights; her rule is the whole lesson.

shards

two currencies, never mixed. the road-shard comes every night the moon crosses, win or lose: the 28 are never skill-gated. game-shards come only from match wins and buy nothing that affects the collection.
