Four rules that are easy to get wrong

1 · Button labels are teal-900. Never white.
White on pink-500 is 2.55:1 and on teal-500 is 2.23:1 — both fail. teal-900 on the same fills is 6.08 / 6.93. The site currently ships white-on-gradient buttons.
white label ✕ 2.55 teal-900 label ✓ 6.08
2 · Focus ring is pink-700 on light, pink-500 on dark.
pink-500 on cream is 2.39:1 — under the 3:1 non-text minimum. 3px solid, 2px offset, never outline:none.
3 · butter-200 is never text.
Surface and highlight only. For warm text use amber-700 (4.99:1 worst case).
4 · Bevel dark edge is #4E8C86 or darker.
Lighter fails the 3:1 boundary requirement — #5E9C96 misses at 2.95:1.

§2 · type

Three families, three jobs

Pixelify Sans
The retro carrier. Titles, eyebrows, metadata, labels. Appears on every surface — phone, card, email — even where there is no chrome. Treat it as the logo. Never body copy.
Baloo 2
Display and headings. The warm, chunky voice. 700/800 only. Never long paragraphs.
Varela Round
Body and UI. Readings, form labels, helper text. Never headlines.
