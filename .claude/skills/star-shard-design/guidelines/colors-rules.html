• Amber (#FFB000) is reserved for "tonight" only. Never a button, never a generic accent — the moment it appears anywhere else, it stops meaning anything.
  • Parchment (#F0D89A) is the accent for everything else: links, borders, secondary emphasis, dividers.
  • Primary ink (#F2EAD6) is body/headings. Muted ink (#9A8A5E) is meta text — eyebrows, counts, labels.
  • Error/status text (#E8A87C) is the only status color. There is no separate warning tone.
  • Borders are parchment at low opacity (rgba(240,216,154,.1–.3)), not a solid gray or black.
