the voice

  • Show it. Don't introduce it. Cut throat-clearing openers, meta-commentary, "here's the part almost nobody knows."
  • The reader is the subject. Second person, present tense. Attribute every value judgment — never state an opinion as fact.
  • "We" appears nowhere. No first person, ever. The product never narrates its own trustworthiness or method.
  • Lowercase headings, sentence-case body. "tonight" not "Tonight's Reading."
  • No em dashes in copy, headers or body. Colon, period, or comma pair instead.
  • Honest about uncertainty. "we'll skip your rising sign rather than guess it" — never invented precision.
  • Say the real astrology term, defined once in four words, used forever after. One new coined noun per surface, max.
