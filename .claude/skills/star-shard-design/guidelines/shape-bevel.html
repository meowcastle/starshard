retired in v2.0

no bevel, no press-translate

v1.0's 2px light/dark bevel (flipping on press, translating the element 2px into the page) is gone. Buttons and panels in the current system are flat 1px parchment-opacity borders with sharp corners; the only interaction feedback is a color/opacity shift on hover, and a soft glow on the armed/emphasized state — see colors-foil.html. Nothing translates or inverts its border on press.
