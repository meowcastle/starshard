no 64px hero, no 200px numeral

v1.0 documented two hard exceptions to its type scale (a 64px phone hero, a 200px tarot numeral). The current live product doesn't have either — its largest heading found in code is 42px, and the smallest label is 11px. Don't invent a new outsized numeral or hero treatment without a real screen that needs one; the empirical range above (see type-scale.html) already covers everything shipped today.
