# WRITING.md — the house style

**One page. Binding on the corpus, the UI copy, and the generative layer.**

## The law

**Show it. Don't introduce it, frame it, or explain why it matters.**

If a sentence's job is to prepare the reader for the next sentence, cut
it and let the next sentence do its own work.

## My tics — cut on sight

| Tic | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Announcing the reveal** | "and here's the part almost nobody knows…" | just say the part |
| **Labelling the move** | "the cost is…", "that is your hunger…" | make the move |
| **Not-X-but-Y** | "this isn't restlessness, it's…" | say what it is |
| **Meta-commentary** | "this page can show its work here" | show the work |
| **Explaining the payoff** | "…which is what makes it meaningful" | let them have it |
| **Throat-clearing openers** | "so", "and then there is", "worth knowing:" | delete |
| **Double-stating** | image, then the image explained | keep the image |

## Tests

1. **Delete the first sentence of any paragraph.** If it still works,
   it was throat-clearing.
2. **Find every "which means" and "that's why."** Most are the writer
   thinking out loud.
3. **Count the abstract nouns per 100 words.** Hunger, cost, tension,
   register, arrangement. Over four, rewrite with objects and verbs.
4. **Read it as a stranger.** Anything requiring a previous sentence to
   parse is a dependency, and dependencies are where people drop.

## Whose voice this is

**Star Shard has no opinions.** The traditions have opinions. The sky
has positions. The reader has a life. We are the instrument, not the
personality.

The failure mode is specific: an astrology product that gradually
becomes the author's views with astrology attached. It's an easy drift,
because a strong voice is what makes writing good — and the moment that
voice has a position on anything outside the reader, the reader stops
being the subject.

1. **The reader is the subject of every sentence.** If a line is about
   us, the world, or an idea, turn it toward them or cut it.
2. **Attribute every value judgment.** *"The old election books called
   this the best station"* — never *"this is the best station."* The
   traditions get to have views. We report them.
3. **No commentary on politics, current events, or culture.** The sky
   doesn't have takes.
4. **Name the tension; don't prescribe the virtue.** We say what's hard
   about being this way. We never say what they should become.
5. **"We" is allowed only about method** — *we compute, we don't guess,
   we won't guess your birth time.* Never *we believe.*
6. **The universality test.** Would this line read identically to
   someone whose politics, faith and life look nothing like the
   writer's? If not, it's an opinion wearing a reading.

## Special, not flattered

Making someone feel special is the whole product. There are two ways and
only one survives contact.

**Flattery doesn't survive comparison.** Co–Star got caught because
users compared screenshots and found identical phrasing. Praise is
generic by construction — it has to be, to be safe.

**Recognition does survive.** The four mechanisms, all of which we
already have:

- **Accuracy that includes the cost.** *"You are chronically early, and
  being early looks identical to being wrong."* Nobody screenshots a
  compliment; they screenshot being caught.
- **Rarity that's real and checkable.** Seedborn is genuinely 3.4%. The
  full moon nearest your birthday genuinely rises in your opposite sky.
  Claimed rarity is worthless; computed rarity is a gift.
- **Specificity nobody could have guessed** — the drafts folder, how
  people take their coffee, what they said they were afraid of in 2019.
- **The recognition beat.** *"You probably already knew most of that.
  That isn't a trick — it's the point."* Handing someone their own
  knowledge back, in words they didn't have to find, is the feeling
  we're selling.

## Length is a feature, not a virtue

The Deep Chart earns ~1,000 words because someone chose to read it. The
arrival earns ~450. A daily earns 60. **When in doubt, the shorter
version is the one that gets finished.**

## What stays long

The named cost in a gait passage. The disagreement between traditions.
The one uncanny verifiable fact. Those are the product. Everything
around them is packaging, and packaging should be thin.
