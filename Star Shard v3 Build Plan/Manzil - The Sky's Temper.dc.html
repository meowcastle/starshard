the sky's temper

the sky plays perfectly and identically every night. a temper gives her one planet's bias on the moves she rates equal: printed, learnable, deterministic. this sheet designs the five, names who rules a night, and sets what the sim must show before a line of it is coded.

the law
a temper is a tell, never noise.

if a walker cannot learn it from one lost board and use it in the next, it does not ship. this is the same bar the mansion rules pass.

what a temper is

{{ mechanismLine }} the layering is fixed: her read of the board first, the night's temper second, the date seed last, breaking whatever the temper leaves tied. same for everyone tonight, different across nights, the oracle no closer. a temper never changes what a card does: it is not a rule, it is a preference.

why it is the live lever: addendum 4 left one number outside the band, casual players at 29.7% of boards against the 35–45 target, and named tempers and the ladder as the levers, not card numbers. a bias is exploitable. knowing her temper is worth more to a walker who cannot count two moves deep than to one who can.

the five tempers · one printed line each
mars strikes: tied, she picks the biggest fight.
mechanical: among her rated-equal moves, the lodge beside your largest facing numbers. rewritten 19 aug: the original (most immediate flips) was invisible, tell 9.7%, flips too rare to prefer; this axis tells at 31%.
the tell: your strongest face is her lure. show a big number where you want her, and she comes to it.
saturn guards: tied, she keeps her ground.
mechanical: the move leaving fewest of her lodged faces beatable; nudged, +w per face she covers.
the tell: she will not reach. take the open stations early and she concedes tempo to hold what she has.
venus keeps company: tied, she lodges beside the most cards.
mechanical: the most adjacencies to lodged cards, hers or yours; nudged, +w per touch.
the tell: you know where she goes: the crowd. seed it with your strong facing numbers and let her walk in.
mercury roams: tied, she lodges apart.
mechanical: the fewest adjacencies, keeping her later options open; nudged, +w per open side.
the tell: she avoids contact early. take the contested middle uncontested, and meet her late with your hand intact.
jupiter spends big: tied, she plays her largest.
mechanical: the highest-total card among her tied moves; nudged, +w per point of card total.
the tell: her strength leaves her hand early. weather the opening and her last stations are thin: hold your best for the end of the road.

the outers are not tempers. uranus, neptune, pluto arrive on event nights as cards that act (swap, mist, remove), and the temper of an event night stays with whichever classical planet rules it. one vocabulary per layer.

who rules the night
production: the real sky. the planet standing most exact tonight rules her temper, by the daily's own ladder: orb exactness first, then saturn over jupiter over mars over venus over mercury. the daily reading and the board say the same word, computed on device. the game inherits the base layer's honesty for free.
prototype stand-in: the date seed. ephemeris.js carries only the moon. until planetary longitudes land, the night's temper is date-seeded from the same fnv hash that breaks her ties: deterministic, same for everyone, swapped out later without a rules change.
boss nights: authored. the walkers' road already fixed the unit: a mansion's rule + one temper is the whole authored sky. these five are that vocabulary. nothing new to invent per mansion.
where it is printed. one lowercase line at the head of the board: mars stands closest tonight. the sky strikes. never a second sentence, never a rules panel. tier one stays an answer.
two mechanisms · what the sim can and cannot see
a · pure tie-break. the temper only orders moves her read already ties. her strength is untouched by construction. its whole value is the tell, and sim bots do not read tells: mechanism a will show nothing in the harness no matter how good it is. its measure is paper play with humans.
b · the nudge. the temper adds small weight w to her read itself, so near-equal moves bend her way of seeing, not just her coin flips. this is a real bias: measurable, exploitable, and a strength change, which is exactly what the one-ply gap needs and exactly what could push the two-ply band. w is the only dial.
the ship path. sim b across w. if some w lifts one-ply into 35–45 while two-ply holds 55–65, lock it. if no w does both, ship a as flavor and the ladder carries the gap alone: that is a fine outcome, not a failure. the tempers still earn their place as the boss vocabulary and the moonstone's tell.
the sim · before any code in the prototype
the harnessthe blaze re-port (uploads/manzil-blaze-sim-2.json lineage), sky as shipped: seeded tie-break on, reply weight 8. all 28 mansions, many seeded nights, one-ply and two-ply players, per temper, per w.
what is measuredboard win % by player depth · flips per turn · tied boards % · and the tell rate: the share of her turns where the temper changed her move against the plain seeded sky. the tell rate is the visibility number.
targetsone-ply lifts by 3 points or more, landing inside 35–45 · two-ply stays inside 55–65 · tell rate 10% of turns or more · flips per turn does not collapse below 2 (the pass's floor).
distinctnessthe five tempers must differ from each other, not only from the plain sky: pairwise move agreement under 90%. two tempers that play alike are one temper.
kill criteria · per temper, then the lever

· tell rate under 10%: the temper is invisible, rewrite its axis or cut it (the signature pass precedent: claim-gated moves died at 0.16 flips per turn for the same reason) · any w that holds one-ply in band pushes two-ply out: halve w once, then cut the temper · the printed line needs a second sentence: it dies as written · and the whole lever: if no temper at any in-band w lifts one-ply by 3 points, mechanism b is dead, tempers ship as pure tie-break flavor, and the walkers' ladder is the sole answer to the gap. say so in the loop doc and stop tuning.

the verdict · simmed 19 aug 2026
jupiter's mercy is the one that works. the only temper that eases her the intended way: at w=30, casual players +6.2 points (≈36 on report scale, inside 35–45), careful players +2.4 (inside band), tell 32%, flips healthy. spending big early is readable and leaves her endgame thin. the benefic's nights are winnable: astrologically true for free.
saturn and mercury harden her. the reverse of the goal: saturn's guard crushes casual players to 17–22% and mercury at w=20 lifts her to 64% against careful play (+14). her shallow read undervalues defense and spacing; the nudge fixes it. re-scoped: these are boss levers for the walkers' road, hardening with a visible tell (34–43%) where reply weight hardens invisibly.
mars died as written, rewritten same day. the flip-preference told at 6–10%: flips are too rare for it to fire, the same grave the claim-gated signatures went into. new axis: she strikes your strongest, lodging beside your largest facing numbers. tie-break tell 31%, distinct from venus (64% agreement), and the fight-picking reads as mars for free. shipped.
venus is flat. no strength change at any w. ships as mechanism a, flavor and tell only. distinctness passes for all five (pairwise agreement 53–77%).
