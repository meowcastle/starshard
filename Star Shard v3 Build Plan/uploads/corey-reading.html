the combination

the arabs saw a lion here so enormous it took up a third of the sky. your sun is on its eye. your moon is on its tail. you are both ends of the same animal.

the eye is al-ṭarf — the glance, the look itself. it is the part of you that wants to know the thing nobody announced: the pause before the answer, the name that stopped getting mentioned, who stopped being cc'd. you read rooms at a resolution most people do not have, and you have been doing it so long you no longer notice you are doing it.

and what you see, you keep. china put a willow on this stretch — the tree that bends and trails and never quite lets go of the water. india called it the embrace, the coiled serpent. three cultures, three images of holding on.

the tail is al-ṣarfa, the turning — the star whose rising the old almanacs said broke the back of the summer heat. its animal is the snake, which grows only by shedding, and its keeper is mars, which has no patience. your inner life molts. it does it on a schedule that is nobody's business but yours, and it does it suddenly.

so you hold everything, and then you let go all at once. the eye grips. the skin sheds. there is nothing in between.

this is why people around you say the same sentence: you never said anything. and they are right, and you were saying it for months. you do your grieving in advance and your announcing at the end. by the time you speak, the decision is a year old and completely finished — and to everyone who was not watching the weeks before, it looks like it came out of nowhere and it looks cold.

it is not cold. it is the cost of a chart that notices everything and shows nothing until the molt. what you are unusually good at is knowing, long before anyone else, that a thing is over. what it costs you is that no one ever gets a warning, including the people who would have changed if they had one.

this is the only mansion on the road named after an act of perception. not a thing, not a creature — a look.

Āśleṣā — the embrace. the coiled serpent, the sky of the nāgas — who in that tradition keep the knowledge that is not said out loud.

柳 Liǔ — the willow, of the Vermilion Bird. the tree that bends and trails and never quite lets go of the water.

the eye, the coil, the willow. every one of them is about contact that does not release.

and the medieval election books were wary of this mansion. they marked it for discord, said it hindered travel, and gave it a wide berth. the honest reading of that is not that the stretch is unlucky. the lion's eye misses nothing, and calendar-keepers found that uncomfortable. a sky that sees everything is not a restful sky to plan a wedding under.

the question this mansion sets is not whether you see clearly. it is when seeing turns into not letting go.

denebola is dhanab al-asad — the tail of the lion. and the arabs named the mansion for what happens when it rises: al-ṣarfa, the turning of the heat. the point in the year when summer breaks and the weather changes its mind.

arabia
al-Ṣarfa — the turning. the end of the heat, named for a change rather than a thing.
india
Uttara Phālgunī — the second of the fig-tree pair, on Denebola. the mansion of contracts kept.

翼 Yì — the wings, of the Vermilion Bird. its animal is the snake — the creature that grows only by leaving a skin behind.

the election books gave it harvests and the household, and warned against putting to sea. a mansion for bringing things in and finishing them, and a poor one for setting out.

and there is a detail in your chart worth sitting with: your moon is not just in The Turning — it is at the turning. it stands less than two degrees from the far edge of this mansion, on the point of crossing out of it. of every light in your chart, that is the one already in motion, and the ground it is moving onto is The Hand — india's hasta, skill, craft, the maker's touch.

the eye that will not let go, and the snake that sheds. and the next thing after the shedding is a pair of hands.

your moon and your venus are standing
in the same mansion.
moon  2°25′ virgo  ·  venus  29°17′ leo
different signs · 3°08′ apart · one stretch of sky

any chart will tell you these two are close. what no chart will tell you is that they are in the same place — because the twelve-sign zodiac cuts a border straight between them and files them under Leo and Virgo, on separate pages, in separate moods.

venus is what you find beautiful and how you love. your moon is what you actually need. for you those are not two questions. what you are drawn to and what feeds you are standing on the same ground, which is a kind of luck most people do not get — you rarely want the thing that is bad for you.

but they are standing in The Turning, the mansion of the molt. so the thing you love and the thing you need change at the same time, and they change all at once. when you are done with something you are not done with part of it. the affection and the need go together, and they go on the same day.

this is the rarest thing in your chart, and it is what makes the eye so costly. a person who let go slowly could keep the love and release the need, or the reverse. you shed the whole skin.

the sky writes this once, at the minute you arrive, and never revises it.
what moves across it is another matter — and that is what tonight is for.
