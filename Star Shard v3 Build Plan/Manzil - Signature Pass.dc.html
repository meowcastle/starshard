the rule that outlived the pass
a −1 spends a tie. ties pay twice now, flipping in the fight and falling to you at the count, so every softening move has to be scored net of the ties it destroys.

it is why the empty district was negative while it softened, and why the softening family does not stack: a second dimming move is worth nothing next to the first. the ghost keeps its −1 and pays for it with a condition that expires as the road fills.
