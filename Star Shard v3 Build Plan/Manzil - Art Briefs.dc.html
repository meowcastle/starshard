art briefs

one artist, one hand, all twenty-eight: cohesion beats polish. each mansion is a seed of light: its real asterism as the constellation mark, iconic not illustrative, drawn to read at 58×78 px. names are canon from the product's naming slate; every brief starts from what the card does in play.

the envelope

parchment #F0D89A ink on the dark ground (#241B08 → #0B0703). amber #FFB000 is reserved for the sky and provenance, never in base card art. no other hues in the base set; the artist series may break this, the base 28 may not.

the frame owns the edges

art lives in the middle window; the two numbers (top-left, bottom-right), the name band, and the level frame sit above it. compose center-weighted with quiet corners: the frame will crop nothing that matters. every card is living: one slow ambient motion, the archetype's breath, never faster than weather. deliverable as a 2-layer file (still + motion layer) per card.

the twenty-eight
