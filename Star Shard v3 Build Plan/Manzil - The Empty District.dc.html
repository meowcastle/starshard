the whole game
the board is the moon's road: the nine mansions ahead of her, the houses she crosses one a night. you and the sky, the hand that deals the planets, take turns setting cards on them. where cards touch, the bigger number claims the smaller. own most of the nine when the road is full. first to three boards takes the night.
a card's two numbers are its two sides: left fights left, right fights right. equal numbers claim too: a tie takes the card. your ground is parchment; the sky's is amber. her hand is open: tonight's planets, five lights you can tell apart. the lead alternates each round.
a card's numbers never change: it fights the same on its first night as its hundredth. levels wake its signature at 2, then wear away the labels: a new card says everything, level 2 drops the name, level 3 drops the move, level 4 is nearly all art. hold any card to read it in full. the throne is two-faced; choose on lodge.
dominion: a card lodged on its own mansion glows and counts as two at the count. the ground keeps it, whoever claims the card. her planets hold dominion where they truly stand tonight.
the climb: an unclaimed mansion's night is a road of nine steps: eight single boards against other players' hands, each reading deeper than the last, then the sky herself at the top, best of five. beat her and the mansion's card is won at level one. the road re-arms with the moon; each new climb wakes the card one level, and at level four the mansion is claimed for good. fall off and the ladder waits where you left it. after the claim: the mansion is yours and its nights go back to the walkers, best of five. the sky herself returns only when the moon crosses one of your chart's own mansions, and then she stays for a whole match.
