the whole game
take turns lodging cards on the nine mansions ahead of her. where cards touch, the bigger number claims the smaller. own most of the nine when the road is full. first to three boards takes the mansion.
a card's two numbers are its two sides: left fights left, right fights right. your ground is parchment; the sky's is amber. her hand is open: tonight's planets, five lights you can tell apart. the lead alternates each round.
a card's numbers never change: it fights the same on its first night as its hundredth. levels wake its signature at 2, then wear away the labels: a new card says everything, level 2 drops the name, level 3 drops the move, level 4 is nearly all art. hold any card to read it in full. the throne is two-faced; choose on lodge.
dominion: a card lodged on its own mansion glows and counts as two at the count. the ground keeps it, whoever claims the card. her planets hold dominion where they truly stand tonight.
first nights: an unclaimed mansion's night is the walkers' road: eight single boards against other walkers' hands, each reading deeper than the last, then the sky herself at the top, best of five, wearing the mansion's own rule. beat her and the mansion is claimed, its card waking at level 2. fall off and the ladder waits where you left it. the climb is daylight rising into open space. after the claim: the mansion is yours and its nights go back to the walkers, best of five. the sky herself returns only when the moon crosses one of your chart's own mansions, and then she stays for a whole match.
