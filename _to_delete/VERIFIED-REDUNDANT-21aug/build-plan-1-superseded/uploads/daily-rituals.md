# Daily-ritual games that last: what the evidence says, and what it means for Manzil

*Research notes for MANZIL-LOOP.md · agent report, 19 Aug 2026. ~30 searches/fetches. Items marked [lit] are canonical published works cited from knowledge of the text, not from a fetched page.*

## 1. The NYT Games ecosystem

**Disclosed scale.** 11.2B puzzle plays in 2025 (11.1B in 2024, 8B in 2023). Wordle: 4.8B (2023) → 5.3B (2024) → 4.2B (2025, ~11.5M/day — a real year-over-year decline that Jonathan Knight calls "very healthy"). Connections 1.6B successful completions, Strands 1.5B, Mini 1.4B in 2025. "Over 50% of weekly users play multiple puzzles daily; over 25% play four or more." Games passed 1M standalone subs by end-2021. Sources: [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91539885/wordle-statistics-show-why-new-york-times-is-turning-game-into-nbc-tv-show), [A Media Operator](https://www.amediaoperator.com/analysis/wordle-tv-show-new-york-times-games-jonathan-knight/), [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/new-york-times-games-wordle-crossplay-design-jonathan-knight/). The Wordle NBC game show (2026) is explicitly framed as a tool to "re-energize lapsed players" — even NYT treats Wordle as a maturing, churning habit.

**Wordle.** Wardle's stated rationale was scarcity — "one puzzle per day… leaving players wanting more" — no app, no notifications; the emoji share grid was invented by players ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wordle)). Hard mode: 20–25% of WordleBot-tracked games ([WordFinder](https://wordfinder.yourdictionary.com/blog/wordle-hard-mode-what-is-it-who-plays-it-and-should-you-play-it-too/)). Failure mode: the streak. A single word ("corer", Oct 15 2024) ended 5.6M streaks ([AOL/Parade](https://www.aol.com/exclusive-wordle-puzzle-ended-5-013624721.html)); the 2022 migration that broke streaks generated real anger ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/wordle-new-york-times-streaks-broken-nyt-migration-dail-1848525236)). NYT offers no streak freeze.

**Connections.** Raph Koster's critique: Connections tests *crystallized* trivia, not learnable verbs; "a missed guess doesn't help you prune the logic space, only the trivia space," so playing more doesn't make you better ([raphkoster.com](https://www.raphkoster.com/2023/09/02/why-nyts-connections-makes-you-feel-bad/)).

**Spelling Bee.** Ranks up to Genius, with Queen Bee as an unadvertised Easter egg. "About 25% of players achieve Genius at least once in a week" ([Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/reading/the-genius-of-the-new-york-times-spelling-bee/)); Genius-or-higher achieved 154M times in 2024. It has *no clean end* — you stop at the rank you're satisfied with. Failure mode: score ceilings ([Spelling Bee Times](https://spellingbeetimes.com/2026/04/04/spelling-bee-score-ceilings-why-you-cant-always-reach-queen-bee/)).

**The Crossword's weekly ramp.** Monday and Sunday draw the most solvers, Tuesday/Wednesday have the highest completion rates, Thursday the biggest variance, Friday/Saturday the lowest completion. Shortz: "you go as far as you find the puzzles pleasurable and when they're no longer fun, you stop" ([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2016/hard-new-york-times-crossword-startup-finds-answer-first-ever-data-analysis/)). The ramp gives the week a *shape* and a ladder for identity ("I'm a Thursday solver").

**Strands**: a no-fail puzzle with a soft cost. Bogost: newspaper games' purpose is to "make the player feel smarter than they really are" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times_Strands)).

**Why the NYT stack retains:** identical puzzle for everyone, finite daily content (a stopping rule built into the artifact), a shareable result that hides the answer, a weekly or rank-based difficulty ladder for identity, and the *portfolio*.

## 2. Chess puzzles and Duolingo leagues

**Chess.com** recalibrated ratings by replaying ~17 *billion* historical puzzle attempts ([Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/news/view/announcing-new-puzzles-rating-system)). **Lichess** Puzzle Storm and Puzzle Streak ([lichess.org/page/storm](https://lichess.org/page/storm), [lichess.org/streak](https://lichess.org/streak)). Common pattern: a *number that is yours and only moves through play*. Failure mode: score-chasing decouples from skill ([Chess.com forum](https://www.chess.com/forum/view/general/doing-puzzle-storm-on-lichess-org-daily-but-no-significant-improvement)).

**Duolingo leagues.** Weekly 30-person leaderboards, ten tiers; opt-out added ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-leagues-leaderboards/)). Deconstructor of Fun credits leagues with +25% lesson completion but: "loss aversion does the heavy lifting… every week, the user is either climbing or defending" ([DoF](https://duolingo.deconstructoroffun.com/mechanics/leagues)). Backfire documented in user venting ([My Senpai](https://my-senpai.com/insights/why-people-quit-duolingo.html)) — no peer-reviewed quantification found.

## 3. Daily-seed roguelikes

**Spelunky**: the single attempt "fundamentally shifts behavior from aggressive exploration to calculated caution" ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-understated-genius-of-the-i-spelunky-i-daily-challenge)). Zach Gage: "If we let you play a thousand games, it wouldn't be interesting. It becomes more interesting the more constricted it is" ([Six Colors](https://sixcolors.com/post/2024/08/interview-game-developer-zach-gage-on-pile-up-poker-and-resisting-dark-patterns/)). **StS2** switched the daily leaderboard to friends-only by default after cheating complaints ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2868840/discussions/0/798966745989725135/)). **Balatro** daily challenge announced, no specifics ([GoNintendo](https://www.gonintendo.com/contents/33117-balatro-to-get-a-daily-challenge-mode)).

**Does a shared seed drive retention?** No developer-disclosed participation share found — **unverified**. Honest reading: a daily seed is a *cheap, durable community hook for the enthusiast tail*, not a mass-retention engine.

## 4. The comfort long-tail

**Desert Golfing**: "I wanted people to feel like they were on a journey… you can make progress if you have literally 10 seconds to spare… no menu, no restarts" ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/7-questions-for-i-desert-golfing-i-creator-justin-smith)); one player pursued 21,000 holes ([Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/one-players-21000-hole-quest-to-beat-the-seemingly-endless-desert-golf/)). **Zach Gage / Puzzmo**: "if a game is fun for 10 hours, it'll be good enough for Puzzmo. Because that's a lot of hours stretched out once a day over a year"; growth throttled to 500 new players/day ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/puzzmo-co-creator-zach-gage-on-building-newspaper-games-that-can-last-forever)). Pile-Up Poker caps at five hands a day *because* "you could burn out on it". **Tetris Effect: Connected** "Full Moon" weekend event — whether it tracks real lunar phase **not verified** ([GameFAQs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/296457-tetris-effect-connected/faqs/78800/others)). **Words With Friends** added daily solo puzzles; ~70% of players now start a session with the solo puzzle ([Zynga](https://www.zynga.com/blog/words-with-friends-adds-letter-lock-mode-your-newest-daily-wordplay-obsession/)).

## 5. Real-world-clock / found-content games

**Pokémon GO, new facts only.** 20M weekly actives, ~10M daily, "approximately half of all players log in seven days per week" (Niantic, Mar 2025); Scopely bought the games unit for $3.5B (2025); lifetime store revenue $9.1B at the July 2026 10th anniversary ([PocketGamer.biz 2025](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/pokmon-go-has-20m-weekly-active-players-nearly-nine-years-after-its-global-launch/), [PocketGamer.biz 2026](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/pokemon-go-celebrates-10th-anniversary-with-over-9bn-in-revenue/)). **Pikmin Bloom**: $44M lifetime by Apr 2024; Niantic called it the most retentive game in mobile (claim, no figure) ([GoNintendo](https://www.gonintendo.com/contents/60472-pikmin-bloom-devs-tout-the-game-as-the-most-retentive-in-the-mobile-industry)). **Sky: CotL**: Chen: "every monetisation system is itself an interactive gameplay system" ([MCV](https://mcvuk.com/business-news/pennies-from-heaven-thatgamecompanys-jenova-chen-on-monetisation-in-sky-children-of-the-light/)). **Neopets**: 400K MAU / ~250K DAU in 2024 ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/neopets-hits-400000-monthly-active-users-tripling-growth-in-2024/)). **Immaculate Grid**: ~200K weekday players by July 2023, acquired by Sports Reference ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immaculate_Grid), [Sports Reference](https://www.sports-reference.com/blog/2023/07/sports-reference-acquires-immaculate-grid/)). **ESPN Streak** ([ESPN](https://fantasy.espn.com/streak/en/)). **Death Stranding**: asynchronous, anonymous, *only positive* social traces ([Fandom wiki](https://deathstranding.fandom.com/wiki/Social_Strand_System)).

The unifying property: **when the real world authors the content, the designer's content budget is infinite and the player's pull is external.**

## 6. Psychology

- **Zeigarnik/Ovsiankina.** 2025 meta-analysis (38 + 20 studies): Zeigarnik *memory* effect essentially null (recall ratio 0.99); Ovsiankina *resumption* effect robust — interrupted tasks resumed ~67% of the time ([Nature HSSC](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-025-05000-w)).
- **Hooked (Eyal) and critics** ([Yu-kai Chou](https://yukaichou.com/gamification-analysis/hook-model-octalysis-habit-addiction/)). Wordle is the counter-model: fixed reward, fixed cadence, zero investment traps.
- **SDT / PENS** (Rigby & Ryan [lit]). Oxford telemetry: 39K players, 2022, time played has negligible effect on wellbeing ([Royal Society Open Science](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rsos/article/9/7/220411/96718/Time-spent-playing-video-games-is-unlikely-to)); 703 Switch players, 140K hours, 2025 — *perceived "gaming life fit"* predicts wellbeing, hours don't ([OII](https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/news-events/its-quality-not-quantity-that-predicts-gamers-wellbeing-new-study-finds/)). Przybylski & Weinstein Goldilocks study ([Psych Science](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0956797616678438)).
- **Vallerand harmonious vs obsessive passion** ([Cyberpsychology](https://cyberpsychology.eu/article/view/13600); [Luxford et al. 2022](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1089/cyber.2021.0321)).
- **Flow / Koster / Lazzaro / Schell** [lit].

## Implications for Manzil

1. Unlimited matches per night is the single biggest risk; one "true" match counts, the rest is practice.
2. The shared nightly hand is your Wordle — lean in; consider a seeded, fixed sky opening.
3. Shareable artifact: the road, spoiler-safe, plus a rarity line.
4. "Claim tonight's mansion" is a good goal but a bad *only* goal; give each mansion a ladder.
5. Mastery track: a private, never-decreasing number; no leagues.
6. Build the weekly rhythm the moon gives you for free; make difficulty legible in advance.
7. Absence must generate content, not debt; show one resumable thing.
8. The deterministic AI is not enough for years on its own; plan async ghosts.
9. Best-of-five is long for a nightly ritual; fine if one match counts.
10. Make expression visible (the hand as identity object).
11. Month-12 mastery should look like Spelling Bee, not Duolingo; reward "you read the sky".
12. Measure fit ("did this feel worth it tonight?"), not only D30.

**Couldn't verify:** participation shares for StS/Spelunky/Balatro dailies; Tetris Effect's Full Moon; Sky's daily-quest retention; quantified Duolingo league churn.
