the combination

your sun landed on the most famous handful of stars in the sky, and your moon landed on the cord that ties the whole road shut. both of them are about being part of something and not being counted separately.

the third mansion is the pleiades. japan calls them subaru, the gathered ones — that is where the car badge comes from, and the stars in it are these. the arabs made them so central you could say simply al-najm, the star, with no qualifier, and everyone knew which. six or seven separate lights that every eye on earth reads as one shine.

and india names this sky kṛttikā, whose deity is agni, fire, and whose emblem is a blade. that is the hunger underneath all the prettiness: you want to cut a thing down to the part that is actually true. you are not the soft light people expect from the loveliest station on the road.

a cluster is loved as a cluster. you have spent real time unsure whether you were being seen, or whether the group you were part of was — and the blade in you knows the difference even when you are too polite to say so.

then the moon, in the last mansion: al-rishāʾ, the cord. it is jupiter-kept, on the wolf — the most generous planet set on the animal that only works in company. the room reads you as connected, the person who knows everyone, whose name comes up in unrelated conversations.

and the twenty-eighth mansion names your cost in the same breath: the connector is the one role a group forgets to invite. you will keep introducing everyone to everyone, and you have to actually say i want to be in this out loud, because nobody will think to say it for you.

so both halves of your chart describe the same wound from opposite ends. the sun is the brightest thing in a group that people can only see as a group. the moon is the knot that holds everyone together and gets left off the list. you are not lonely in the ordinary way. you are extremely well attended and structurally uncounted, and those are not the same problem.

what it makes you very good at is the blade — the moment where you say the plain true thing the whole warm room was talking around. people do not expect it from you, because they have you filed under lovely. the correction lands harder for arriving from the pleiades.
