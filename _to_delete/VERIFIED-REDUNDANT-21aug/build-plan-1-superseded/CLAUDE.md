# Star Shard — product context (distilled 18 Aug 2026 from meowcastle/starshard docs)

## What it is
A JavaScript game anchored in real astrology — not an astrology app with game elements. Two layers:
1. **Base layer: a solid, checkable astrology app.** Real ephemeris (astronomy-engine, full API), Placidus houses (Porphyry fallback >66° lat, disclosed in plain words), the five classical aspects with orbs said out loud, all ten planets on the chart, tropical zodiac. Free: arrival + the Deep Chart (five tabs over one ring: SHARD / HOUSES / PATTERN / DEPTH / BECOMING). The chart is the marketing.
2. **The value prop: the 28 lunar mansions + the Star Shard.** The lunar zodiac Western astrology dropped — 28 mansions of 12°51′ (4 steps each = 112), the moon crossing one per night. The shard = your natal chart as an object/archetype; fragments = the 28, gathered one a night; "lunar mansion" is astrology's own word.

## The innovation (INNOVATION.md verdict — be precise)
Frame everything as **recovery, never invention** (novelty is a liability in this field). Not ours: the cross-tradition comparison (Robson 1923, Volguine 1936, Hamade 2024), the 28 birth-types (Busteed & Wergin 1974). Genuinely unoccupied, in order of strength:
1. **Computed lifetime rarity** — "Uranus sextile your MC: never in your life; 3 more windows by 2028, then not until 2052." No software anywhere does this per-user. The headline.
2. **Divergence rendered, not harmonised** — where the four traditions (al-manāzil, nakshatra, xiu, European) disagree at a degree, show it as fact. A century of "one lost system" smoothing, unopposed.
3. **The instrument** — degree-indexed, four traditions side by side, frame-explicit (tropical/sidereal shown and switchable; xiu are unequal + RA-based — assert nothing).
The Becoming = our synthesis (cite gandanta + Vimshottari lineage). Traveler types must name the mansion pair, not the gap. Seedborn = 3.5%.

## The one measure (SHARD-MODEL.md)
Everything is ranked by rarity in bits: −log₂(P). Chart highlights scored `rarity × prominence × tension`; ship top 5–7, print the bits. The shard **refines** the chart (36 overlay cells, not 336): sign 1-in-12 → mansion 1-in-28 → step 1-in-112. Identity tier = **784 archetypes (sun-mansion × moon-mansion)** — the deepest honest-rarity tier (~10M people each); with rising = 21,952 shards. Best demo: **co-location** — mean 3.5 sign/mansion disagreements per chart; 21.7% of charts have two points the chart separates that the shard joins (e.g. Moon 26° Cnc + MC 5° Leo, both mansion 10). Every fragment carries its count ("the 41st time the moon has crossed here since you were born"). Never fake rarity; common is said as common.

## Money (DECIDED)
Free arrival + Deep Chart, no account needed to see your shard. **One purchase $19–24** unlocks the game: nightly loop, daily/weekly, codex. Owned forever, no subscription, hard paywall, no trial (trials reduce LTV in this category). Cosmetics = only recurring surface, in the codex, never in the reading flow. Nothing affecting collection purchasable; never sell time. Audience ~25% aged 13–17 — the compliance floor. Sales page leads: "it explains itself" + "buy it once, it never changes." Email = primary reminder channel (iOS PWA push is weak).

## Daily / weekly
The daily reads **your chart, not your sign**: tonight's mansion + one live transit (priority: orb exactness → Saturn>Jupiter>Mars>Venus>Mercury → touches sun/moon/rising). 60–90 words. Special nights = moon crossing your sun's / moon's / rising's / Becoming's mansion (~monthly each). Weekly = 7 beats (standout, the exact orb printed, two-way, backdrop from outers via standingWeather, rhythm, best days, the honest note). Laws: computed lifetime rarity in every slow transit; recall ("last time Mars stood here…"); name one cost every week; 250–300 words. Model gets derived facts only, never birth data; never invents astronomy; deterministic per window; ~80 tokens; hand-written fallback always.

## Voice (WRITING.md)
Lowercase. Show, don't frame. Reader is the subject; no "we"; never break the fourth wall; no prophecy ("you will" banned) — counsel, tendency, conduct. Use the astrology term, define once in four words, then use forever. One coined noun per surface max: shard (arrival), mansion/station (night loop), the Becoming (deep chart). Recognition over flattery: accuracy that includes the cost; computed rarity; "most people do." Daily 60–90w, arrival ~450, deep chart ~1000.

## UI laws (UI-PRINCIPLES.md + calm pass)
Reading is the page, first thing, no card. Three tiers: answer → tonight's sky → the numbers (opt-in, one level). One specific link down ("your moon in the 8th →", a push not a tab-switch). Ceremony scales inversely with frequency (arrival paced once; nightly = one screen; threshold nights earn ceremony back). Ritual surface accrues value from sameness — don't churn it. Tab bar = shard / tonight / chart. Collection not streak; no guilt. Deliberate imprecision at tier 1; decimals live in tier 3 (except the weekly's exact orb).
