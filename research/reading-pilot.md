## The Full Reading, as it would render

# mira — farbank of the bearer

*aries sun · scorpio moon · scorpio rising · full moon · sunday-born*
*read from the sky over chicago · april 12, 1998 · 9:14 pm*

the sky kept a record of the minute you arrived, and this is what it says.
read it slowly. none of it is filler and none of it is anyone else's — it
was assembled from the exact way the sun, the moon, and the eastern
horizon stood over chicago in that one minute.

## i · your sun — late aries, the bearer's sky

you're an **aries sun** — you knew that. here is the part almost nobody
knows: the sun crosses more than two skies while it's in aries, and yours
was the second one — late aries, the stretch the old desert astronomers
called *the little belly*: the soft place under the ram's chin where
treasure is carried close and kept warm. on our road that sky is called
**the bearer**, and it bends everything people think they know about
aries. yes, you start things; yes, you charge. but this aries *carries* —
bags, secrets, other people's unfinished business, plans that aren't ready
to be said out loud. things get handed to you, and they always have been,
because some part of people can tell you will not drop them.

and the sun was in the *last quarter* of that sky — nearly through, the
road already tilting toward taurus. carried things were never meant to be
kept forever; they are meant to be carried **somewhere**. so the question
your sun asks is never whether you can carry it. it is: *where is this
going — and when do you get to set it down?*

## ii · your moon — early scorpio, the crown's sky

on the other side of the wheel, your **moon is in scorpio** — and scorpio
moons have a reputation, most of it half right. yours sits in scorpio's
first sky, under the three bright stars the old calendars drew as a crown
on the scorpion's brow — the sky of vows, bindings, and promises meant to
outlast the people who make them. on the road it's called **the crown**,
and it is the part of you that doesn't show at the door. whatever your
aries sun is carrying, your scorpio moon already knows what it is *for*.
the crown doesn't hold things; it **keeps** them. there is a difference,
and you live inside it: held things can be set down. kept things are
sworn.

and your moon stands in the last quarter of its sky too — the same
position as your sun, mirrored across the wheel. both of your lights at
doors. thresholds are home ground for you: your deepest keeping and your
bravest carrying both happen at the ends of things — last weeks, last
conversations, the packing-up hour after everyone else has gone.

## iii · the full moon you were born under

you were **born under a full moon** — and here this page can show its
work, because that is not a separate fact from the two above. a full moon
only happens when the moon faces the sun across the whole width of the
sky. your aries sun and your scorpio moon — the distance between them, the
exact thing that can make you feel split in two — is the same geometry
that lit the sky the night you arrived. full-moon babies do not arrive
subtle. people read light off you even when you're saying nothing, which
is worth knowing, because it means you were never hiding as well as you
thought.

## iv · sunday's child

you arrived on a **sunday — the sun's own day; it's right there in the
word.** of the seven day-keepers the sun is the plainest and the most
generous: it doesn't scheme, it doesn't hedge, it just rises. sunday's
children get trusted fast and forgiven fast — and reliably underestimate
how much a room rearranges itself around their weather. yours does.

## v · scorpio rising — and its red heart

at 9:14 pm in chicago, **scorpio was climbing the eastern horizon** — so
scorpio is your rising sign, the direction you take the road in. and not
just any stretch of it: the sky standing up in the east was the scorpion's
center, where **antares** burns — the red star every sky-culture on earth
named the scorpion's heart. a rising like that cannot carry at arm's
length. everything you pick up ends up held *close*, whether you meant it
or not — which is why you should choose what you pick up more carefully
than most people ever have to.

## vi · your answering sky

directly across the wheel from your sun sits the far end of **libra** —
the scales, the sky of balance and grip the old astronomers drew as the
scorpion's reaching claws. on the road it's called **the claws**, and it
answers you in the most literal way the sky allows: **the full moon
nearest your birthday, every year of your life, rises there.** once a
year, on your own day, the sky lights the station opposite yours and asks
the bearer's question turned around: *what are you still gripping that you
were only ever supposed to carry?*

## vii · how you walk

now the page can say what it has been building toward. an aries sun on one
bank; a scorpio moon on the far bank. a carrier of treasures rooted in a
keeper of vows. both lights standing in doorways — and the whole thing lit
full by its own distance. on the road, that walk has a name: **farbank**.

farbank travelers are born whole and *wide*. the two halves of you are not
missing each other — they are holding opposite ends of the same river, and
everything you love gets strung across it like a bridge. the tension is
real, so it deserves its real name: the aries in you wants to deliver and
be done; the scorpio in you wants to keep and be bound. on bad weeks that
feels like being accused of two opposite failures at once — too quick to
move on, too slow to let go. both accusations are wrong, and for the same
reason: each one is only reading one bank. what's actually true is
simpler. everything worth keeping in your life gets built *on the
crossing* — decided in the open, at a threshold, in full light — because
that is not a compromise between your banks. it is the only place they
were ever going to meet.

---

**farbank of the bearer** — that is the mark the sky wrote down before
anyone asked your name. the ring above holds all of it: the aries sun, the
scorpio moon, the two doors, the star that answers. it is mostly dark.
that is not a lack; it is room. the road fills it one crossing at a time,
and the first crossing is tonight's.

## Production notes

**~1,100 words, and every section now opens on equity the reader walked in
with** — their sign, their moon sign, "born under a full moon," "sunday's
child," "scorpio rising," Antares. The coined layer (the bearer, the
crown, the claws, farbank) rides second position as nicknames and card
names, exactly per ANCHORS.md. The esoterica that used to lead (station
numbers, Steps as proper nouns, "Keeper," the Current) is gone from the
surface entirely.

**The structural sell that emerged:** each sign contains 2–3 stations, so
the station system reads as *"which aries you are"* — refinement of a
trusted system, not replacement. This framing should lead marketing copy
too.

**Fact discipline:** all anchors verified against the engine's own output
(Sun 23.0° Aries · Moon 5.6° Scorpio · Asc 14.4° Scorpio · weekday
Sunday); star lore per the verified mansion corpus (al-Buṭayn, al-Iklīl,
al-Qalb/Antares, al-Zubānā). The corpus batches inherit this rule: no
anchor claim that the engine or the verified corpus can't back.

**Corpus v2 impact (SIGIL-READING §7):** unchanged quantities, one new
requirement — every strike/root body opens with its sign-anchor clause
("late aries," "early scorpio"), which the station data already determines.
Station → sign(s) mapping is pure arithmetic; Claude Code should add
`signSpan` to stations.js so the composer never hand-maintains it.
