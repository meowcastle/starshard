# Research: Star Myths — Origins, Roads, and the Return

*Star Shard research corpus · August 12, 2026 · full agent report — Dogon, Maya, Tanabata, Plato, wayfinding, quick sweep, coined-word candidates*

## 1. Dogon star lore + the Sirius controversy

**What Griaule/Dieterlen actually reported.** In "Un système soudanais de Sirius" (Journal de la Société des Africanistes, 1950) and later *Le Renard Pâle* (1965), Marcel Griaule and Germaine Dieterlen described a Dogon cosmology centered on **Amma** (creator), the **egg of the world**, and the **Nommo** (primordial water-spirits/twins, one sacrificed and resurrected). They reported that Sirius (*sigi tolo*) had an invisible companion, ***po tolo*** — "star of the *po*," the fonio grain (*Digitaria exilis*), "the smallest seed cultivated in the region" — described as small, extremely dense (made of a metal heavier than iron), circling Sirius in ~50 years. This matches Sirius B, a white dwarf invisible to the naked eye. Griaule and Dieterlen themselves "stated plainly that they could not explain how the Dogon had acquired this knowledge, and they did not claim to have done so" ([Spoken Past source review](https://spokenpast.com/articles/dogon-sirius-mystery/)).

**The contamination/critique literature.** Robert Temple's *The Sirius Mystery* (1976) bolted on amphibious aliens from Sirius ("the Nommo") — pure pseudo-archaeology. Critiques: Ian Ridpath (1978) found internal contradictions (some accounts describe Venus, not Sirius); James Oberg (1982) noted Sirius B was in European popular-science print from the 1920s and the Dogon were never isolated; Noah Brosch (2008) suggested an 1893 French eclipse expedition as a vector (unproven). The decisive blow: **Walter van Beek's restudy** ("Dogon Restudied," *Current Anthropology* 32:2, 1991 — [full PDF](https://home.iscte-iul.pt/~fgvs/VanBeek,Dogon.pdf), [Leiden repository](https://scholarlypublications.universiteitleiden.nl/handle/1887/9039)). Van Beek, after ~11 years of fieldwork, reported: "No Dogon outside the circle of Griaule's informants had ever heard of *sigu tolo* or *pó tolo*... no one, even within the circle of Griaule informants, had ever heard or understood that Sirius was a double star." Informants presented with the Griaulian creation myth (egg of the world, Amma's roles, Nommo as creator/redeemer) "emphatically state that they have never heard of them." Van Beek attributes the system to "bricolage" co-created in an unusual fieldwork dynamic — leading questions, a few privileged informants (Ogotemmêli, Ambara), colonial power dynamics, and even Biblical imports (the Nommo sacrificed "standing upright, arms outstretched, tied to a tree... with iron"). Griaule's daughter Geneviève Calame-Griaule countered with initiatory secrecy; van Beek conceded the epistemic point but held that the divergence across dozens of informants "went well beyond what a simple secrecy model could plausibly explain." Consensus: Sirius B "is not visible to the naked eye under any conditions"; the Sirius-B material is an artifact of the encounter, not ancient Dogon astronomy ([Spoken Past](https://spokenpast.com/articles/dogon-sirius-mystery/); see also [Bad Archaeology](http://www.badarchaeology.com/extraterrestrials/the-sirius-mystery/)).

**Verdicts.** Dogon-knew-Sirius-B claims: **AVOID** (debunked). Temple/ancient-aliens framing: **AVOID** hard (it's also racially condescending — it assumes Africans couldn't have produced sophisticated cosmology). The Griaulian cosmology as "authentic ancient Dogon religion": **CONTESTED** — treat as a Griaule–Dogon co-creation, not ethnographic fact. The *concept* you care about — **the smallest seed that is heaviest; the dim deep companion of the brightest star; creation spiraling out of a vibrating seed-grain**: **USABLE AS INSPIRATION** with caveats. It's a genuinely beautiful idea that exists in the published record; the honest credit line is "inspired by the cosmology recorded by Griaule & Dieterlen among the Dogon of Mali — a record now understood as a collaboration between ethnographer and informants" — glossary-cite the controversy, never claim "the Dogon believe X," never use Dogon lexemes (*po*, *tolo*, *nommo*, *amma*) as product labels, and never invoke Sirius B as secret knowledge. The Dogon are ~800,000 living people with a living religion.

## 2. Maya sky-roads and star origins

**Milky Way as road — how solid?** Three separable claims:

- **The dark rift as the Black Road to Xibalba: SOLID (textual + ethnographic).** The Popol Vuh crossroads scene is explicit: "At length they arrived at a crossroads... One was Red Road and another was Black Road; White Road was one while another was Yellow Road," and only one speaks: "'Me! Take me, for I am the lord's road.' Thus spoke the Black Road" — the road to Xibalba ([Christenson's Popol Vuh translation, FAMSI](https://www.famsi.org/research/vanstone/2012/PopolVuhBrief.pdf)). The identification of this Black Road with the Milky Way's dark rift comes from Dennis Tedlock's Popol Vuh commentary and Barbara Tedlock's K'iche' ethnography; contemporary Maya speakers do call the Milky Way's dark cleft the road of Xibalba. It is an interpretive linkage, but a well-anchored one.
- **The White Road: SOLID.** *Sakbe/sacbe* (sak "white" + be(h) "road") names the raised limestone causeways (e.g., the ~100 km Cobá–Yaxuná sacbe), AND Yucatec ethnography records the Milky Way itself called *Sak Beh*, "the white road in the sky," the path souls travel — terrestrial roads as enacted cosmology ([Mayan.org on sacbeob](https://mayan.org/civilization/sacbe/)).
- **Schele's full system (Milky Way = Wakah-Chan World Tree, rotating through the night as tree/canoe/road): CONTESTED.** *Maya Cosmos* (Freidel, Schele & Parker 1993) built this from the Palenque Tablet of the Cross and iconography ([Schele's "Creation, Cosmos" paper, Mesoweb](https://www.mesoweb.com/pari/publications/RT10/Creation.pdf)), but later scholarship criticized its shamanic-interpretive framework as "largely superseded," and epigraphers (David Stuart and others) have revised the "Wakah-Chan" reading itself ([Maya Cosmos reception summary](https://grokipedia.com/page/maya_cosmos_(book))). Use the road/rift material, hold the World-Tree-=-Milky-Way equation loosely.

**Three hearthstones: SOLID core, reconstructed astronomy.** Inscriptional bedrock: Quirigua Stela C says that on 4 Ajaw 8 Kumk'u (the creation date, August 13, 3114 BCE in the standard correlation) the gods "planted" three throne-stones at the "Lying-down-Sky, First-Three-Stone-Place." The identification with **Alnitak, Saiph, and Rigel** forming a triangle around **M42 (the Orion Nebula) as the smoke/fire of the hearth** rests on modern K'iche' ethnography (Dennis Tedlock: K'iche' speakers call these stars the three hearthstones, *Oxib' Xk'ub'*, and M42 the fire, *Q'aq'*) projected back onto the Classic texts ([Mesoweb](https://www.mesoweb.com/pari/publications/RT10/Creation.pdf); [Mexicolore on the turtle and hearthstones](https://www.mexicolore.co.uk/maya/home/turtle-and-hearthstones)). The maize god reborn from a cracked turtle (Orion's belt on the turtle's back at Bonampak) is part of the same complex. This mirrors the actual Maya household: every kitchen has three hearthstones — sky and home mirror each other. Wonderful, usable image.

**Pleiades as Tz'ab-ek: SOLID** — the rattlesnake's rattle, well attested in Yucatec sources ([overview](https://ancientmayalife.blogspot.com/2018/02/constellations-and-ancient-maya.html)).

**2012 contamination: AVOID.** John Major Jenkins's "galactic alignment" of the solstice sun with the dark rift, and all 13-baktun apocalypse material, is rejected by Mayanists ([Archaeology Magazine's debunk](https://archive.archaeology.org/0911/2012/)). Any use of the dark rift must steer wide of "alignment/end-date" language — that's the contaminated register.

**Usable concepts:** the sky as a road system with a crossing where a road *speaks*; the black road vs. the white road; three hearthstones + first fire as the act of creation; the road walked by souls that is also a road you can physically walk. Credit as scholarship (Popol Vuh, Tedlock, Schele/Freidel with caveat); the Maya are millions of living people — don't label products *Sakbe*, *Xibalba*, *Wakah-Chan*.

## 3. The celestial river + Tanabata

**Confirmed.** The Milky Way as the Heavenly/Silver River (Chinese 天河 *Tianhe* / 银河 *Yinhe*; Japanese 天の川 *amanogawa*). The Weaver Girl **Zhinü = Vega** and the Cowherd **Niulang = Altair** (Japanese Orihime/Hikoboshi; Korean Jingnyeo/Gyeonu), lovers separated on opposite banks of the river, permitted to meet once a year on the 7th night of the 7th month when **magpies form a bridge** across the river; rain that night is "the tears of Orihime and Hikoboshi." The myth is ~2,600 years old in China (roots reaching the *Classic of Poetry*), formalized as Qixi by the Han dynasty; introduced to Japan as Kikkōden by **Empress Kōken in 755**, popularized in the Edo period when it merged with Obon customs. Living practice is enormous and fully public: tanzaku wishes on bamboo, the Sendai festival (Aug 6–8, one of Tōhoku's three great festivals), Hiratsuka, Asagaya, plus diaspora festivals in São Paulo and Los Angeles; Tokyo Disney literally casts Mickey as Hikoboshi and Minnie as Orihime ([Wikipedia: Tanabata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanabata); [Cosmundus: Qixi/Tanabata/Chilseok](https://www.cosmundus.com/en/qixi-tanabata-chilseok/)).

**Verdict: SOLID, and respectful use is very achievable.** This is a pan-East-Asian festival myth (China/Japan/Korea/Vietnam), publicly celebrated, commercially retold constantly (anime references it every July), and already licensed into global entertainment by East Asian companies themselves. "Born with sun and moon on opposite banks" as a product-type *description* is squarely in the tradition's own register of retelling. Guardrails: (1) credit the Chinese origin, not just Tanabata — this is Qixi's myth first; (2) coin your own vocabulary rather than borrowing *Orihime/Zhinü* as type names; (3) don't flatten it to "Japanese Valentine's Day"; keep the specific furniture (river, banks, magpie bridge, once-a-year crossing, rain-as-tears) which is what you actually want.

## 4. Plato's Timaeus and the native star

**Verified, public domain (Jowett translation, Project Gutenberg [#1572](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1572)).** The passage is Timaeus **41d–42b**. Verbatim from the Gutenberg text as fetched: the Demiurge "divided the whole mixture into souls **equal in number to the stars, and assigned each soul to a star**; and having there placed them **as in a chariot, he showed them the nature of the universe**, and declared to them the laws of destiny"; souls are then sown "in the earth... the moon... and the other instruments of time"; and — the anchor quote — "**He who lived well during his appointed time was to return and dwell in his native star, and there he would have a blessed and congenial existence.**" (Lamb's 1925 Loeb renders it "the habitation of his consort star" — [Perseus, Tim. 41d](http://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0180%3Atext%3DTim.%3Asection%3D41d).) Caveat for family-friendly use: the continuation of 42b–c is a reincarnation-as-punishment ladder ("he would pass into the nature of a woman," then beasts) — quote 41d–42b and stop; do not paraphrase the demotion ladder.

**Anamnesis, verified verbatim:** Meno 81 (Jowett, [Gutenberg #1643](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1643)): "The soul of man is immortal... and having been born again many times, and having seen all things that exist... has knowledge of them all... **for all enquiry and all learning is but recollection.**" Phaedrus 249c (Jowett, [Gutenberg #1636](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1636)): "this is **the recollection of the knowledge which she attained when in the company of the Gods.**"

**Verdict: SOLID.** 2,400 years old, canonical, public domain, no living community holds it. This is your load-bearing spine: *you were assigned to a star; you are sown into time; living well is the road home; knowing is remembering.* Cite it openly and by name — citing Plato reads as literate, not appropriative.

## 5. Polynesian wayfinding

**What it is.** Non-instrument open-ocean navigation, nearly extinguished in Hawai'i, revived when the Polynesian Voyaging Society built **Hōkūle'a** (launched 1975; Tahiti voyage 1976) navigated by Micronesian master **Mau Piailug**, who broke tradition to teach **Nainoa Thompson**, who synthesized the modern **star compass**: the horizon divided into 32 houses; ~220 memorized stars identified by rising/setting points; direction held by stars, sun, swells, wind, and birds; latitude read from meridian crossings and **zenith stars** ([Nainoa Thompson on the star compass](https://hokulea.com/the-star-compass-by-nainoa-thompson/); [PVS wayfinding overview](https://hokulea.com/polynesian-wayfinding/); [PBS Wayfinders](https://www.pbs.org/wayfinders/wayfinding.html)). Confirmed: **Hōkūle'a = Arcturus, the "Star of Gladness," Hawai'i's zenith star** — its declination (~+19°) matches Hawai'i's latitude, so it passes directly overhead there; it marked "the northern destination in long voyages from the Marquesas and Tahiti to Hawai'i." The sky is organized into four star lines: Ke Kā o Makali'i (the Canoe-Bailer), Iwikuamo'o (the Backbone, Polaris to the Southern Cross), Manaiakalani (the Chief's Fishline), Ka Lupe o Kawelo (the Kite of Kawelo) ([Hawaiian star lines](https://archive.hokulea.com/ike/hookele/hawaiian_star_lines.html)).

**Verdict: SOLID scholarship; HIGH sensitivity.** This is a living, hard-won cultural reclamation with named living lineage-holders; the canoe is regarded as a family member. Respect protocol: the *concepts* — wayfinder, zenith star, star houses, holding a course by memory of risings — are describable in English and safely inspiring; the *Hawaiian vocabulary* (Hōkūle'a, the star-line names, compass-house names) must not become product labels, and the product must never imply it transmits actual wayfinding knowledge or PVS endorsement. Glossary credit: "inspired by the Polynesian wayfinding revival led by the Polynesian Voyaging Society, Mau Piailug, and Nainoa Thompson."

## 6. Quick sweep

- **Emu in the Sky (SOLID; living knowledge, glossary-only).** A *dark* constellation — the Coalsack (by the Southern Cross) as head, body along the dust lanes through Scorpius/Sagittarius: **Gugurmin** (Wiradjuri), **Gawarrgay** (Kamilaroi/Euahlayi). Its seasonal orientation is a calendar: rising at dusk Apr–May = mating; horizontal Jun–Jul = eggs; vertical Aug–Sep = chicks; "sitting in the billabong" Oct–Nov ([Ngarrngga case study](https://www.ngarrngga.org/case-study/case-study-1-the-celestial-emu); [Fuller, Hamacher & Norris, "The Emu Sky Knowledge of the Kamilaroi and Euahlayi Peoples"](https://www.academia.edu/11575118/The_Emu_Sky_Knowledge_of_the_Kamilaroi_and_Euahlayi_Peoples)). Perfect scholarly parallel for the Maya dark rift — *the shape made of darkness is the figure* — and validates a "dark-lane" motif as a cross-cultural pattern. But this is living, community-held knowledge recorded with elders' permission: cite in glossary as scholarship; coin nothing from its words; ICIP (Indigenous Cultural and Intellectual Property) norms apply.
- **Egyptian imperishable stars (SOLID; ancient, low-risk).** The circumpolar stars that never rise or set — *ikhemu-sek*, "the ones not knowing destruction" — are the destination of the king's soul in the **Pyramid Texts** (5th–6th Dynasty, c. 2400–2300 BCE, the oldest religious texts on Earth): "He is no longer in the earth. He is in the sky... he lives with the gods" among the imperishable stars ([World History Encyclopedia](https://www.worldhistory.org/article/148/the-pyramid-texts-guide-to-the-afterlife/); [Ikhemu-sek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikhemu-sek)). The semantic gift: *the stars that never set = what in you cannot be destroyed.* No living practitioner community; translation-derived epithets ("the Imperishable") are fair game.
- **Stellar nucleosynthesis (SOLID — the one literally true star-seed story).** Burbidge, Burbidge, Fowler & Hoyle, "Synthesis of the Elements in Stars" (*Reviews of Modern Physics*, 1957 — "B²FH") established that essentially every atom heavier than helium was forged in stellar cores and supernovae, then scattered to seed later worlds. Sagan, *Cosmos* (1980): "**The nitrogen in our DNA, the calcium in our teeth, the iron in our blood, the carbon in our apple pies were made in the interiors of collapsing stars. We are made of starstuff.**" ([quote compilations](https://www.goalcast.com/carl-sagan-quotes/)). Use this as the product's truth-floor: the mythic register can always land on "and this part is literally true."
- **The "starseed" new-age movement (AVOID its vocabulary).** Origin: Brad Steiger's *Gods of Aquarius* (1976) and *The Star People*; modern form claims souls incarnated from **Pleiades, Sirius, Arcturus** ("Pleiadians, Sirians, Arcturians"), "lightworkers," awakening symptoms, light language; >1 billion TikTok views on #starseed; psychologists explain adoption via the Forer effect, source-monitoring errors, and ontological confusion, and note links to science-distrust and conspiracy thinking ("conspirituality") ([The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/starseeds-psychologists-on-why-some-people-think-theyre-aliens-living-on-earth-197291); [Wikipedia: Star people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_people_(New_Age)); [Jules Evans on the movement's darker history](https://julesevans.medium.com/the-weird-history-of-starseeds-7df5127be9c3)). Keep total lexical distance: never *starseed*, *lightworker*, *Pleiadian*, *Sirian*, *Arcturian*, *ascension*, *5D*, *awakening codes*. Note the collision risk: the movement has already colonized "Sirius" and "Pleiades" as soul-origin words — one more reason your Dogon and Pleiades material must stay in the scholarly glossary, not the product surface. "Star shard" is clean: no new-age lineage, and it points at the nucleosynthesis truth (a shard of a star is chemically what you are).

# COINED-WORD CANDIDATES

Pattern: coined English/classical blends on the surface; real traditions credited in a scholarly glossary; no living-culture lexemes as labels.

**Type (a) — moon trailing one sky behind the sun (carries what came before; memory/ember/depth):**

1. **Emberwake** — bows to the Maya three-hearthstone creation (the first fire whose smoke is M42) and the wayfinding canoe's wake. Semantic: the moon rides in the sun's wake carrying a coal from the first hearth — what follows behind keeps the fire. Respect: hearth imagery is universal; glossary credits Quirigua Stela C + Tedlock without using Maya words.
2. **Deepseed** — bows to the Dogon *po tolo* concept: the smallest seed is the deepest beginning and the heaviest thing in the sky; the dim companion that trails the brightest star. Respect: concept-calque only, zero Dogon lexemes; glossary credits "the cosmology recorded by Griaule & Dieterlen" with the van Beek caveat and explicit rejection of ancient-alien readings; never mention Sirius B as "mystery."
3. **Sternlight** — bows to Polynesian wayfinding (the navigator holds the course partly by what stands behind the canoe). Semantic: the light carried astern; you steer forward by what's behind you. Respect: nautical English only.

**Type (b) — sun and moon in opposite skies (the far crossing; river-banks):**

4. **Farbank** — bows to Qixi/Tanabata: Vega and Zhinü's loom on one bank of the Silver River, Altair on the other. Semantic: born with sun and moon on opposite banks; whole only across a crossing that comes rarely and is worth the year. Respect: credit China–Japan–Korea jointly; keep rain-as-tears and once-a-year cadence as flavor text; don't name anything Orihime.
5. **Bridgewing** — bows to the magpie bridge (a bridge made of many small living wings, built fresh each meeting). Semantic: the crossing exists only because many small helpers hold together. Respect: same as above; magpies are the credited image.

**Primordial burst event:**

6. **The First Kindling** — bows to the Maya hearth-lighting at 4 Ajaw 8 Kumk'u *and* to stellar furnaces. Semantic: creation as lighting a fire between set stones — the moment the sky's kitchen was lit.
7. **The Great Sowing** — bows to Timaeus 41e (souls "sown" into the instruments of time) and to supernova nucleosynthesis (stars literally sowing heavy elements). Semantic: the burst is not a destruction but a planting; every shard is seed-grain. Public domain + literally true; strongest candidate.

**Collection-as-remembering mechanic:**

8. **Recollection** (mechanic name), styled **"to re-collect."** — bows to Plato's anamnesis via Jowett's own word: "all enquiry and all learning is but recollection." Semantic: gathering the shards *is* remembering who you are; the pun (collect again = remember) does the design work for free.
9. **Anamnesa** (or plain **Anamnesis** for the codex/lore name of the mechanic) — the Greek term, softened. Semantic: the scholarly true-name of Recollection, for lore-depth players. Zero cultural risk.

**The greater road (of which Earth's moonroad is one leg):**

10. **The Silverway** — bows to the Chinese Silver River (银河, the standard Chinese name of the Milky Way) and the Maya *Sak Beh* white road of souls. Semantic: the one great river-road through the sky; every world's moonroad is a causeway joining it. Respect: coined English blend; glossary credits both traditions.
11. **The Wayline** — bows to Nainoa Thompson's star lines (the four great lines that organize the whole sky) and the wayfinding course held in memory. Semantic: the greater road is not paved but *held* — a line that exists because travelers keep it. Respect: English only; glossary credits the Polynesian Voyaging Society revival by name; no Hawaiian star names on product surfaces.
12. **The Imperishables** — bows to the Egyptian *ikhemu-sek*, the circumpolar stars that never set, destination of the soul in the Pyramid Texts. Semantic: the fixed hub of the Silverway — the stars that never leave the sky, where nothing that reaches them is ever lost (use for the road's terminus, or as an epithet/tier for eternal shards). Respect: ancient tradition, translation-derived epithet; low risk.

**Cross-cutting respect architecture (validated by the research):** the two-layer pattern — coined words on product surfaces, a scholarly glossary crediting Popol Vuh/Tedlock, Griaule–Dieterlen-with-caveat, Qixi/Tanabata, Plato, PVS/Piailug/Thompson, Kamilaroi–Euahlayi–Wiradjuri emu scholarship, Pyramid Texts, and B²FH/Sagan — is exactly what the critical literature supports. The three hard exclusion zones: (1) any "ancient people secretly knew modern astronomy" framing (Dogon/Sirius B), (2) any 2012/alignment register around the dark rift, (3) the entire new-age starseed lexicon.
