# The void: the window is the seat cure, the empty circle is the record narrowing, and the eye is now the calendar's seat instrument

**8 September 2026. For Design.** 1a on both dials, 1b, 1c as control, all on both windows, with
the seat column beside spread and the lonely-card rate. 130 vectors in the station-law suite. 896
boards a cell, two seeds, walker boards. Plus one diagnostic row Design did not ask for and will
want.

**Design's scenario happened.** On the slid window 1a narrows and 1b cools and neither does the
other's job. But the sheet has a result underneath that one, and it changes what the pick is
about: **the biggest seat move available on the void's night is not a law. It is the window.**

The flag on the void said *this night wants seat cooled*, and it was written against the shipped
board, where the deep seat is 29.7, the highest plain deep seat in the tested set. **Slide the
window four and the deep seat is 15.4, which is the calendar's median, with no law on the board.**
That is −14.3, nearly three times what the eye has ever done, and it costs one `_boardOff` entry.

Spread does not move with the slide (37.9 → 39.0) and the shape rotates: door-first the floor is
the tortoise at 28.6, slid the floor is the dragon at 28.0 and the tortoise is second at 52.6,
because m21 to m27 are all tortoise ground and the tortoise's grant is a count. So after the
slide the void's night has one problem left, and it is spread, not seat. That reframes the sheet.

## 1a the empty circle: the record narrowing, on one window, on one dial

**Slid, right: spread 39.0 → 14.7.** The largest narrowing measured on any night; the crow's m4
record was 16.7. Tiger −30.2, bird +17.9, dragon +10.5, tortoise −3.6. It is a true NARROWS, top
down and floor up, and it rotates the top to the bird at 51.5 with the tiger now at 36.8. Seat
moves +4.2 fresh and +4.0 deep, which is the wrong direction and inside the bound. Skill barely
moves.

**Door-first, right: fails.** Tortoise +24.3, dragon −12.3, spread to 49.4. Same clause at the
same index with the thread at the middle and the gate on its right, tiger ground both, and the
debit lands on the tiger's most sheltered station; the tortoise, whose grant is a count, collects.
This is the count-law pattern again: the hush 1/2, the shell 1/2, now the circle 1/2. **A count
law is worth what its stations are worth, and the window sets that.**

**The `best` dial answers the question it was built for, and the answer is arithmetic.** Design
put the second notch on the sheet to learn whether the crow's narrowing came from the choosing or
from the numbers. On the slid window `best` narrows 13.9 to `right`'s 24.3 and pushes the deep seat
+10.7, over the bound. **The choosing is what makes the crow's shape a seat law**: a debit that
chases the richer neighbour follows the leader's best card, and the leader is the one with the
richer neighbour more often. Fixing the debit to a station removes the seat exposure and narrows
harder. The card's own instinct, "fewer decisions for the count, the same lever", was right, and
the sheet says it is a better lever.

**That is the portability row, and it passes.** The eye at defender cools the seat by about five
on a tiger road and a tortoise road, door-first and slid, and does nothing else. It taxes the bird
3.5 to 3.9 here, a quarter of what it cost the bird on the throne's crossing. Design's sentence
for what this would mean is exact: **the eye is the calendar's seat instrument the way the price
is its shelter instrument.** It conditions on a comparison (a tie), but a tie is a comparison of
the two cards in front of it, not a reading of the board's shape, which is why it carries where
the crow did not. The rule from Friday holds with that clarification: *a comparison between the
striker and the struck is local; a comparison across stations inherits the night.*

## 1c the price: 8/8, and its biggest row yet

Door-first it is inert (spread −0.4): the thread's crossing is tiger ground where the tiger does
not swing. **Slid it takes 14.8 off the tiger and nothing off anyone else**, spread 39.0 → 23.5.
The void's crossing on the slid window is where the tiger's untakeable cards go to strike, and the
price charges exactly that. Eight nights, eight passes, and this is the largest single effect it
has had. It does not need to ship here; it is the yardstick, and the yardstick says the slid
window has a lot of shelter at the crossing, which is the same thing the empty circle's tiger
−30.2 says from the other side.

## The diagnostic: both laws at one station

Design's card said 1b sits *beside* 1a because they fix different things. I ran them together on
the slid window to see whether they fight. **They do not.** Circle/right + eye/defender: seat
+6.8 → −0.2 fresh, deep 15.4 → 14.5, spread 39.0 → 17.8, fresh skill +6.3. Nearly additive: the
circle's narrowing minus a little, the eye's cooling in full, and the circle's +4 seat cancelled.
It is one row and it is two laws on one station, which the calendar has never done and the
level-explains-itself rule would have to be argued past. I am not recommending it. I am reporting
that the arithmetic allows it, in case the design call goes that way.

## Recommendation

**First, slide the window.** `_boardOff` 24:4, the void at the middle, the same slide as m2, m19,
m21, m23, m25, m28 and now m15. This alone takes the worst deep seat on the calendar to the median
and costs nothing. It should happen whichever law ships, and it should happen even if none does.

**Then the empty circle, `right`, on that window.** `lawAt {24:{station:5, kind:"void"}}` in
Design's numbering, **index 4** in the reference and in Code's map, `voidN` right. The record
narrowing, a true NARROWS, seat inside the bound, the card's own two lines keyed on the station.
The `best` notch is retired: it is a seat law wearing the circle's clothes.

**The eye is the honourable second, and it is now proven.** If Design reads the void's night as a
seat night even after the slide, the eye at defender does −4.6 on this window and has done within
a point of that on every road it has stood on. It becomes the calendar's answer to "this night
wants seat cooled", the way the price is the answer to "how much shelter is at the crossing", and
it should be on the shelf for the loop work's komi question.

**The lonely-card deny is deleted** on the rate above. Everything frozen melts.

**For the loop bookmark.** Two things this sheet adds. The window is a seat instrument worth up
to 14 on its own, larger than any law, and the six slid nights already shipped were slid for
fiction rather than for this; the re-cut queue should read each night's deep seat on both windows
before anything else. And the eye is the first law with a portability row on seat rather than on
spread, which is the column the komi question needs.
