# Research: the astrology app market

*Star Shard research corpus · August 13, 2026 · full agent report.
Synthesis and recommendations live in `MARKET.md`. Figures marked
**estimate** are third-party modelling (Sensor Tower / AppMagic /
Similarweb), not disclosed by the companies.*

## 1. The landscape

**Co–Star.** Free NASA-ephemeris natal chart + daily horoscope,
minimalist, social layer for comparing friends' charts, famously blunt
push notifications. Freemium + à la carte one-time IAP, subscription
added later: Pro-Star $8.99/mo; Your Year Ahead $11.99; Advanced Chart
$8.99; Eros $6.99; question packs $2.99–$6.99; **$2.99 to add a friend
manually**. 20M+ downloads; ~25% of US women 18–25 had downloaded it by
Apr 2021 (disclosed); 4.3M MAU (2026). Funding ≈$21M total including a
**$15M Series A (Spark, Apr 2021)**. **Acquired by Midjourney,
announced 24 July 2026**, terms undisclosed; ~24 staff joined, founder
Banu Guler became Midjourney's Chief Design Officer.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/midjourney-acquired-the-astrology-app-co-star/) ·
[Axios](https://www.axios.com/2021/04/14/astrology-app-co-star-raises-15-million-funding)

**The Pattern.** Psychological-language identity app that deliberately
avoids astrology jargon; core value is "Bonds" relationship analysis.
Go Deeper+ $14.99/mo, $83.99/yr; Connect+ $24.99/qtr; profile slots
$9.99–$19.99. 4.0 stars, 15K ratings, **age-rated 13+** — the only
major one.

**CHANI.** Human-written astrology from Chani Nicholas plus audio
meditations and a personalized rising-sign podcast. $11.99/mo or
$107.99/yr. 2M+ downloads, **4.9 stars on 57K ratings** — best-rated in
the category. **Zero venture capital, bootstrapped**; ~$14M annual
revenue (Inc. **estimate**), ~50% YoY growth; grew 3→35 employees with
an $80K salary floor and a 4-day week.
[Inc.](https://www.inc.com/gabrielle-bienasz/how-this-company-uses-astrology-to-make-business-decisions/91254002)

**Sanctuary.** Marketplace for live 1:1 readings. Sanctuary+ $14.99/mo;
intro readings $4.99; web $3.99–$9.99/min. 4.8 stars, 44K ratings.
$6.5M raised.

**Astro-Seek.** Free ad-supported web calculator, exhaustive technique
coverage, near-zero interpretation. ~4.2M monthly visits; **69.1%
female**; **6.88 pages/visit, 7:06 average session, 30.5% bounce**
(Similarweb **estimate**). Proof that depth alone retains.

**TimePassages.** Mobile one-time unlocks **$0.99 each**, $9.99
unlimited-natal pass. Desktop all one-time: Basic $79, Standard $179,
Advanced $209, **Complete $699**. The only vendor spanning $0.99
consumer to $699 professional on one engine.

**Time Nomad.** iOS-only, on-device, offline, no account — birth data
never leaves the device. Free + individual IAP unlocks. 4.8 stars.

**AstroMatrix.** Maximum feature density at near-zero price. Subs from
$2.49/mo, and a **Lifetime License at $24.99 / $34.95 / $49.99** —
three simultaneous price points, i.e. live price-testing. The closest
consumer-priced one-time precedent.

**Nebula (OBRIO/Genesis, Ukraine).** Horoscope content + live psychic
marketplace. **$50M ARR in 2023, +250% income growth** (disclosed); 30M
users Dec 2023, later "exceeded 65M." Also the category's worst
billing-complaint profile (Trustpilot, BBB).
[Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2023/12/05/obrio/)

**Astrotalk (India).** Pay-per-minute marketplace, ₹5–₹200+/min, ~20%
commission. **FY25 revenue ₹1,176cr (~$140M), PBT ₹285cr**; 1.5M
transacting users; **80% of revenue from repeat buyers**; $1B valuation
via ESOP buyback (2026).
[Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/astrotalk-turns-unicorn-at-1-bn-valuation-via-esop-buyback-12253413)

## 2. Revenue reality

| Company | Figure | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Astrotalk | ₹1,176cr (~$140M) rev, ₹285cr PBT FY25 | disclosed |
| OBRIO / Nebula | $50M ARR 2023 | disclosed |
| CHANI | ~$14M/yr | Inc. estimate |
| Co–Star | never disclosed; ~$3.2M US app revenue 2023 | AppMagic estimate |

US app-store revenue estimates 2023 (AppMagic via Statista): Nebula
$7.0M · CHANI $6.8M · Co–Star $3.2M. The whole US astrology-app category
grossed ~$39.7M in 2019 (Sensor Tower **estimate**).

**Co–Star's arithmetic is the cautionary tale:** ~$21M raised, 20M+
downloads, ~$3–5M/yr revenue — roughly **$0.15–$0.25 of lifetime revenue
per download** — then an undisclosed exit to an AI lab that wanted the
team. **The two VC-funded pure-content plays (Co–Star, Sanctuary) are
the two with no evidence of profitability. The profitable ones are
bootstrapped (CHANI) or marketplace-based (Astrotalk, Nebula).**

**Benchmarks to plan against.**
[RevenueCat, State of Subscription Apps 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)
(115K apps, $16B): download-to-paid at D35 is **10.7% for a hard paywall
vs 2.1% freemium**; median monthly revenue one year post-launch **~$72**;
top decile $2,500+; only **17.3% of new subscription apps reach
$1,000/month within two years, and 4.6% reach $10,000/month**. Web
revenue is 3.2% of global app revenue but **41% of top-tier apps
generate some**.
[Adapty, Lifestyle](https://adapty.io/blog/lifestyle-app-subscription-benchmarks/):
a hard paywall without trial converts at **18–38% install-to-paid**, and
**trials reduce LTV by 21.2% in Lifestyle — the only category where
direct buyers outperform trial cohorts.**

**Treat stated willingness-to-pay with suspicion.** A Harris poll (Jan
2024, n=2,069) reports 29% of Americans pay monthly for astrology at a
$20–49 median — while the entire US category grossed ~$40M in a
comparable year. Pew (May 2025) is the counterweight: 30% consult
astrology yearly, **but only 1% rely on it "a lot"** and 20% do it "just
for fun."
[Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/05/21/3-in-10-americans-consult-astrology-tarot-cards-or-fortune-tellers/)

## 3. Does anyone do one-time purchase?

**Yes, but it's a barbell — heavy at the professional end ($79–$699),
heavy at the human-report end ($7–$25), thin in the middle.** Star Shard
is aimed at the thin middle.

- **Professional desktop, pure one-time:** Solar Fire $360 · Sirius $550
  · Kepler 7 $300 · TimePassages Complete $699 · Janus $135.
- **Consumer lifetime options** (23.2% of subscription apps offer one):
  AstroMatrix $24.99–$49.99; **Astro Gold $69.99 lifetime — and Astro
  Gold is the warning.** It ran one-time for over a decade, then moved to
  subscription at v9.0, stating it needed recurrence "to continue
  maintaining, improving, and supporting the app."
  [Astro Gold FAQ](https://www.astrogold.io/ufaq/astro-gold-ios-subscription-model/)
- **Co–Star is the largest consumer-scale à la carte precedent** — every
  IAP above is one-time, and it shipped consumables *before* the
  subscription that is now its most-cited complaint.
- **The real one-time volume market is human-written PDF reports.** Etsy
  natal readings cluster $7–$25; the top listing is **$23.69 with 20,100
  reviews.** That's the consumer price-ceiling signal.
- **16Personalities: $29 one-time** for the Premium Career Suite after a
  free test — the closest structural twin to free-chart-then-paid.

## 4. What users complain about

**Co–Star** (4.8 stars, but aggregated review sentiment 64.8% negative):
vague or nonsensical copy; anxiety-inducing tone (the blunt voice is
both its best acquisition asset and its top in-app complaint);
**retroactive paywalling** after a 2025 update; nickel-and-diming — the
$2.99 add-a-friend charge is singled out, with reviewers comparing it
unfavourably to *"a one-time $6 fee"*; and **identical phrasing
appearing in different people's readings**. Astrologers note it
interprets only Sun/Moon/Rising, ignoring seven planets, twelve houses,
and all aspects.

**The Pattern** (4.0 stars, 88.3% negative sentiment): paywall regret is
the #1 theme — *"everything that used to be free before is now a paid
feature"*; mass-identical insights; and the most actionable complaint in
the entire corpus — ***"The Pattern doesn't include a simple note of
what natal aspect is triggering"*** the insight.

**CHANI** (4.9 stars, ~2.9% negative): complaints are *operational* —
refunds, login errors, no iPad app — not editorial. The only pricing
complaint is a request for income-tiered pricing. **CHANI proves the
content isn't the category's problem; the business model and the tone
are.**

**The unmet need, synthesized:** (1) **explainability** — tell me which
placement produced this line; nobody serves it; (2) depth that survives
friends comparing screenshots; (3) a sharp voice that doesn't cost daily
anxiety; (4) **a price that can't change under you**; (5) a learning
path for users who get hooked.

## 5. What drives payment and sharing

Co–Star: the friends layer and the notification *voice* — its
notifications became a Know Your Meme entry with **13,662 documented
instances by March 2019**. The Pattern: relationship analysis in
skeptic-legible language. CHANI: the named human author. Astrotalk:
**80% of revenue from repeat buyers** — human connection retains better
than generated content.

**The share unit that travels is text, not charts** — short, first
person, emotionally sharp, recognizable at thumbnail size, and
impossible to fully understand without generating your own. That last
property is the conversion mechanism.

## 6. The young-audience angle

No credible public dataset covers 13–17 specifically — our 62% female /
25% aged 13–17 profile is ahead of published data, which is both an
informational edge and the largest compliance exposure. Pew (adults
only): 43% of women 18–49 believe in astrology vs 20% of men; **54% of
LGBT adults** consult astrology.

**Apple's Ask to Buy** applies by default to under-18s in some
jurisdictions and cannot be disabled — parental approval is required for
downloads *and* purchases regardless of age rating. **This argues for
the one-time model:** a parent waving through a single bounded $20
charge is an easier ask than an open-ended subscription.

**COPPA's amended rule (effective 23 June 2025)** now weighs actual user
demographics, not just design intent, and adds a mandatory written
security program, retention limits, and separate consent for
third-party disclosure. **Birth date + time + location is unusually
sensitive PII for a minor** — our in-browser computation is the right
posture.
[FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/ftc-finalizes-changes-childrens-privacy-rule-limiting-companies-ability-monetize-kids-data)

**UK Age Appropriate Design Code** applies to any service "likely to be
accessed by children" and reads **"nudge techniques" broadly — streaks,
daily-login rewards, scarcity, FOMO.** Our nightly loop is in scope. A
**DPIA is mandatory**, and its absence is what regulators hit first
(Reddit fined £14.47M in 2026). Penalties reach £17.5M or 4% of
worldwide turnover.

Mitigations that preserve the loop: non-scarce rewards, catch-up
windows, a finite set, no school-hours notifications, server-side spend
caps for under-18 accounts — most of which our ethics floor already
mandates.

## 7. Adjacent categories

**Human Design** — same input (birth data), same output (identity
artifact), far less saturated, and **no meaningful one-time option
exists.** Its four-type vocabulary is rarer and more
explanation-demanding than the zodiac, which makes it *more* shareable,
because the explanation is the share.

**Tarot** — ~$1.48B market (2026); digital is 28% of revenue, up from
12% in 2020 (vendor report, low confidence). **320+ tarot/oracle
Kickstarter campaigns in 2025 raising ~$12.8M combined.** The **daily
single-card pull is 27% of all readings** — the cleanest nightly-loop
primitive in the category. **Labyrinthos** is the model: free learning
app as funnel, physical decks as the product.

**Identity artifacts** — Receiptify was used 1M+ times in its first
months on a ~$2,000 grant with zero monetization; Instafest drove a
downstream app to four consecutive days of 100%+ day-over-day download
growth. The lesson is inverted: these convert to nothing because there's
no product behind them. **The artifact is extraordinarily cheap
distribution — but the paid thing must already exist, one tap away, at
the moment of the share.**
