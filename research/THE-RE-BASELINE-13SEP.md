**Lightening the debit made the seat worse, and removing it made the seat worse still.** The
seat heat was never in the debit. It is in the crossing counting two: on a nine-station road the
last word at the crossing is the leader's, and a crossing worth two is a last word worth two.
Every form that keeps the credit is over the bound; the heavier the debit against it, the more
the seat swings back toward the leader as the debit becomes the thing worth fighting for.

**The debit alone is inside every bound and does the brief's core.** Tortoise 27.9 → 38.8, the
tiger −15.4, seat −1.2 fresh and −4.0 deep, careful play unhurt. What it does not do is narrow by
twelve; it narrows by four, because the bird falls with the tiger and the floor rises to meet a
lower ceiling. **If Design wants the tortoise off the floor, this is the law: "the station on the
crossing's left counts one less."** If Design wants the twelve, the seat is the price and it is
not a price the bound allows. Kind `release` becomes a one-line `_slotW` debit at `L−1`, no
credit; the void's second line, on its own, with the sign on the other flank.

### What changed

**The bird fell on every night, by 3.6 to 17.9.** Design asked about the drum, the razor and the
throne: the drum is the largest drift on the ring (−17.9; the bird was never 42 there, it is 24),
the razor is the smallest (−3.6; the gathered stars' road barely uses the return), and the throne
has no old same-window row, its bird stands at 37.8 on the fixed engine.

**Because the bird was the floor or near it on half the ring, the plain spreads are wider than
the scorecard says**: the guide 49.7, the gate 49.3, the bearer and the chamber 49.0, the follower
47.1, the thread 46.1, the throne 44.3. Seven nights over 44 wide, plain.

**Three law verdicts change, and one flag becomes a fail:**

- **The hush widens its own window.** −3.0 before, **+7.9** now, with the bird −13.5 and every
  other quarter up 6 to 10. The district's law still cools the seat (−6.5 fresh) and it still does
  what it was built for on m21's door-first board; on the slid board the client plays, the bird was
  the phantom strikes' biggest beneficiary and the hush was the law that most hid it. **The pilot
  house's law fails the spread bound on its real road.** It should be re-read, not pulled: the row
  is one seed-pair and the widening is all in one quarter, but it is over.
- **The crow's fresh seat is +10.2**, over the bound; it was +4.1 on the home row. The narrowing is
  intact (−17.8, still the second largest on the ring behind the void). Seat exposure was the
  m4 card's own flagged risk and it is now the measured one.
- **The heart's beat widens 5.1**, a tenth over the bound, on a window (m18..m26, the listener's
  slid road) that also carries the listener's decision. Marginal; one more seed pair would settle
  it either way.
- **The shell's absolute fresh seat is 17.4** (plain 10.9, law +6.5), the flag from the stale-six
  note, unchanged in kind and now on clean numbers.

**Passes that stand:** the gate (−7.9), the toll (−3.7, still small), the razor (seat −4.1/−6.3,
flat), the price (**−19.6, the largest single narrowing on the ring**, all of it off the tiger on

**The deep seat, plain, is over 25 on four nights**: the gate **34.0**, the thread 29.9, the
