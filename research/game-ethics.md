# Research: Moon-Gated Daily Ritual — Precedents, Ethics Floor, Live Calendar

*Star Shard research corpus · August 11, 2026 · full agent report*

**The core mechanic in one sentence:** the moon moves through one of 28 mansions roughly per day (sidereal month ≈ 27.3 days; ~13°/day vs 12.9° mansions — [Lunar station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_station)), so "collect the card only while the moon stands in that mansion" is a real-clock mechanic with a deterministic ~27.3-day return. That determinism is the ethical trump card — but structurally the mechanic is a textbook "Playing by Appointment" pattern ([darkpattern.games](https://www.darkpattern.games/pattern/10/playing-by-appointment.html)), so everything below is about which precedents neutralize the risk.

## PART A — Precedents

**Wordle** — Wardle: "I kind of deliberately did what you're not meant to do if growth is your goal"; "people have an appetite for things that transparently don't want anything from you"; on push notifications: "I would definitely have to think about the contract I'm establishing with the player" ([TechCrunch interview](https://techcrunch.com/2022/01/12/josh-wardle-interview-wordle/); [TIME](https://time.com/6143715/wordle-sale-josh-wardle-interview/)). The spoiler-free emoji grid was player-invented, then formalized. **Copy:** daily scarcity as gift ("everyone gets today's moon together"); an aesthetic spoiler-free share artifact; a session-end signal. **Refuse:** >2-minute daily draws; push-notification acquisition.

**Animal Crossing: NH** — the real-clock precedent: real seasons, holidays, shop hours; research ties its temporal design to routine and wellbeing ([qualitative study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/359667866_Animal_Crossing_and_COVID-19_A_Qualitative_Study_Examining_How_Video_Games_Satisfy_Basic_Psychological_Needs_During_the_Pandemic); [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.800683/epub)); the community itself polices "time traveling" as undermining the philosophy ([Fandom](https://www.fandom.com/articles/time-traveling-animal-crossing)) — players understand and defend honest clocks; absence punished only cosmetically. **Copy:** clock as content; gentle reversible absence; annual events. **Refuse:** destructive decay. Star Shard bonus: the sky can't be time-traveled — the cheating debate is designed out.

**Neko Atsume / Tamagotchi** — the idle-games study ([Cutting et al., IJHCS](https://ray.yorksj.ac.uk/id/eprint/7953/1/BusyDoingNothingWhatDoPlayersDoInIdleGames.pdf)): 67.5% check many times daily in seconds-long sessions; "progress while gone" removes anxiety; 89% talk about the game — face-to-face sharing (78%) dwarfs social posting (28%); model engagement as habit, not flow. Tamagotchi effect = attachment to a digital object that needs you ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamagotchi_effect)) — but death-on-neglect is the cautionary half. **Copy:** glanceable live sky; seconds as a valid session; intimate over broadcast sharing. **Refuse:** care-pressure. The moon doesn't need you; it just returns.

**Pokémon GO Community Day** — the closest analogue: timed collection windows with reruns. Cutting 6h windows to 3h caused sustained backlash — work, weather, disability, time zones ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-go-niantic-stufful-community-day-hours-mobile-f-1848697447); [GameRant on FOMO/accessibility](https://gamerant.com/pokemon-go-timed-events-fomo-accessiblity/)); mercies came late (Community Day Classic reruns). **Copy:** synchrony + a published rerun schedule — the 27.3-day return is a stronger promise than Niantic ever made. **Refuse:** narrow clock-hour windows; a mansion "day" = the full ~24h+ transit, local-time agnostic.

**Duolingo streaks** — 7-day streak → 3.6× course completion; motivation shifts to loss aversion; Streak Freeze exists because loss demotivates, and adding slack raised DAU ([Duolingo's own blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit)). Dark side documented: streak anxiety, 11:58pm junk lessons ([Trophy.so case study](https://trophy.so/blog/duolingo-gamification-case-study); [UX Mag no-shame streaks](https://uxmag.com/articles/the-psychology-of-hot-streak-game-design-how-to-keep-players-coming-back-every-day-without-shame)). **Copy:** mercy as first-class, milestone celebration. **Refuse:** selling streak repairs; zeroing resets. Prefer counting **up** ("142 mansion-nights witnessed").

**Snapchat streaks — the teen cautionary tale.** Interviews with 14–18s: streaks create "a net of obligations, expectations and customary behaviors"; loss causes real distress requiring relationship repair ([Hristova et al., CHB Reports](https://oars.uos.ac.uk/2901/1/Why%20did%20we%20lose%20our%20snapchat%20streak.pdf)); quantitative link to problematic smartphone use and FOMO in early adolescents ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2772503023000476)). Key structural difference: **dyadic** — losing one damages a friendship. **Refuse entirely:** mutual obligation mechanics. Social = asynchronous gifts, no reply-timers, no shared counters.

**Gacha/live-ops** — FTC v. Genshin (Jan 2025): $20M; misrepresented odds; multi-tier currency "obscured real monetary costs"; marketing to teens via anime aesthetics; order bans loot-box sales to under-16s without parental consent ([FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/genshin-impact-game-developer-will-be-banned-selling-lootboxes-teens-under-16-without-parental)). Resin/energy systems create login obligation without satisfaction ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/genshin-impact-resin-patch-changes/)). Daily-login chains with paid restoration are a catalogued dark pattern ([darkpattern.games](https://www.darkpattern.games/pattern/11/daily-rewards.html)). **Learn:** cadence discipline, event dramaturgy. **Refuse publicly:** paid pulls, currency, energy, limited-forever. Talking point: **"No odds to disclose, because there are no odds. The sky is the drop table."**

**Collection psychology** — the incompleteness effect: visible empty slots raised completion 20.3% → 78.6%, but the effect **collapses when completion feels infeasible** ([JAMS study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11747-021-00835-6); foundational: [Carey 2008](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/joepsy/v29y2008i3p336-347.html)) → every empty slot needs a live return-countdown. Near-miss effects recruit gambling circuitry ([Clark et al., Neuron](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2658737/)) — Star Shard has no randomness; **never fake near-misses**. Zeigarnik tension is benign when completion is calendar-guaranteed. Panini's pain is randomized duplicates — keep swap sociality (gifting art variants) without randomized acquisition.

## PART B — The ethics floor

**Regulatory state 2024–26:** FTC active on games with minors — Epic/Fortnite $245M dark patterns + $520M package w/ COPPA ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/03/ftc-finalizes-order-requiring-fortnite-maker-epic-games-pay-245-million-tricking-users-making)); Genshin $20M (above). EU **Digital Fairness Act** proposal targeted Q4 2026 — dark patterns, addictive design, virtual-currency transparency, minors' protections ([Freshfields analysis](https://www.freshfields.com/en/our-thinking/blogs/technology-quotient/the-eus-proposed-digital-fairness-act-a-game-developers-guide-to-potential-imp-102ltio)); EP Oct 2025 resolution on minors and gambling-like mechanics ([EP](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20251013IPR30892/new-eu-measures-needed-to-make-online-services-safer-for-minors)). Belgium's ban under-enforced; Netherlands lost EA/FIFA on appeal; UK self-regulation (Ukie 2023) underperforms per Xiao's *Loot Box State of Play 2023* ([PDF](https://scispace.com/pdf/loot-box-state-of-play-2023-law-regulation-policy-and-2c1eqm0in6.pdf)). **What binds Star Shard:** with no paid randomized rewards, loot-box regimes don't apply — staying outside them is a strategic moat. What does: **COPPA** (Genshin order treats anime aesthetics + teen marketing as constructive knowledge of a young audience) and the **UK Age Appropriate Design Code** — Standard 13 prohibits exploitative nudges while permitting wellbeing nudges ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/age-appropriate-design-a-code-of-practice-for-online-services/13-nudge-techniques/)). Also: APA Health Advisory on adolescent social media flags compulsive-engagement features ([APA](https://www.apa.org/topics/social-media-internet/health-advisory-adolescent-social-media-use)); 5Rights *Playful by Design* for pressure-free play ([5Rights](https://5rightsfoundation.com/resource/playful-by-design/)).

### The guardrail list

**DO:** (1) frame absence as return — "the mansion returns in N days," live countdown on every empty slot (feasibility is what makes incompleteness motivating); (2) count up, never down — lifetime "nights witnessed," auto-granted "cloudy nights," never purchasable; (3) ship a session-end signal — "That's tonight's sky. See you under tomorrow's moon."; (4) make the astronomy the transparency story — publish the ephemeris, every card's availability computable years ahead; (5) windows = the moon's real ~24h+ transit plus grace (~48h effective); (6) social = asynchronous gifting only; (7) notifications opt-in, per-event, factual, default OFF for minors; (8) publish the ethics as product copy; (9) COPPA/AADC hygiene now — age gate without incentive to lie, high-privacy defaults; (10) merch may depict the collection, never advance it.

**DON'T:** (1) no purchasable pulls/cards/time-skips/streak repairs — ever, including "just cosmetic" randomized bundles; (2) no virtual currency; (3) no missable-forever content — every foil has a stated return path (next eclipse, anniversary reissue, trade); (4) no shame states — no broken-chain iconography, no "your moon missed you," no decay; (5) no countdown-urgency UI — present presence ("the moon is in Alnath tonight"), not expiry; (6) no engineered near-misses or fake scarcity; (7) no mutual obligation mechanics; (8) no dark-pattern age gating; (9) no infinite scroll appended to the ritual.

**Verdict on the core mechanic:** miss-it-wait-27-days is ethically acceptable *without* a catch-up mechanic provided (a) the return is visibly scheduled on every empty slot, (b) the window is the real transit + grace, (c) nothing compounds a miss, (d) framing is "the mansion returns," never "you missed it." If data later shows teens bingeing at 11:59pm, add a "lantern" mercy: one held card per cycle, claimable late — free, automatic, non-stacking.

## PART C — The live calendar

**Cadence:** industry layers weekly micro / monthly medium / quarterly tentpole beats, with templatization and a 2–3-event buffer; "the hidden failure mode of LiveOps is not player fatigue but team fatigue" ([Playio](https://blog.playio.co/liveops-strategy-mobile-games-retention); [PocketGamer.biz 2026 trends](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/2026-live-ops-trends-templatisation-personalisation-and-ai/)). **Star Shard's structural cheat: astronomy IS the calendar** — daily beat auto-generates once 28 cards exist; minimum viable two-person cadence = one templated moon-phase skin/month + 4–6 tentpole treatments/year, built a season ahead; everything pre-computable years out.

**Beats, Sep 2026 → Dec 2027** (dates verified in `research/transits.md`): Sep 26 Harvest Moon launch tentpole · Oct 4 Saturn opposition badge · Oct 6 Moon-occults-Jupiter variant · Oct 24–Nov 13 Mercury-rx mirrored card backs · **Nov 24 + Dec 24 + Jan 22 triple-supermoon arc → collect all three = "Perigee" badge** · **Dec 14 Geminids (best shower; the mini-game trigger: a shooting star streaks the site — clicking it starts Shard Runner)** · Dec 21 solstice dark-mode skin · **Feb 6 2027 "Ring of Fire" annular foil** · Feb 19–20 Mars opposition + eclipsed Snow Moon double feature · **May 20 2027 literal blue-tinted card — "once in a blue moon"** · Jul 1/Aug 11/Oct 10 Venus–Mercury "cosmic waltz" triptych (mid-date unobservable — tell it honestly: the album records what the sky does, not just what we can see) · Jul 18 mag-0.001 eclipse = connoisseur hidden badge · **Aug 2 2027 TOTAL ECLIPSE — the flagship: "Day the Sun Went Dark," unique foil with a scheduled reissue promise** · Sep 15 2027 first-anniversary Harvest Moon event · **monthly: the Moon occults the Pleiades = mansion #3's card shimmers for a real, checkable reason — the signature recurring beat nobody can copy.**

**Easter eggs:** discovery = social currency; triggers must be retellable in one sentence (Konami code, "do a barrel roll") ([TechCrunch on Google's Konami egg](https://techcrunch.com/2014/03/19/googles-konami-code-easter-egg-unlocks-cheat-mode)); hide in layers; never gate behind payment or data. **Side-scroller gating:** unlock via sky-knowledge (tracing tonight's asterism, typing a mansion's Arabic name) and/or auto-reveal during real meteor showers; once found, docked forever (hidden ≠ missable-forever); finders get a subtle profile mark; seed the reveal through fandom wikis/Discords — drop one cryptic clue in the Aug 2 2027 eclipse art.
