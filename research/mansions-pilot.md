## v2 change: Japanese-first naming — and why it's even better than the vibe call

Per Justin's direction, mansions lead with their Japanese names. Two things make
this stronger than an aesthetic choice:

1. **It works cleanly for all 28.** Japan has *two* mansion traditions: the modern
   27-station sukuyō fortune system (which drifts one station from our grid in the
   Aquarius–Pisces stretch), and the older **nijūhasshuku (二十八宿)** — the
   28-mansion cycle used in Japanese calendars, temples and Buddhist star mandalas.
   We name from the nijūhasshuku, which maps 1:1 to our 28 stations. The 27-sukuyō
   stays underneath as the compatibility engine. One grimoire line covers it.
2. **The Fushigi Yūgi warrior names ARE the old Japanese readings.** Watase didn't
   invent the warrior names — 鬼宿 is read *Tamahome*, 星宿 is *Hotohori*, 昴宿 is
   *Subaru*, 尾宿 is *Ashitare*. The extraction table confirms the pattern across
   27 of 28 mansions. So Japanese-first display names mean **your mansion's name
   literally is a Fushigi Yūgi character name** — because the characters were named
   from the mansions. `[VERIFY]` the full wamyō (old-reading) list against a
   printed nijūhasshuku source before launch; the FY wiki is the scaffold, not the
   citation.

**Display pattern (proposed):** kanji + old Japanese reading + English gloss, with
the Arabic, Sanskrit and Chinese names as the "in four skies" depth layer. The
Arabic corpus (the sajʿ sayings, the anwāʾ calendar) remains the richest *lore*
source — this changes the front of the card, not the depth behind it.

**Flags:** `[HISTORY-ONLY]` Picatrix/Agrippa = presented as history, never practice
· `[THIN]` = sources run out; we say so rather than pad · `[VERIFY]` = confirm
against a primary source before launch.

**Position (equal-28, tropical):** 25°43′ Aries – 8°34′ Tau
**Stars:** the Pleiades (M45) · determinant Alcyone (η Tau)
**FY:** Subaru, warrior of Byakko `[VERIFY wamyō]`

### The names

| Sky | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Japan** | **昴宿 (Subaru)** | the gathered stars — the everyday Japanese word for the Pleiades to this day; count the stars in the car logo ♡ `[VERIFY cite]` |

| China | 昴 Mǎo | White Tiger of the West · animal: rooster |

flagship of the "four cultures, one moon-road" story.

opened the autumn rains the desert year turned on. `[VERIFY]` the crop-blight
ḥadīth (sunnah.com) before any use.

sailors, huntsmen, and alchemists." `[HISTORY-ONLY]` Picatrix: safe sailing,
fire-work, married love.

**FY:** Tomite, warrior of Genbu `[VERIFY wamyō]`

### The names

| Sky | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Japan** | **虛宿 (Tomite)** | via the Chinese station: **the Void, Emptiness** |
| China | 虛 Xū | "Emptiness" · Black Tortoise of the North · animal: rat · **same determinant star, β Aqr** |
| Arabia | Saʿd al-Suʿūd | **"the Luckiest of the Lucky"** — the grandest of the ten *Saʿd* (luck) asterisms |
| India | Śatabhiṣā | "the hundred physicians" · deity Varuṇa · symbol: an empty circle (neighboring star — PARTIAL) |

**The entry's own tension is the content:** three skies looked at the same star.
One saw the luckiest day of the year. One saw emptiness. One saw a hundred healers
holding an empty circle. *maybe good luck is just an open space where something
kind can land.* That line is the card.

### The old calendars

Arabic anwāʾ: enters ~February 12 — **the thaw**. The rhymed sajʿ, still quoted in
Gulf weather media every February (a living calendar, not a museum piece):

> *"When Saʿd al-Suʿūd rises, wood flourishes, hides soften, everything frozen
> melts, and water runs through the plants."*

### Election lore — *tradition says*

good intentions." Robson: build, marry, make friends, travel. `[HISTORY-ONLY]`

### The names

| Sky | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Japan** | **星宿 (Hotohori)** | "the Star" mansion |
| Arabia | al-Jabha | "the Forehead of the Lion" — the brow of the old-Arab super-Lion that sprawled across a third of the sky |

proudly — it's what makes the STRONG matches mean something.

The Arabic calendar's oddity: **the only mansion allotted 14 days of the solar
year instead of 13.** The lion got an extra day. `[THIN]` — deeper anwāʾ detail
awaits the specialist reviewer (Ibn Qutaybah is untranslated); not padded.
