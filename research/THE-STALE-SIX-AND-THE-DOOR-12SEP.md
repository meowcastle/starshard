# The stale six re-cut, the ill road split, and the door is a leader's square on every night

**12 September 2026. For Design and Code.** Three asks from the 12 Sep notes, one batch. 896 boards
a cell, two seeds, walker boards, 138 vectors green. **The door-order row is now section 4 of the
plain harness** (`runN.js`), leader/answerer share and the quarter of the first door card, fresh and
deep; it comes free with every m-plain from here on, as asked.

**The finding that runs under all three:** the leader lodges the door first on **every night
measured, 66 to 73 percent**, fresh and deep, on eight different roads. It is not m9's fact. It is
the game's: loser-leads plus a nine-station road makes the door the opening move's natural square
everywhere. So the leader share will not tell one night from another. **The quarter split will**,
and it moves a lot.

## 1. The ill road split (m9, standard window)

| | leader first | byakko | suzaku | seiryuu | genbu |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| plain, fresh | 72.1% | 23.9 | **38.6** | 20.7 | 16.9 |
| ill road 1, fresh | 73.0% | 21.3 | **45.5** | 17.3 | 15.9 |
| plain, deep | 70.9% | 25.6 | 20.6 | 19.0 | 34.8 |
| ill road 1, deep | 68.2% | 28.3 | 22.8 | 18.4 | 30.5 |

*(Par for a random seven-card hand is 25 a quarter.)*

**Fresh is Design's first branch, exactly.** On plain the bird takes the door far above par, 38.6
against 25. Under the ill road the bird's share *rises* to 45.5 while all three other quarters fall
(tiger −2.6, dragon −3.4, tortoise −1.0). The home-count door keeps drawing the bird and the tax
turns the others away. **The ill road is a bird law on a bird door: placement, not fix.** It is
retired as a spread instrument for m9; if the ill road ever ships it is as a level fiction on a
night whose door the house's own quarter already wants, and it will widen there too.

The deep row is a caution rather than a contradiction: deep, the bird is at par at the door (20.6)
and the tortoise takes it most (34.8), because the tortoise's grant is a count and the door is a
count square once every card is level three. The bird's +10.9 in the deep quadrant row is measured
on an all-bird fixed hand, where the door-taker is a bird card by construction, so the split cannot
speak to it. What the split does say is that the law's *fresh* effect, the one a real player meets,
is the bird's door share compounding. That is enough to decide it.

## The door-order rule, amended

Design adopted the door-order row as a pre-sheet read on the strength of one night. Eight nights
later the leader share is flat at about 70 everywhere and cannot do the job it was adopted for.
**The quarter split can**, and it is what killed the ill road. Amended rule: before a night's sheet,
read the deep seat on both windows and the door's quarter split on both windows; the leader share
is printed with them for completeness and should not decide anything.
