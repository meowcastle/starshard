# The 28 Names — epithet slate (v3, for review)

**Pattern locked per Justin:** English epithet as the display/archetype name ("The
Void"), all four cultural names as the depth layer, star-seed interstellar art
direction on the cards. Each epithet is a translation or direct derivation from
one of the four real traditions — **no invented lore**. The `source` column says
which sky named it; where the Arabic meaning was awkward for a family-friendly
card (e.g. "Luck of the Slaughterer"), the epithet draws from another sky of the
same station and the Arabic stays, honestly glossed, in the depth layer.

**Naming-system rule (so the two systems never blur):** mansions are always
"The ___". The mirror shard's 16 element-archetypes will use compound single
words (Hearthkeeper-style) — different morphology, no collisions.

## Sanity checks on the set

- 28 distinct names, no collisions with product vocabulary ("The Crossing" is
  reserved for the daily transit; "The Star" avoided as too generic + tarot clash).
- Tone spread: cozy (Hideaway, Chamber, Listener), cosmic (Gathered Stars, Void,
  Root, Thread), bold (Throne, Storm, Claws, Blaze) — a real deck, not 28 flavors
  of soft.
- Source mix: 13 Arabic, 8 Sanskrit, 4 Chinese, 1 quadruple, 2 structural — the
  "all the cultures" brief, embodied in the names themselves.
- Every card's depth table still shows all four skies with match flags; the
  epithet is presentation, not replacement.

## Star-seed note for Claude Design (tarot template)

Card context stays (dark teal / cream ink per the design system), art direction
leans interstellar: the mansion as a *seed of light* — its actual asterism as the
constellation mark, the epithet as the large type, kanji as the corner glyph, the
four names small along the base. The Void and The Ghost should look like cards
people fight over.
