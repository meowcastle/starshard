## 🔧 CORRECTED — applied or one-line edits

**1 · The ḥadīth (pilot #3) — attribution corrected.** It is real but **not Abū
Dāwūd**: *Musnad Aḥmad* 8495 — *"When the star rises in the morning, the blight
is lifted"* — graded ḥasan by al-Arnaʾūṭ (discussed, not unanimous), and
commentators read "the star" as the Thurayyā and the blight as crop blight.
The related canonical anchor is Bukhārī 2193 (fruit not sold until the Thurayyā
rises). **Pilot edit:** cite "a tradition preserved in Musnad Aḥmad" + Bukhārī
2193 for the practice; keep the framing light.
([hadithprophet 12729](https://hadithprophet.com/hadith-12729.html) · [Bukhārī 2193](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2193) · [Mufti WP 495](https://muftiwp.gov.my/en/artikel/irsyad-al-hadith/4435-irsyad-al-hadith-series-495-the-rise-of-thurayya-star-is-the-sign-of-the-end-of-a-pandemic))

**2 · Claws → Scales (#16) — framing corrected, and improved.** "The Romans cut
them off" was wrong: **Babylon had both readings**, and the Balance came first —
zibanītu, "the scales of heaven," in star lists c. 1000 BC. The *Greeks* demoted
the stars to the Scorpion's claws; *Rome restored* the older Balance. Best part:
Arabic *zubānā* (claw) is etymologically related to Babylonian *zibanītu* —
which meant *balance*. **Even the word "claw" has a scale hidden inside it.**
Applied to the reissued batch 3.
([languagehat](https://languagehat.com/zubeneschamali/) · [All Skies](https://ase.exopla.net/index.php/Zubenelgenubi) · [Libra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libra_(constellation)))

**3 · The galactic center (#19 → #21) — moved, and it's better now.** Sgr A*
sits at ecliptic longitude ≈ 266.9° = **26°51′ Sagittarius tropical**, which is
**not** station #19 — it is station **#21, The Empty District.** Independently
verified by the agent against my computation. And Ibn Qutaybah's own words for
al-Balda: *"a patch of sky with no stars in it."* So the corrected fact is the
best trivia in the deck: **the starless mansion secretly contains the heart of
the galaxy.** (The sidereal claim — Mūla points at the galactic center — is
separately true in the Vedic frame; the grimoire can hold both.) Applied to the
reissued batch 3.
([Sgr A*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagittarius_A*) · [Ibn Qutaybah](https://shamela.ws/book/9093/122) · [Vedanet](https://www.vedanet.com/the-shiva-kali-axis-in-vedic-astrology-and-its-alignment-in-2020/))
