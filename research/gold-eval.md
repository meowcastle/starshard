## 1. The comparison is not like for like, and that matters

The gold combination sections are built out of borrowed material: al-ṭarf,
aldebaran, praesepe, kṛttikā, the bat, the goat, the stag. The shipped
cells are forbidden every one of those by the borrowed-fact gate, because
the portrait renders directly under the cell and would say them twice.

So the machine was asked to reach the same place with the tools taken
away. The honest test is not whether the text matches. It is whether the
**distinction** survives, whether the pull line still earns a screenshot,
and whether §5c holds. On that test:

**Eight of eight preserve the pairing's distinction. Five of eight are
better than the approved text at the `cost` slot.** Zero fail §5c: no
cell describes a mansion instead of the pairing, none offers advice, none
reads as flattery, none survives a two-word swap.

That is the pass grading itself and passing. It should be said plainly
before the rest of this document, which is about a defect the eval found
that no gate was ever built to see.

## 2. Cell by cell

### 06·10 · exceeds gold

Gold's fusion: *"so the storm has to be conducted from a chair."* Shipped
finds a mechanism instead of an image: composure makes irreversibility
read as intent. `sun`: "the size of what you did is entered as the size of
what you meant." The pull is the best line in the eight:

> you are never seen deciding, only seen having decided, so the worst hour
> of your life stands in the record with your posture on it and no evidence
> that it took you by surprise.

Gold's cost was "nobody checks on the person who never looks like they
need checking on." Shipped: "apologies, though, have to look like they
cost the person making them, and yours never will, so the ones you owe
cannot be paid, and the people owed them wait, and then stop." Same
insight, one turn further, and it names a consequence rather than a
condition. **No changes proposed.**

### 01·08 · exceeds gold

Gold: "you turn up first because someone has to put the kettle on," cost
"feeders eat last and call it a personality." Both are good lines and both
are portable to any Ghost pairing. Shipped derives the whole cell from one
observation gold does not make: arriving first means no one ever had to
decide they wanted you, so the question never closes, and the giving is
the answer to it. Cost: "a household that stops needing you and one that
has gone off you reach you as the same event — as quiet."

One flag, `sun`: "for thirty years." **Rewrite proposed** (see §4).

### 28·08 · exceeds gold

Gold states the cost: "you belong to both sides and are counted by
neither." Shipped derives it: a join that works has nothing of you left in
it, so only the failures still name you. Closing line:

> the middle is a length of rope, and the households you built have a word
> for each other that has never been used about you.

**No changes proposed.**

### 23·04 · matches, and inherits well

Gold's last sentence is "the person you are following has no idea they are
being used as a pace-setter by someone faster." Shipped takes that
throwaway and builds the cell on it: the count was taken off someone's
best two years, everyone now keeps it permanently, and because the gap is
a choice rather than a speed it cannot be closed by going faster, so the
whole room accelerates forever toward a mark that moves. This is the
clearest case of the corpus improving on its own source.

Three flags, all the same kind: "before you were thirty" (`lead`), "has
been running for decades" (`moon`), "ten years of raising a whole place's
rate" (`cost`). **Rewrites proposed.**

### 09·22 · matches

Distinct from 09·12 in the same column, which is the thing the collision
gate cannot check: 09·12 is the Glance that knows early, 09·22 is the
Glance that works at range. "the wire was never a downgrade — the same
instrument, at a longer reach." Low-severity: "people you have not sat in
a room with for a decade." **No changes proposed.**

Worth noting for §3: gold 09·22's own cost line is *"it is entirely
possible to reach forty inside that arrangement."*

### 03·28 · better fusion, one hard fail

Gold admits its own redundancy: "both halves of your chart describe the
same wound from opposite ends." Shipped makes the two mansions act on each
other instead: the cluster is six lights read as one, the cord is the
tension holding them in a line, and the steadiness belongs to the pull
rather than the person. On the day both ends let go, "all six of you are
still there, undamaged, and unrecognisable to everybody." That is a better
cell than the approved one.

It also contains the corpus's clearest universality failure:

> a man holds one shape for two decades and then, three months after the
> funeral, wants different things in different weeks

Reader gendered male, two decades asserted, and a bereavement supplied.
**Five rewrites proposed** covering the gender, the elapsed time, and the
death.

### 09·12 · matches, written to one person's life

The distinction is exactly gold's (the eye grips, the skin sheds) and the
cost is better than gold's: gold says no one gets a warning; shipped says
"nobody in that house got a bad year out of you," which is the same fact
told generously, and recognition over accusation is the house rule.

But the cell narrates Corey's marriage. "go to her mother's at the
weekend." "eight years out, remarried, you still hold her at the
resolution of that last year." Corey's reading may say this. The cell that
every 09·12 user reads may not: it hands them a wife, a separation, and a
second marriage. **Two rewrites proposed** that keep the kitchen and the
year and drop the biography.

### 04·11 · the one real divergence

Gold's Mane is the annual crown: "what moved you at twenty embarrasses you
at thirty," and the fusion is "no attachment to position at all." Shipped
drops the shedding entirely and reads the Mane as display-as-size, which
is the line RESUME-784 records eight cells teaching into row 11. Its
fusion is different: everything you have was given, and the one thing you
took is a distance, so closing it would file it with the rest.

This is coherent, it is more specific to the pair than gold's (which is
partly Follower-general), and it is defensible. But it is the one cell of
the eight where the approved distinction was not inherited, and that is
worth a conscious decision rather than a silent one. **Justin's call:
keep, or re-cut against gold.** One time assertion flagged either way.

## 3. What the eval actually found

The eight cells are good. The scan they prompted is the finding.

**367 of 784 cells (47%) assert elapsed adult time about the reader.**
557 sentences. By slot: `cost` 180, `lead` 91, `tension` 86, `sun` 68,
`pull` 68, `moon` 64. Raw counts across the corpus: "twenty years" 167,
"thirty years" 149, "decades" 132, "for a decade" 42, "fifteen years" 28,
"forty years" 21.

Verbatim, from the first row alone:

- 01·03 `moon`: "so twenty years of the hardest evidence available has not produced a sentence you can finish about yourself."
- 01·05 `sun`: "nobody running purely on duty answers for thirty years; duty runs out."
- 01·15 `cost`: "thirty years of it has built no habit at all of being reached."
- 01·17 `cost`: "you hold to it for thirty years without a review."

**Why this is a defect and not a style note.** PRODUCT.md puts roughly a
quarter of the audience at 13–17. To a fifteen-year-old, "twenty years of
the hardest evidence available" is not unflattering, it is *false*, and a
false sentence in the signature card is the one thing the whole
architecture is built to avoid. WRITING.md's law is recognition, accuracy
that includes the cost. An assertion of elapsed decades is the exact
texture of the generic astrology the project positions against, and it
lands hardest in `cost`, the last slot read and the emotional payload.

**Why the gates never saw it.** It is not an 8-gram, not a 5-gram frame,
not a banned construction, not a leaked fact. It is a repeated
*assumption*, and it is invisible cell by cell for the same reason §0
says everything else is: nobody reads two cells side by side except us.
This is §4c's mechanism arriving one generation on again. The writers
stopped announcing the cost, then stopped announcing the count, and
started **assuming the calendar**.

**And the few-shot taught it.** Three of the eight approved readings
assert the reader's age: 01·08 "at seventy-six, you will know precisely
how true that has been," 04·11 "what moved you at twenty embarrasses you
at thirty," 09·22 "reach forty inside that arrangement." Those readings
were written *for named adults*, where the assertion is true and earned.
Handed to a generator as gold, the habit generalised to 47% of the
corpus. That is the causal chain, and it is the strongest argument in this
document for why §5b.2 was supposed to run before the other 756 cells,
not after.

Smaller, related, and quantified so they are not overstated:

- **Gendered reader: 1 clear case.** 03·28's "a man holds one shape." The
  other 29 hits on `a man who` / `a woman who` are third parties inside a
  scenario, which is correct house style and should be left alone.
- **Asserted family: 4 cells** name the reader's children or daughter
  (13·26, 14·26 among them). "the funeral" appears in 11 cells, mostly as
  a scene rather than an assertion, which reads fine.

## 4. Proposed rewrites

Twelve slots across five cells. Every one validated: word counts inside
the §1 ranges, zero hits on the nineteen banned frames literally and
punctuation-normalised, zero hits on the ten style regexes, and the full
harness re-run over a patched corpus returns **784/784 clean, 0
collisions, 0 over-used frames**. In `run/gold/proposals.json`.

| cell | slot | was | becomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 09·12 | sun | "go to her mother's at the weekend" | "turn up to the birthdays" |
| 09·12 | cost | "eight years out, remarried, you still hold her at the resolution of that last year" | "still held at the resolution of that last year" |
| 23·04 | lead | "by ear before you were thirty" | "by ear, early" |
| 23·04 | moon | "has been running for decades" | "has never once been stated" |
| 23·04 | cost | "ten years of raising a whole place's rate" | "years of raising a whole place's rate" |
| 04·11 | cost | "thirty years of what was handed to you … since you were nineteen" | "everything that was handed to you … before you were old enough to notice it was happening" |
| 01·08 | sun | "for thirty years" | "ever since" |
| 03·28 | lead | "twenty years of being called steady" | "a lifetime of being called steady" |
| 03·28 | sun | "two decades pulled straight from both ends" | "years pulled straight from both ends" |
| 03·28 | moon | "she dies … twenty years of evidence" | "one of them goes … years of evidence" |
| 03·28 | tension | "a man holds one shape for two decades … after the funeral" | "somebody holds one shape for years … after the ending" |
| 03·28 | cost | "learned about you in twenty years" | "learned about you across all of it" |

The pattern in every one: **the duration was never carrying the
sentence.** "twenty years of being called steady" and "a lifetime of being
called steady" say the same thing, and only one of them is a claim about
the reader's date of birth. That is what makes this cheap to fix at scale.

## 5. What I would do next

1. **Add an eleventh gate: the calendar gate.** Flag any second-person
   sentence asserting elapsed years. It is a twenty-line regex and it
   would have caught 557 sentences before a human read one. Pair it with
   the positive instruction §4c says a ban always needs, or the tell will
   move again: *duration may be shown in the scene, never asserted about
   the reader.* A cell may say "the birthdays were done properly." It may
   not say "for thirty years."
2. **Sweep the 367 at the same fixed absolute standard** RESUME-784 uses
   for frames. Most are one-word deletions, as above. This is a mechanical
   pass with human sign-off, not a rewrite.
3. **Fix 03·28's gendered reader** whatever else happens. One cell, one
   word, and it currently tells a share of its readers the card is not
   about them.
4. **Decide 04·11**: keep the divergence or re-cut against gold.
5. **Commit the recovered gold set** as `combos-goldset.json` so §5b.2 is
   re-runnable, and add the three age-asserting few-shot lines to
   GENERATION.md §4g's list of things the gold teaches that the corpus
   must not inherit. §4g exists because the expensive lesson lived only in
   a scratch file; this is the same shape of loss.
6. **Then the columns.** The 28 sittings are still owed and still item 1.
   This eval says what to read for on the first pass.
