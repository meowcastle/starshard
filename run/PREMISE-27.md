## What a sun in The Guide *is*

A sun in **The Guide** takes other people the rest of the way. Not advice, not
encouragement — you go with them, along a route you know and they do not, and
they get where they were trying to get. The god here is the one who **knows the
paths and travels them for other people's sake**: roads, lost things, animals
that stray. It is the last station on one of the wheels, and what it does is
close a journey that somebody else started.

You are extremely good at this. It is probably the least remarked-on competence
in your life, because the traveller experiences it as *having arrived*, not as
having been taken. That is correct. It is what a good delivery feels like from
the inside.

**And the mansion withholds arrival — yours.** The one who walks travellers to
the far side **does not come with them.** Your work ends at the boundary of the
country the traveller wanted, which is not a country you were going to. Every
journey in your life terminates with you turning around at an edge, alone, with
the person you brought there already walking on and not looking round, which is
exactly what you wanted for them.

**And the one talent this mansion does not confer is knowing what to do once
the delivery is made.** Not sadness about it — a genuine blank. The route was
the whole of the competence. Standing at the edge with nothing to do next is a
situation you have been in a hundred times and have never got any better at.

## The distinctions this row must hold

### 27 The Guide vs 26 The Chamber — *both close the road*

**This line is already drawn and all 28 cells of row 26 are written against
it.** **26 encloses; 27 goes ahead of someone.** You are writing the other
side.

- **The Guide** — you **move**, out in front of a person, on a route, and you
  **stop at a boundary** that is not your destination. The thing you supply is
  passage.
- **The Chamber** — the enclosure **stays put**, holds, has an inside and an
  outside, and is not looked at. Nothing in it leads anybody anywhere.

**Row 26's batch D caught a real drift proving this bites**: its `26·14` was

way.** If your cell has you building or holding something that people live
inside, it is a 26 cell. Yours is never a structure. It is a walk.

Row 13 is written. **13 starts motion in someone else and the motion runs on
without it, uncounted, under another name.** You do not start anything — the
traveller was already going — and you do not detach: **you are physically along
for the distance and then you stop.** If your cell's output leaves your hand
and runs on, it is a 13 cell. Note `27·13` is your farlight cell, where both
are live at once.
