## Batch A — 14·01 … 14·04

- **14·01** *work / trade.* The tradesman who carries a finished piece into the
  shop the day it works, failures included. The method is named after him in
  six other shops, credited openly, and pays him nothing. **Credit and payment
  as two separate arrangements.**
- **14·02** *the family you came from.* The object you made at twenty-four
  hangs in your mother's hall and is the family's proof of itself — and the
  account of that house which would correct it has been carried, undelivered,
  since you were fifteen. You built the exhibit for a case you would testify
  against.
- **14·03** *money.* Seven people in the city do this work; everyone who hires
  treats the seven as one rate. You are visibly the best of them and are paid
  the group average. Being singled out by eye has never moved the price.
- **14·04** *the body.* Twenty years of holding a length under one training
  partner you could beat, by unspoken choice. The body that produced is the
  first thing anyone mentions, and no sentence you own ends a conversation
  about it.

## Batch B — 14·05 … 14·08

- **14·05** *friendship.* The thing you and one friend made in a back room,
  finished and admired and yours by name. Your curiosity was three interests
  away by the time custody was decided, so the friend who stayed is now the
  authority on it — and correcting him would convert seven years of stewardship
  into a theft he never committed.
- **14·06** *a public room, where the scene goes right.* Four warm, accurate,
  unqualified sentences about a man's work, said at a launch. They are on every
  edition since, in your name, unrecallable. His last books are thin; the only
  available withdrawal is a silence loud enough to end him.
- **14·07** *resumed after years away.* Nine years out, the sign still carries
  your name, kept there in real fondness. The return is exactly as expected and
  entirely ceremonial — a seat, a photograph on the stairs, no vote. No clause
  was ever written, because writing one meant imagining a day you and the place
  were on different sides.
- **14·08** *grief.* The memorial object you made after his death is the best
  work of your life and the place everyone else now goes to grieve him. The
  family owns the version of him it carries, and stopping any part of it would
  read as taking him back off the people who loved him.

## Batch C — 14·09 … 14·12

- **14·09** *strangers.* A stranger who met the work before he met you. You
  read his intention at the handshake; the reading converts into nothing but
  storage. He leaves with the thing and you keep the minute for eleven years.
- **14·10** *institutions.* A position carved three occupants before you, where
  every use of your one signed piece arrives dressed as a tribute — **and a
  tribute has no refusal form.**
- **14·11** *the 14/11 distinction.* Booked into a room sized to the reputation
  and arriving with the actual object: narrow, finished, genuinely good.
  Quality and width arrive fused and neither is adjustable, so the impression's
  excess is charged to the work, and *overrated* gets passed on the object by
  people correcting an estimate the object never made.
- **14·12** *an ending.* A total molt nine years back: the maker is gone
  entire, the name never moved, decisions about the piece happen in rooms you
  are not in, and the owing does not shed.

## Batch D — 14·13 … 14·16

- **14·13** *the adjacent pair.* A large, properly attributed body of work, and
  a trade quietly populated by people whose first good day happened at your
  bench and who owe you nothing on paper. The field's makers all began in your
  room, none was told so.
- **14·14** *THE DIAGONAL.* Quality is read by the room as proof that
  arrangements exist. The half-second in which a guard would go in is empty at
  every point in the sequence.
- **14·15** *not being placed.* The work is housed everywhere and correctly
  credited; the maker has no gallery, faculty or firm, so the nearest
  institution is whichever one is keeping it — and it speaks for the piece as
  its own holding.
- **14·16** *a fight.* A four-year dispute over a taken design in which you
  carry the taker's case at full weight — a grip that closes on both sides and
  therefore closes nothing.

  had it in two of four and kept it only where it is the point (`14·11`).
- **`sun` openers that state a general law about good work** — batch D had two

  batch B's four.

  The row's central fact is attribution, so this phrase is the row's natural
  tic. Say it once per cell at most, and prefer the specific form (name on the

  them diagonals. Do not add the twelfth.

## The row-13 boundary

Row 13 (The Hand) was written immediately before this one and is in the corpus.
**The Hand's making is causal and untraceable — motion going out under someone
else's name, uncounted. The Jewel's is visible and attributed.** If a cell of
yours has output that is untraceable, uncounted, or running under another
person's name, it has walked into row 13. Everyone knows exactly what you made
and exactly who made it — that is the precondition for the cost, not a
softening of it.
