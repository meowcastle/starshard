## Batch A — 22·01 … 22·04

- **22·01** *work / a trade.* The delivery that never failed, the man who never
  walked off, the week that never came apart — settled by half past nine on an
  ordinary morning, and no record anywhere holds a line about it. **Every
  collapse you prevented is unprovable: a thing that did not happen looks
  exactly like a thing that was never coming.**
- **22·02** *the family you came from.* Your brother calls that house a happy
  one and he is not lying — he was nine. What he was kept clear of was kept
  clear of by somebody, and the childhood your siblings describe is accurate
  *because* a nine-year-old was working.
- **22·03** *money.* It never leaves as one amount — a tank of fuel, a bill
  settled before it reaches the table, a coat bought twice so one can be handed
  on. Four households came through bad winters on you and each would say,
  honestly, that nobody helped.
- **22·04** *the body.* A room adjusting around a body makes a sound, and you
  have been getting it since fourteen. Nobody has ever said one word to you
  about your body; you have had the room's real position on bodies at full
  volume for twenty years, out of conversations about other people.

## Batch C — 22·09 … 22·12

- **22·09** *the 22/09 contrast — intake feeding a grip.* One sentence a woman
  said about her sister in a kitchen you were passing through, nine years ago.
  You have kept them apart at every table since, and both of them let it go
  long ago.
- **22·10** *inherited position.* The name over the door was your
  grandmother's, and you have known since nineteen exactly what the senior
  staff think about that, in their own words, none of which were said to you.
  The firm runs because of you and the record cannot show it.
- **22·11** *display as size.* The first thing anyone learns is that you are
  the one to go to, said at a volume you did not set. You are known for the
  half that was free; the half with a price on it has no audience and no way of
  getting one without your saying so.
- **22·12** *an absolute ending.* Eleven months after you left, the department
  came apart in a way nobody could account for, and the review put it down to a
  difficult year. No sentence exists that would get it from you to them.

## Batch D — 22·13 … 22·16

- **22·13** *craft.* The cabinet ordered plain and delivered plain, with the
  shelf set where her mother can reach it without asking, and nobody at that
  address has ever remarked on it. **Both halves are unshowable** — the
  difficulty it answers never became visible, and the making went out under a
  name that is not yours.
- **22·14** *a piece of work everyone can see.* Visitors get walked through the
  corridor width, the chairs, the quiet room by the door instead of at the end.
  Eleven years of listening turned into a floor plan — the only part of your
  working life anybody can point at.
- **22·15** *not being placed.* *How do you two know each other?* — and the
  true answer runs through a kitchen at midnight and a sentence her friend has
  never said aloud. **People tell you everything because you are not
  anybody's**, and afterwards nobody involved can say what you were to them.
- **22·16** *a fight.* Eleven years into a boundary dispute that should have
  taken one. You know what the man next door is actually defending, it has
  nothing to do with the fence, and winning that one is worth more and shows
  nothing.

## Live banned-token traps

**Banned in all 28 cells of this row:** **`three`** · **`crossed`** ·
**`worlds`** · `footprints` · `strides` · `dabih` · `giedi`.

**`three` and `crossed` are ordinary English.** `third`, `thirteen`, `cross`,
`crossing`, `crosses` are separate tokens and pass; the bare forms do not — the
failure is one letter away and looks fine.

`ear` and the epithet are permitted. Sign names and month names leak, including
the bare modal **`may`**.

## The two boundaries

**Row 20 The Flock is written and merged.** The Flock is about **where you
stand** — of a group and at its edge at once, and the position lets you *sort*.
The Listener is about **what reaches you** — an intake that cannot be shut.
**If your subject is standing half in and half out of a group, or on the roll
and off it, it has walked into row 20.**

**Row 09 The Glance is the grip** — the read taken and held. Yours is the
**channel**, always open, never closed. If your cell is about *retaining* a
reading rather than *not being able to stop taking it in*, it is a 09 cell.
(`22·09` is the one cell where both are deliberately live.)
