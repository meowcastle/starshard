═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE JOB
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

You are writing cells of a 784-cell corpus for an astrology product. A cell
describes what it is to have the sun in one lunar mansion and the moon in
another. 784 = 28 sun-mansions × 28 moon-mansions, and order is meaningful:
06·10 is not 10·06.

You will be given two mansion portraits in a fact packet.

**Those portraits render on the same page as your text, directly below it.**
Anything you explain about a mansion, the reader is about to read again in
better words. Do not describe the mansions. Write only what happens when a
person is both.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE SPINE — read this twice
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  The portrait owns the mansion. The combination owns the interaction.

Every mansion appears in 56 cells — 28 as the sun's, 28 as the moon's. The
failure this whole architecture exists to prevent is two of those 56 reaching
for the same fact and producing the same sentence. Two users compare shards,
find the identical line, and the product is a generic engine with better
typography.

**You may name each mansion by its epithet and its keyword, and nothing
else.** No star names. No tradition names. No deities. No election lore. No
guardian animals. No Sanskrit, Arabic or Chinese terms. Those are all in the
portrait, on screen, already. The packet's `banned` array lists them; a gate
checks it.

What the combination is allowed to say is the only thing the portrait cannot:
**what it is like to be both of these at once.**

`sun` and `moon` are about the pairing, not the mansion. The test: could this
paragraph appear unchanged in a cell with a different partner mansion? If yes
it belongs in the portrait, and it is already there.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE SIX SLOTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Fixed shape, every cell. Design's markup renders these in this order.

  lead      25–40w   the two mansions as one sentence. Sets the paradox
                     and stops.
  sun       55–75w   what the sun's mansion does IN THIS PAIRING
  moon      50–70w   what the moon's mansion does AGAINST THAT SUN
  pull      20–35w   the fusion. Rendered as a pull-quote.
                     THE LINE SOMEONE SCREENSHOTS.
  tension   60–80w   what the pairing makes hard, or what it gets called
  cost      50–70w   what it is unusually good at, and what that costs

~290 words per cell.

Word counts are hard gates. Note: the harness counts a spaced em dash as a
word, so a lead with two dashes reads ~2w over a hand count.

**`cost` says what it costs. It never says what to do about it.** Name the
tension, don't prescribe the virtue.

**`cost` may not announce itself.** The slot's job is to describe the trade,
never to introduce it. If a cell can be rewritten to start at the second
clause, it should be. This is the single most-repeated instruction in this
document because it is the tell that keeps coming back.

**`lead` may not restate the hero.** The page already prints
"sun in The Storm · moon in The Throne" directly above your lead. A lead
beginning "sun in…" is the most visible template tell available. Six of eight
cells in the pilot opened with the template.

**LEAD VARIETY.** An earlier tranche closed the header-restating problem and
then settled straight into a new pattern: **twelve leads in a row shaped
"[Moon epithet] does X, and [Sun epithet] does Y, so Z."** Across 784 cells
that is just as visible. Vary it. Some leads should open on the consequence
and never name either epithet at all. Some should open mid-situation. Naming
both epithets in the lead is permitted, not required — and if you write four
cells, they must not all open the same way.

**`tension` names the thing. It never announces that it is about to.**

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
VOICE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Second person. Lowercase throughout except proper nouns and mansion
epithets. No first person — "we" appears nowhere. The reader is the subject
of every sentence.

**Recognition, not flattery.** Accuracy that includes the cost is what gets
screenshotted. A compliment is not. Say the hard thing plainly and do not
soften it with advice.

**Say the astrology word.** Use the real term, define it once in four words
on first contact, then use it forever. Never paraphrase a technical term
into a description of itself.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
BANNED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

· announcing a reveal — "here's the part nobody knows"
· labelling the move — "the cost is…", "what it costs is…", and every
  paraphrase of them. A ban produces a paraphrase; the instruction that
  replaces it is the positive one above: describe the trade, never
  introduce it.
· not-X-but-Y — "this isn't restlessness, it's…"
· meta-commentary about the reading
· throat-clearing openers
· any sentence whose subject is the app
· **naming that the traditions disagree about a mansion.** That is in the
  portrait.

**THESE EXACT FRAMES ARE BANNED OUTRIGHT.** They are not a style preference;
each one is a construction an earlier row drove into the ground, and the
anchored regexes do not catch most of them. Check your draft against this
list by hand before you hand back.

    "what it costs is"        "what that costs is"
    "at full size and"        "is the word and"
    "the word and it"         "there is no way"
    "is no way to"            "and it is the"
    "so there is no"          "and it does not"
    "you have never once"     "and none of them"
    "and not one of them"     "not one of them knows"
    "and every one of them"   "and you are the only"
    "and there is nothing to" "is real and it is"
    "and you have never been"

**Do not evade one by inserting a word.** An earlier pass turned *"from
people"* into *"from the people"*, which fools the tool and not the reader.
If a frame is banned, the sentence is rebuilt, not respaced.

This list is not exhaustive and it is not the point. **The point is the
mechanism:** a ban produces a paraphrase unless you know what the slot does
instead. That is why `cost` has a positive instruction above and this list
does not stand alone.

**FABRICATED ATTRIBUTION — the most expensive error available.**
No "the old books". No "the older readings". No "the tradition is". No
"classical sources". No "every tradition". No "the traditions say / gave /
call / treat".

**Nobody wrote down the 784 mansion combinations.** The pairing genuinely has
no inherited literature — that is exactly why this layer is generated. Asked
to sound authoritative about a pairing with no tradition behind it, the
instinct is to invent the tradition. Seven of the first eight pilot cells did
exactly this. The product's whole position is recovery, not invention, and a
fabricated attribution is precisely what a practitioner checks.

**The combination attributes nothing.** The portrait does all attribution.
The combination speaks only about the reader, and carries its authority in
the observation itself.

**VOCABULARY IS A WHITELIST, NOT A BLACKLIST.**
Free vocabulary: the planets · the twelve signs · sun / moon / rising · the
houses · the five aspects · retrograde · new and full moon · orbs in degrees.
**Everything else is off-limits.** "Significator" and "aversion" are both
real terms and both outside it — and aversion describes signs, not mansions,
so applying it here is a category error a reader can catch.

**NO LEAKAGE.** No date, no time, no place, no sign name, no degree token.
You received two mansion numbers and two portraits. You have no birth data
and must not write as though you do.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE DIAGONAL — same mansion, sun and moon
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

These 28 break the template.

· Name: the epithet alone, doubled in the address. "The Storm of the Storm"
  is not a name.
· `lead` cannot state a paradox, because there is no second term. It states
  an ABSENCE OF COUNTERWEIGHT.
· `sun` and `moon` must not restate each other. One takes the mansion
  outward, one takes it inward: the sun's is how the room receives it, the
  moon's is what it is like from inside.
· `pull` is the concentration itself.
· The gate requires one of these markers somewhere in the cell: "no
  counterweight", "nothing opposite", "doubled", "both at once", "twice
  over", "undiluted", "no second".

These are the most likely to read as filler and the most likely to be a
user's favourite thing about their shard.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
KILL CRITERIA — rewrite, don't patch
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A cell is rewritten if it:
· describes a mansion instead of the pairing
· shares an 8-gram with any sibling cell
· offers advice
· reads as flattery
· could be about a different pairing with two words changed
