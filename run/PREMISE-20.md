## What a sun in The Flock *is*

A sun in **The Flock** belongs to a group and is never entirely inside it, and
both halves of that are permanent. You are a member. Nobody disputes it, you
are on the list, you turn up. Being simply *in* the room the way the others are
in it has never once happened — you are always also standing slightly off it,
seeing the shape of the thing while you are inside the thing.

*(Note the construction avoided there: the `and you have never…` family is on
PROMPT.txt's banned list, and this paragraph wanted it badly. Watch for the
same pull in your own draft.)*

The image is exact and it is worth holding onto: half the birds are heading to
the water and half are heading away from it, and **no single bird is doing
both** — but from the ground it reads as one animal with two intentions. That
is what you can see and the others cannot. From inside a group, the group looks
like a group. From your position it visibly comes apart into the people going
one way and the people going the other, and you knew which was which years
before anybody said so.

**The talent is sorting** — an unusual accuracy about what in a thing matters
and what will blow away. **The difficulty is that you apply it to harvests that
were never yours.** You can tell which half of a marriage is load-bearing,
which two people in a partnership are actually working, which of a family's
stories is the one holding the rest up. Nobody asked. And a sorting, once said
out loud, does not go back in — the partnership comes apart along the line you
named, and everyone remembers who named it.

## The distinctions this row must hold

### 20 The Flock vs 22 The Listener — *both stand at an angle to a group*

**The distinction is position versus intake.**

- **The Flock** — this is about **where you stand.** You are of the group and
  at its edge at the same time, and what that position gives you is the ability
  to *sort*: to see the seams a group cannot see in itself. You pay for that by
  sorting what was never yours to sort, irreversibly.
- **The Listener** — this is not about membership at all. It is about **what
  reaches you**: hearing what a room is actually saying under what it is
  saying. The organ works in the dark and cannot be shut. The price is
  unwitnessed labour: you carry the season's work and nobody thanks the animal
  that carried it.

Row 22 is being written alongside this one. **Never write your subject as
hearing what is underneath.** A 20 cell whose talent is picking up the real
meaning under the stated one has walked into 22's row. Your subject *sees a
shape from a position*. It does not *receive a signal*.

### 20 The Flock vs 15 The Veil and 21 The Empty District

Both are written, and both are adjacent ground. **15 is unplaced** — nobody can
locate you at all. **21 is capacity by vacancy** — an absence others fill
without asking. **You are neither.** You are firmly and publicly placed, inside
the group, on the roll — and simultaneously off it by a few degrees. If your
cell would work with the subject not being a member, it is a 15 cell.

## ⚠ Banned tokens that collide with this row's vocabulary

Read your packet's `banned` array before you draft. **Banned in all 28 cells of
this row:**

**`four`** · **`groups`** · **`eight`** · `waters` · `divine` · `elephant` ·
`leopard` · `tusk` · plus the transliterations.

**`four` is the one that will catch you.** It is an ordinary numeral and this
row is about counting people in a group — *four years, four of them, the four
who stayed*. All of those fail the gate. Use a different number or a different
construction. `fourth` and `fourteen` are separate tokens and pass; bare `four`
does not.

`flock` and the keyword are permitted — they are the epithet and the keyword.

The leak gate catches sign names and month names, including the bare modal
**`may`**.
