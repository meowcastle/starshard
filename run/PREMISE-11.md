## What a sun in The Mane *is*

A sun in **The Mane** is comfortable being looked at, and **the comfort is the
mansion rather than the personality.** This is not confidence you developed. It
is a property you have, the way a room has a width. You do not perform, you do
not work at it, and you could not turn it off if you decided to.

**A mane does exactly one job: it makes the animal read as larger and better
than the animal.** That is the mechanism and it is neutral. You register as
more than you are — in a doorway, on a list, in the first eight seconds — and
the reading is not something you constructed and not something anyone can be
talked out of. It arrives before you do.

**The difficulty is never scarcity. It is the suspicion that the portion
arrived rather than being won.** What you have was assigned, not earned; you
know it, and so does everyone in the room, and there is no test available that
would settle the question either way. People who worked for less than you have
are polite about it, and their politeness is the thing you can hear.

And there is a second cost in the same fact. **What is spread open cannot also
be held in reserve.** A thing standing wide open is furniture nobody thinks to
ask permission to use. Nothing about you is behind anything. You have no
withheld version to produce when it would help, because the spread is the whole
arrangement, and it is on all the time.
