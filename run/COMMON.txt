1. WHAT YOU DO
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

You write four cells of the 784-cell combination corpus, all sharing one sun
mansion. You are one of seven agents writing the same sun-row concurrently.
Your brief names your four addresses, your packet paths, and the angles the
earlier batches in this row have already taken.

Read, in order:
  1. this file
  2. run/PROMPT.txt — the constitution
  3. your four fact packets
  4. THE PREMISE in your brief — one paragraph naming what a sun in this
     mansion *is*. It is the strongest predictor of whether a row comes back
     clean. Do not skim it.
  5. the cells your brief names as already-written siblings

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. OUTPUT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A JSON array of four objects, written to the path your brief gives:

[
  { "address": "07·01",
    "lead": "...", "sun": "...", "moon": "...",
    "pull": "...", "tension": "...", "cost": "..." },
  ...
]

· `address` is zero-padded, middle dot U+00B7, sun first: "07·01".
· Six string fields, exactly. No extra keys, no nesting, no markdown.
· Plain text inside the strings. No asterisks, no quotes-as-emphasis.

3. VERIFY BEFORE YOU HAND BACK — this is not optional
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Then merge-check privately against the corpus: concatenate your four cells

Fix until clean. Hand back only clean output.

4. THE GATE IS A FLOOR, NOT A VERDICT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The regexes are anchored and miss banned frames that appear mid-sentence.
Agents on earlier rows caught their own tells that the harness passed —
label-first `tension` openers, repeated `cost` openers, a not-X-but-Y that
slipped the pattern. One reported plainly: "the harness misses several
literal banned frames, so I audited separately." That was the correct move.

**The rule that generalises, and the one to actually use:**

    IF THREE OF YOUR SLOTS OF ONE KIND OPEN ALIKE, TWO ARE WRONG.

Read your four `cost` slots as a column. Then your four `tension` slots.
Then your four `lead` slots. Not as four cells — as four firsts-of-a-kind.
Repetition is invisible cell-by-cell and obvious in a column.

**The tell moves.** Each gate finds the current one and the next row invents
a new one. Row 06's writers announced the cost; row 10's stopped announcing
the cost and started announcing the count ("and not one of them…", "not one
of them knows…", "and every one of them…"). Whatever your four cells have
started doing in unison, that is this row's tell. Find it yourself.

The frame gate: no 5-gram may appear in more than 3% of a checked set, floor
of 2. Tuned empirically — 4-grams at 3% flag ordinary English, 6-grams flag
nothing. Five words recurring is a construction; four is often just English.

**And the gate cannot see the explicit banned-frame list at all.** PROMPT.txt
carries nineteen literal frames earlier rows drove into the ground. Grep your
own output for every one of them before you hand back — rows 07 and 12 were
written from a brief that had lost the list, and the two of them carried
eighteen hits between 56 cells where the previous 420 cells carried fourteen.
That is what happens when the list is not in front of the writer.

**And the reference material is not exempt.** `PREMISE-14.md` as first written
contained a banned frame, and a batch agent caught it — reading the premise
twice, as instructed, plants the frame. If you find one in a file you were
handed, say so in your report and do not carry it into a cell.

Do not modify `research/combos.json` or another agent's file. Merge-check into
a private temp file.

5. ANTI-REPETITION, THE PART NO TOOL CAN DO
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**Semantic duplicates are invisible to every gate.** Mansions 1 and 24 both
carried the keyword "the opening" for eleven rows before anyone noticed —
found by printing the packet header, not by a tool. Two cells can share zero
n-grams and still be the same cell.

Your brief lists the angles already taken in this row, one line each. Batch A
has none; batch G has twenty-four. **Read them and go somewhere else.** An
angle is a life-situation, not a phrase: "the person who is asked to hold the
group's memory" is an angle. "Uses the word 'threshold'" is not.

Your brief may also name a roles-swapped twin that already exists — if you
are writing 07·12 and 12·07 is on disk, read it and make yours a different
life. Same two mansions, opposite arrangement, and the arrangement is the
whole content.

6. THE TWO STRUCTURAL SPECIAL CASES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**The diagonal** — SUN·SUN, sun and moon in the same mansion. PROMPT.txt has
the doubled template. It is a different shape, not a cell with a repeated
term, and the gate requires an explicit no-counterweight marker.

**The opposite** — SUN·(SUN+14 mod 28). The moon stands in the sun's
farlight. This is a real and nameable arrangement and the one structural fact
the pairing has that neither portrait contains. Write it as a real
arrangement; do not name a technical term for it and do not attribute a
tradition to it.
