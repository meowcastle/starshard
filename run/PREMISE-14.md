## What a sun in The Jewel *is*

A sun in **The Jewel** is visible and undefended, **and those are the same
condition, not two facts about the same person.** The making here produces a
thing that can be seen: a piece of work, a reputation, a house, a way of doing
something that people can point at and name you for. It is good, and it is
noticed, and the noticing is not incidental — craft of this kind attracts
attention as a property of being good.

What was withheld is the instinct to arm against that attention. **The problem
is never the making.** Producing the thing has always come easily.
The trouble starts the moment it exists and is admired: it is out where
everyone can reach it, and the reflex that would put a hand over it — the
edit, the qualification, the withholding, the terms — simply is not installed.
Other people assume a thing that good is protected somehow, because in their
experience things that good always are. Nobody checks. It isn't.

So what happens to your work after it is finished is decided by whoever is
standing nearest, and you find out at the same time as everyone else.

## The distinctions this row must hold

### 14 The Jewel vs 13 The Hand — *both are about making*

**The distinction is whether the made thing carries your name.**

- **The Jewel** — the making is **visible and attributed.** It is yours by
  name, it is admired, and it is unguarded. The problem sits **downstream**:
  what happens to a finished thing everyone can see and nobody is defending.
- **The Hand** — the making is **causal and untraceable.** What is made is a
  motion, it goes out under someone else's name, no count is kept. The problem
  sits **upstream**, in the palm.

Row 13 is being written alongside this one. **Never write your subject's work
as unrecorded.** A 14 cell in which the output is untraceable, uncounted, or
running under another person's name has walked into 13's row. In your cells
everyone knows exactly what you made and exactly who made it — that is the
precondition for the cost, not a softening of it.

### 14 The Jewel vs 11 The Mane — *both display*

Row 11 is not written yet, so you set the first reading. The line to hold:
**11 is display as a property of size** — the thing is large and cannot be
made smaller, so it registers whether or not you meant it to. **14 is display
as a consequence of quality** — the thing is good, and good work draws
attention the way a bright star does, and the defencelessness is what makes it
a cost rather than a benefit. If your cell would work with the work being
merely *big* rather than *good*, it is an 11 cell.
