## Batch A — 20·01 … 20·04

- **20·01** *work / a trade.* Hired for the opening weeks of a place; the
  sorting is complete by the ninth day — a verdict with a position behind it
  and no tenure, delivered by someone who will be gone before the consequences
  land.
- **20·02** *the family you came from.* The ranking of your own household was
  finished at seventeen and held, unreleased, for thirty years. It has aged
  into stored goods and describes an arrangement that no longer exists.
- **20·03** *money.* A pooled sum spoken of as one figure that is actually six
  wallets. The one-object version is what lets people who love each other
  unevenly avoid a reckoning; a thin year resolves it out loud whether you
  speak or not.
- **20·04** *the body.* One place behind the heaviest appetite in every room
  since twenty-two, which reads as moderation. You can name which two of that
  crew it is taking, you are on the list you made, and the body kept the
  arithmetic at somebody else's pace.

## Batch B — 20·05 … 20·08

- **20·05** *friendship.* The new friend acquired fast and finished fast; every
  old friendship known entirely from its first six weeks, held together by
  attendance rather than by you.
- **20·06** *THE OPPOSITE / farlight.* A group sorted once, privately, in the
  third year — the verdict never spoken and never revisable.
- **20·07** *resumption.* A trade rejoined at forty-one after eleven years out.
  The returner is the last person who can measure the place against what it
  was, and has no standing to say so.
- **20·08** *grief.* A family nine years on still living off a version of the
  dead man you could take apart in a sitting, and have not.

## Batch C — 20·09 … 20·12

- **20·09** *strangers.* The first evening at a table of long-acquainted
  strangers you have just joined: one exact read of the whole group taken at
  the door and never re-opened — and the read they took of you at the same
  distance, equally permanent and wrong.
- **20·10** *inherited position.* The chair given to the one member who was
  never entirely in the room, where a private sorting exits the seat as
  institutional policy and the place starts curating what you are shown.
- **20·11** *display as size.* Being known for the half-step itself — a
  position you never chose, arriving at a width with no lower setting, so the
  apartness is the conspicuous fact about your membership.
- **20·12** *an absolute ending.* Leaving a thirty-year body in one season with
  every obligation to it paid — and the total exit read as the verdict you
  never spoke.

## Batch D — 20·13 … 20·16

- **20·13** *craft.* A shared workshop you are a paid-up member of. The sorting
  never gets spoken — it gets built into the racks and jigs, so the room is
  quietly fitted to the half of it that works and the other half drifts out
  with no grievance to bring.
- **20·14** *a piece of work everyone can see.* One plain, credited,
  undefended account of the trade you are on the register of, which shows the
  seam you can see from the edge — and gets picked up as a weapon by both
  sides while carrying nothing about how to read it.
- **20·15** *not being placed.* Membership is documented and unquestioned, and
  no other sentence about you exists. No faction can count you, and the roll is
  exactly what makes the unplaceability read as withholding.
- **20·16** *a fight.* You named the seam a decade before it opened, and now
  hold both cases whole and load-bearing — two grips, not neutrality — and the
  faction that moved fastest took it while you were still being accurate.

## Live banned-token traps

**Banned in all 28 cells of this row:** **`four`** · **`groups`** ·
**`eight`** · `waters` · `divine` · `elephant` · `leopard` · `tusk`.

**`four` is the one that catches people** — batch A hit it twice in drafting
(*"the four who stayed"*, *"four years"*). It is an ordinary numeral and this
row is about counting people. `fourth`, `fourteen`, `forty-one` pass; bare
`four` does not. Batch B wrote every count as *twelve, eleven, nine, a dozen,
three* and never went near it.

Per-moon found so far: `head` (01) · `crow` (04) · `knowledge`, `hidden`,
`coiled` (09) · `including`, `little`, `king`, `royal` (10) · `fortune`,
`front`, `legs` (11) · **`back`** (12 — which also rules out the sun keyword in
that cell) · `open` (13) · `artisan` (14) · `wind`, `young`, `shoot` (15) ·
the curved-span word, `claw` singular, `fire`, `force` (16 — plural *Claws* is
the epithet and passes).

Sign names and month names leak, including the bare modal **`may`**. Batch A
also flagged that no cell in the corpus dates itself to a decade — do not write
*"the nineties"*.

## The row-22 boundary

Row 22 (The Listener) is being written in parallel. **The Flock is about where
you stand** — of a group and at its edge at once, and the position lets you
*sort*. **The Listener is about what reaches you** — an intake that cannot be
shut. Grep your output for *hear / heard / listen / underneath / beneath /
signal*. Every talent in this row is **a shape seen from a position**, never an
intake.
