## What a sun in The Turning *is*

A sun in **The Turning** changes completely or not at all, and stays bound to
whoever it changed in front of. The season here does not soften — it breaks.
When you are done with a thing you are done to the skin: the job, the city,
the way you ate, the person you had been being. There is no gradient, and the
people around you find the completeness alarming precisely because they were
watching for one. They expected you to taper. You did not taper. One morning
the weather was simply different and everything downstream of it had already
rearranged.

**What does not break is the tie.** This is the second half, and the
half that makes the mansion hard. You finish things absolutely and you keep
answering the phone. The contract survives the molt — what is finished is
finished completely, and what is owed is still owed, and both are true in the
same week without either one softening the other. From outside those two
halves read as contradiction, which is why you are very good at endings and
very bad at explaining them. From inside they were never two things.

## The distinctions this row must hold

### 12 The Turning vs 07 The Return — *both are about a thing leaving*

**The distinction is whether it comes back, and what survives if it doesn't.**

- **The Turning** — the departure is total and one-way. What persists is not
  the thing but **the obligation attached to it**.
- **The Return** — the departure is provisional. What left is retrievable,
  and what persists is **the thing itself**.

Row 07 is being written alongside this one. **Never write a break as
something recoverable.** A 12 cell that leaves the door on the latch, or
treats a finished thing as resumable, has walked into 07's row and taken its
content. Your subject sheds. What it keeps, it keeps deliberately and
separately — and it is a person or a promise, never the shed thing.

### 12 The Turning vs 04 The Follower — *both about what a pursuit leaves behind*

**The Follower keeps everything for a very long time** — the standard, the
memory, the position behind, accumulated by staying. **The Turning keeps
almost nothing and cannot be talked out of the one thing it keeps.** A 12
cell is never about accumulation, never about holding a position over years,
never about a grudge carried. It is about a clean total break with a single
unbroken thread running out of it.

## The verb-family that belongs to this row

break · shed · molt · finish · close out · done to the skin · one-way ·
no gradient · still owed · the tie holds · answer the phone anyway

**And the family that does not:** come back, resume, retrieve, draw up,
again, still available, on the latch. Those are 07's.
