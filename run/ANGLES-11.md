## Batch A — 11·01 … 11·04

## Batch B — 11·05 … 11·08

## Batch C — 11·09 … 11·12

- **11·09** — a man at a party spent about ninety seconds adjusting his estimate of you downward, and you watched the whole adjustment at a resolution he does not know exists. you still have his face from that evening.
- **11·10** — they have been calling you the obvious next one for six years. nothing has been decided anywhere, the seat is not vacant, and the word obvious is carrying the entire weight of an appointment nobody has had to make.
- **11·11** — nothing is being kept for later. no reserved version, no private amount, nothing that is not already out where a room can reach it, doubled — the size is the whole of the evidence and there is no second exhibit.
- **11·12** — you still drive his mother to her appointments on thursdays. the thing that made her yours finished in you in a single season four years ago, and neither fact has ever been made to explain the other.

## Batch D — 11·13 … 11·16

- **11·14** — eleven months went into it, and the first person to see it finished said it looked like it came easily, and meant that warmly, and everyone since has said a version of the same thing.
- **11·15** — the person introducing you gets four words in, stops, and hands it over to you. what you give back is not an answer either, and the room takes that for discretion and moves on to the drinks.
- **11·16** — his side of it is the thing you cannot put down, held at the same weight as your own, two years into a fight the room settled long ago as a big party leaning on a small one.
