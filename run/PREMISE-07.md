## What a sun in The Return *is*

A sun in **The Return** is a person built around the settled expectation that
things come back. Not hope that they will — a load-bearing assumption that
what left is retrievable. The friendship resumable after four silent years.
The abandoned draft picked up mid-sentence. The version of yourself you were
at nineteen, still down there, still available if you lower something and
draw it up. The gesture is exactly that: reaching down and bringing something
back up, and you are unusually good at it. People come to you when a thing is
thought lost, because you are the one who does not accept that a thing is
lost.

The cost sits on the other side of the same assumption. **If nothing is
finally gone, nothing is finally ended.** Your departures are provisional in
a way the other person never agreed to. You do not close doors, you leave
them on the latch, and you have been surprised more than once to learn that
someone treated a latched door as a shut one and went and built a life
accordingly. Leaving is not the optional half of returning — it is the half
you keep trying to make optional.

## The distinctions this row must hold

### 07 The Return vs 12 The Turning — *both are about a thing leaving*

**The distinction is whether it comes back, and what survives if it doesn't.**

- **The Return** — the departure is the provisional half. What left is
  retrievable, and because it is retrievable, goodbyes stop feeling final.
  What persists is **the thing itself**.
- **The Turning** — the departure is total and one-way. The weather breaks;
  a snake does not shed a percentage. What persists is not the thing but
  **the obligation attached to it** — the break is complete and the debt is
  still owed.

Row 12 is being written alongside this one. **Never write recovery as a
molt.** A 07 cell that describes a change as complete, one-way, and shed to
the skin has walked into 12's row and taken its content. Your subject
recovers, resumes, retrieves, re-enters. It does not finish.

### 07 The Return vs 01 The Gate — *both are about arrival*

**The Gate arrives first, once, and does not supply the second half.** The
Return arrives *again*, and the whole content is that there was a first time.
A 07 cell is never about being early, being unannounced, or opening
something. It is about resuming something that has a history you were part
of. If your cell would still work with no prior visit, it is a 01 cell.

## The verb-family that belongs to this row

come back · resume · retrieve · draw up · pick up mid-sentence · the second
time · again · still there · reachable · unfinished on purpose · on the latch

**And the family that does not:** shed, molt, break, finish, close out,
complete, done to the skin, owe. Those are 12's.
