# Angles already taken in row 07 — The Return

Sixteen cells written by batches A–D. **Read them, do not repeat them.**
An angle is a life-situation, not a phrase. Go somewhere these did not go.

Full text of all sixteen is in `/tmp/ss/run/cells/07-a.json` … `07-d.json`.

## Batch A — 07·01 … 07·04

- **07·01** *work / a trade.* A career made entirely of second attempts: sent
  to the shelved product, the client who walked, the case somebody else
  signed off. Never handed a thing at its beginning. Commits at first-response
  pace before reading why it failed the first time.
- **07·02** *the family you came from.* The one who kept going back. Every
  candidate day is a day you are about to be welcomed back on, so the
  carried thing never comes out. The sister who stopped going is the one the
  family discusses; you are why the family still assembles.
- **07·03** *money, and what is out.* Eleven amounts in eleven lives, none
  written off, because writing off means believing it is gone. Nobody inside
  holds the total. Nobody has ever received a gift from you — only an open
  position they were never told about.
- **07·04** *the body, appetite, physical habit.* You resume at the load you
  left it at. A fixed width back to the body you had at twenty-three, so the
  current body is never read on its own terms. The same joint gives out every
  re-entry.

## Batch B — 07·05 … 07·08

- **07·05** *friendship.* The retrieval faculty runs on appetite for lapsed
  people, so the reunion is the flattest part. The group's reunion-organiser,
  done by the second hour. The never-left friend two streets away gets
  nothing, because nothing about her needs tracking.
- **07·06** *a public room, being seen losing control.* Known as the one who
  repairs wreckage; seen once at full volume by forty people and ran his own
  retrieval on it, competently. Four years on the incident is still alive in
  the group's mouth because he is the only one who will not concede it is
  over.
- **07·07** *THE DIAGONAL.* Nobody has ever had a last-anything with this
  person in either direction. No leaving party, no farewell, no marked event.
  Absence registers as *out*, so nothing was mourned at the time. Every ending
  in the life was performed by the other person.
- **07·08** *grief, the dead, the still-present absent.* The dead are not
  memorialised, they are supplied — ordinary traffic kept running, unmetered.
  The bereaved seek this person out; the living compete against claimants who
  never have a bad week and cannot be named without sounding monstrous.

## Batch C — 07·09 … 07·12

- **07·09** *returning to near-strangers.* The retrieved are one-encounter
  acquaintances — twenty minutes on a platform, one shift, a house entered by
  mistake. The reunion is a resumption on your side only, and reads to them
  as a claim.
- **07·10** *institutional re-entry at rank.* Left twice, readmitted twice at
  grade. The post was set down rather than surrendered, and the bearing makes
  re-entry read as a recall arranged above. Nobody has said out loud that the
  seat was kept.
- **07·11** *the public comeback.* Every resumption staged where the room can
  watch, so second attempts are received before they are heard. No ending you
  announce is believed. Nothing taken up again has come home with a verdict.
- **07·12** *the resumption carried out by someone who already finished.* The
  retrieval is real and the wanting died in one season, so the debt is what
  walks in the door, and the friendship is described by her as the one that
  never went anywhere.

## Batch D — 07·13 … 07·16

- **07·13** *craft / repair.* A workshop of things never finished because
  "finished" has no meaning. You resume someone else's abandoned build in
  their hand rather than restarting it, so people bring you dead work late.
  Thirty years is one continuous revision with no body of work to show.
- **07·14** *possessions.* Lending costs nothing because retrieval is assumed.
  Half of what you own is in other people's houses with no list and no second
  conversation. Theft cannot happen to you, so nobody has ever been held to
  anything.
- **07·15** *not being placed.* The return is your only address. Six
  households each believe they are the fixed point, none can locate you
  between visits, each has invented the missing part of your life. Two of them
  compared notes at a wedding.
- **07·16** *conflict, a grip.* The nineteen-year fight that has never been
  over for longer than a season. You hold the sentence that would win it and
  you carry his side better than his own people do, so the pressure goes on and
  comes off at once.
