## What a sun in The Chamber *is*

A sun in **The Chamber** builds the enclosure other people will live inside,
and **the mansion is specific that this is structural work rather than
hospitality.** You are not making anyone comfortable. You are making the thing
that has an inside — the boundary that turns a covered space into a room, with
somewhere that is in it and somewhere that is not. Corners, not ornaments.

**It holds.** That is the first fact about it and the one everything else
follows from. Nothing you have built has come down. What you put up stays up,
carries its load, and goes on doing that for decades without adjustment.

**And precisely because it holds, nobody in a room thinks about the wall.** A
thing that has never once failed produces no occasion on which anyone has to
consider it. The people inside are not ungrateful — gratitude never arises,
because a wall is not an event. It is the condition under which their evening
is happening, and conditions are not noticed.

**Nothing here supplies the impulse to mention it either.** That is the part
that makes this a cost rather than a complaint. You are not waiting to be
thanked and then not thanked. The sentence that would point at what you are
holding up has never formed in you. The coil at the bottom of the thing, in the
dark, holding — and nobody has ever asked it whether it minds, including you.

## The distinctions this row must hold

### 26 The Chamber vs 25 The Hideaway — *both are shelter, and the road runs pole then wall*

**The distinction is permanence-and-being-unremarked versus contingency-and-concealment.**

- **The Chamber** — the enclosure **does not hide anything** and it **will not
  fall.** It is structure: a boundary, an inside and an outside, load carried
  and distributed. Being unnoticed is a consequence of reliability, not of
  cover.
- **The Hideaway** — the cover **hides**, and it **stands on one thing.** What
  is under it is not available for inspection, and the support is single, so it
  comes down in one piece without warning.

Row 25 is being written alongside this one. **Never write your subject as
concealing anything, and never make the structure precarious.** A 26 cell in
which something is hidden, or in which the thing you built might give way, has
walked into 25's row. **Yours holds, in plain view, and is not looked at.**

### 26 The Chamber vs 27 The Guide — *both close the road*

Row 27 is not written yet, so **you set the line first.** **26 encloses; 27
goes ahead of someone.** Nothing in your row leads anybody anywhere. Nobody is
taken across, walked out, or delivered. The movement in a 26 cell is inward and
the structure stays put. If your cell has you out in front of a person on a
route, it is a 27 cell.
