## What a sun in The Claws *is*

A sun in **The Claws** goes two ways at once and **does not experience this as
indecision.** That distinction is the entire mansion, and every cell in this
row has to get it right. This is not a person who cannot choose, or
who hedges, or who is torn. It is a person for whom the two directions are both
real, both committed to, both actually being carried — the same field ploughed
and harvested at opposite ends of the year, and both of those are the work.

The structure holds *because* of the doubleness. Two pillars leaning against
each other stand by pushing in opposite directions; take one side away and what
is left is not a simpler structure, it is rubble. So when somebody tells you to
pick one, they are not offering you relief. They are describing a collapse.

**⚠ Do not use the word for that structure.** It is in this row's packet
`banned` array — it comes off the portrait — and so are `fire` and `force`.
Write *two pillars*, *carrying in two directions*, *the weight goes both ways*.
Reading this premise twice, as instructed, is exactly how the banned word gets
planted; that is why it is not written here.

**What was withheld is the single-mindedness that makes things fast.** You will
get there, and you will get there carrying both, and you will get there after
the person who took one direction and ran. That is the cost and it is a real
one: not confusion, not weakness of will — **speed.** And because from outside
the two-way carry looks exactly like hesitation, you will be given advice for a
problem you do not have, by people who mean well, for your whole life.

## The distinctions this row must hold

### 16 The Claws vs 19 The Root — *both refuse the simple version*

**The distinction is direction and stopping.**

- **The Claws** — goes **two ways at once, laterally, and both are real.** The
  doubleness is load-bearing. The cost is **speed**: nothing is fast, because
  everything is carrying in two directions. The failure mode is never arriving
  at one.
- **The Root** — goes **one way, straight down, all the way.** No fork, and no
  stopping point. The cost is **destruction**: you cannot examine a root
  without pulling the plant up, and nothing supplies the signal that says
  *understood well enough already.* The failure mode is arriving at the bottom
  and finding the thing you were examining is dead.

Row 19 is being written alongside this one. **Never write your subject as
digging.** A 16 cell in which the talent is finding the cause underneath the
plausible ones, or taking a thing apart to see it, has walked into 19's row.
Your subject does not go *under*. It goes **across, twice, at the same time.**

### 16 The Claws vs 06 The Storm — *both live with a thing that cannot be undone*

Row 06 is written. Its subject is irreversibility — the push that cannot be
recalled. Yours is not about permanence at all. If your cell would still work
with only one of the two directions in it, it is not a 16 cell.
