## Batch A — 26·01 … 26·04

- **26·01** *work / a trade.* A framer whose own work has never generated a
  callout, whose reputation is built entirely out of turning out to other men's
  failures — **the trade stores its memory in incidents and he produces none.**
- **26·02** *the family you came from.* A nine-year process that closed
  correctly, inside a family arrangement that never closes. The Bearer's one
  faculty is choosing the hour a thing is handed over, and the position itself
  has no hour.
- **26·03** *money.* One twenty-year arrangement, six unreconciled money
  policies inside one man. The structure absorbs all six so nothing ever forced
  them into a rule, and what gets inherited is a machine with no rationale.
- **26·04** *the body.* Twenty-nine years without a day lost, a chase against a
  weight set at twenty-four that has not closed an inch, conducted at the
  family table in full view and never once remarked on.

## Batch B — 26·05 … 26·08

- **26·05** *friendship.* A six-person group of twenty years, every member
  fetched by you from outside, one at a time. The searching face works outward
  only, so the closeness that grew is between people you introduced.
- **26·06** *a public room.* A hall of a hundred hears the place is closing and
  hears you get to your feet and name what you will put in. **Two permanences
  held apart:** an act with no undo, and a structure that has never looked like
  failing — and the structure never produces the occasion on which the pledge
  could be revisited.
- **26·07** *resumption.* Nine years abroad, then readmitted to the Saturday
  club you started at twenty-six, as a parent with a car. **The ease of
  resuming is the same fact as the anonymity**, since a structure is not
  attributed from the inside.
- **26·08** *grief.* Eight years after her death you are what keeps the family
  house — payment, roof, boiler — the only place she is not past tense, for a crowd of drawers with no edge on it, none of whom applied.

## Batch C — 26·09 … 26·12

- **26·09** *strangers.* Strangers get a complete permanent reading off one
  evening and leave with no impression of you at all, **because a thing that
  works from day one gives an encounter nothing to be about.**
- **26·10** *inherited position.* Rank as a filter: everything is tidied in the
  corridor before it reaches your desk, so the institution runs on arrangements
  you wrote and nothing ever arrives in the state it was actually in. The
  question of whether the position is owed cannot be put, because a question
  from that chair is heard as an instruction.
- **26·11** *display as size.* Noticed at a size you did not set, for nothing
  you made. The looking lands on the person and stops; the work has no edge to
  catch it. No quiet version of a sentence exists, so a mild reservation lands
  as a scene.
- **26·12** *THE OPPOSITE / farlight.* A place you set up eleven years ago,
  ended completely in one season, with every obligation met to the day since —
  and the unbroken record of care reads to everyone inside it as devotion.

## Batch D — 26·13 … 26·16

- **26·13** *craft.* Forty years of structural joinery on one village road —
  the tread, the frame, the benches. The making keeps no ledger, and **the only
  door left unrepaired is your own.**
- **26·14** *a piece of work everyone can see.* A public building with your
  name on a plaque, copied by four other counties off drawings you sent, and
  never once thought about by the people who use it weekly.
- **26·15** *not being placed.* Twenty years at a trust whose whole character
  is the terms you drafted; four job titles, none fitting, and no one who can
  say what you do.
- **26·16** *a fight.* Three years of a partners' dispute in a firm you built,
  both cases carried at full weight from opposite sides, while eleven employees
  think it is an ordinary year.

## Live banned-token traps — the worst list in the corpus

**Banned in all 28 cells:** **`back`** · **`deep`** · **`great`** ·
**`foundation`** · **`square`** · `serpent` · `legs` · star names and
transliterations.

- **`back` is the worst and it caught batch A twice in drafting** — *came back
  on the snag list*, *paid back*. Substitutes that worked: *returns*, *the
  callouts*, *behind them*.
- **`foundation` is banned and this mansion is about foundations.** Batch A
  wrote *what that rests on*, *what is holding the roof*, *carrying what it was
  put there to carry*.
- **`26·07`'s moon keyword is unusable** — The Return's keyword is *what comes
  back*, and `back` is banned. Carry it by epithet and by *resume / interval /
  come again*.
- The ban is exact-token, so `backing`, `deeper`, `greater`, `foundations`,
  `squarely` pass. **The failure is one letter away and looks fine.**

will not fall** — being unnoticed is a consequence of reliability, not cover.

precarious.**
