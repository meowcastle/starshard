## What a sun in The Hand *is*

A sun in **The Hand** starts things that then run without it. The talent is
making something exist that did not, and the mansion does not care whether the
thing is a chair or an argument — but the characteristic output is not an
object sitting on a table. It is **motion started in something else.** You say
the sentence and eight months later a person has changed careers on the back
of it. You push once and the thing goes on rolling, under someone else's name,
on someone else's legs, needing nothing further from you. This is not
generosity as a virtue. It is a hand that opens.

**The difficulty is the open palm**, and it is a difficulty of *accounting*,
not of will. There is no built-in instinct here for keeping count of what has
left. You do not know your own total. Nobody presents you with one either,
because the things you started stopped carrying your name at the second
handoff — the work is everywhere and the maker is nowhere in it. So the record
of your life is held entirely by other people, in fragments, none of whom can
see the others — you could assemble it and nobody else could, and you are the
party with no list.

## The distinctions this row must hold

### 13 The Hand vs 14 The Jewel — *both are about making*

**The distinction is whether the made thing carries your name.**

- **The Hand** — the making is **causal and untraceable.** What you make is a
  motion, it leaves your hand immediately, it runs under someone else's name,
  and no count is kept. The problem sits **upstream**, in the palm: things go
  out and are not recorded.
- **The Jewel** — the making is **visible and attributed.** The thing you made
  is bright, admired, and yours by name — and you have no instinct to guard it.
  The problem sits **downstream**: the artisan's problem is never the making,
  it is what happens to a finished thing that everyone can see and nobody is
  defending.

Row 14 is being written alongside this one. **Never write your output as
admired.** A 13 cell in which the work is looked at, praised, displayed, or
taken from you has walked into 14's row. Your subject's work is *unattributed*
— that is the whole cost. Nobody is looking at it, and that is not modesty, it
is the mechanism.

### 13 The Hand vs 14 again, on adjacency

13 and 14 are neighbouring stations, so `13·14` and `14·13` will be read
together and are the two cells where the distinction is the content. Elsewhere
in the row, hold the line by vocabulary.

## The verb-family that belongs to this row

start · set going · push off · brace against · hand it over · it left ·
running on their legs · under their name · no count · the palm opens ·

guarded, taken, the finished thing, credit, signature. Those are 14's.
