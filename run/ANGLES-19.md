## Batch A — 19·01 … 19·04

- **19·01** *a trade.* The fault-finder sent first to a stopped production
  line, who goes under the failed part to the decision that put it there —
  while the ticket says restore service by noon.
- **19·02** *the family you came from.* Ten years into the family papers. Every
  account tested has failed, and what is held is not a better story but the
  demonstrated falseness of the only one they had — which cannot be handed to
  anyone.
- **19·03** *money.* The five-name shared account of a place run in common. You
  counted it properly, unasked, and the thing everyone is proudest of turned
  out to have been carried for years by the line nobody defends.
- **19·04** *the body.* Twenty years of logged attacks, elimination run on
  yourself past what any clinic would accept. Every finding correct, every
  finding a subtraction, nothing ever returned to the list.

## Batch B — 19·05 … 19·08

- **19·05** *THE OPPOSITE / farlight.* A nine-year private inquiry that stopped
  being interesting in its third week. The descent runs on answers failing, not
  on appetite — and the appetite is at the far end of the road, where it can
  never call anything off.
- **19·06** *a public room.* A members' meeting, sixty people, one straight
  question answered honestly: the foundation described exactly to the people
  standing on it, once, with no smaller version in existence.
- **19·07** *resumption.* A friendship dismantled in your twenties and rung up
  twelve years later. What he experienced as an ending you had filed as an
  interval, and the resumption is the dig continuing one layer lower.
- **19·08** *grief.* Four years after the funeral. The dead put up no
  resistance, so the excavation goes further into him than was ever possible
  while he was alive — and the version the family could bear is what comes
  apart.

## Batch C — 19·09 … 19·12

- **19·09** *strangers.* The descent runs at full speed on someone you have
  known twenty minutes, because a stranger costs nothing to lose. People are
  taken to the bottom by someone whose surname they never learned, with no
  second meeting to correct it.
- **19·10** *inherited position.* Rank hands you the papers, and a level
  bearing makes dismantling the institution's founding account read as
  housekeeping until the ground is already up in the corridor. The private
  question of deserving a seat you did not build gets settled by there being
  nothing left to deserve.
- **19·11** *the thing you are known for.* The reputation is a size you did not
  set and cannot lower, so the excavation runs in public **including the wrong
  guesses**. The private version of the service does not exist, and the word
  for it from any seat is *showman*.
- **19·12** *an ending as the result of the finding.* Other people's findings
  get absorbed; yours are not survivable, and the thing closes inside a season
  with no slow bad year in which a case might have been made — so you have
  never seen what a marriage or a firm does with the truth about itself.

## Batch D — 19·13 … 19·16

- **19·13** *craft.* An instrument restorer who will not accept a repair that
  works in place of the reason the failure began. The object comes apart to the
  last joint, and the hands rebuild so much of it that what goes home under a
  dead maker's label is substantially yours, uncounted.
- **19·14** *a piece of work everyone can see.* The self-built public record
  that goes under an official version twenty years old — the finding stands in
  the open at full length, in one name, with no institution, no press office,
  nothing over it.
- **19·15** *not being placed.* The true account of where you came from, dug
  past the three family versions. Correct to the household, and it places you
  on no side, in no country, among none of the people who share the name.
- **19·16** *the 19/16 contrast.* Two real causes under one failure, each of
  which had to be taken to the bottom before either could be believed. Eleven
  years, both correct, nobody living in the house.

## The row-16 boundary

Row 16 (The Claws) was written immediately before this one and is in the
corpus. **The Claws goes two ways at once, laterally, both real and
load-bearing — the cost is speed. The Root goes one way, straight down, all the
way, with no stopping signal — the cost is destruction.** Grep your output for
*both / at once / two directions / the fork / holding both sides*. Batch C
caught itself writing "the wrong half of that is the interesting half" — no
doubleness was meant, but *half…half* is the row-16 silhouette. Every subject
here goes one way, and the direction is down.
