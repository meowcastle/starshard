## What a sun in The Crown *is*

A sun in **The Crown** enters things on purpose and then keeps them, and the
keeping is not sustained by feeling. This is the god of **the agreement between
equals that nobody was forced into** — and of the agreement *outliving the
feeling that made it*, which is a colder idea and a warmer practice than it
sounds. You do not stay because you still want to. You stay because you said
you would, and that turns out to be sturdier than wanting, and the people
inside your arrangements have simply never had to wonder.

**Everything here is judged in decades.** Whatever you build is the kind of
thing that is assessed at twenty years or not at all — a marriage, a firm, a
promise made to somebody at a kitchen table who is now sixty. Nothing you have
entered has needed renewing, because it was entered rather than drifted into,
and a thing entered on purpose does not require topping up.

There is a harder fact underneath, and it explains why this station sits on the
scorpion. **The arrangement is what a dangerous thing consents to be governed
by.** What you keep in place is not a mild thing kept mild. It is something
with real force in it, held by an undertaking, and the undertaking holds.

**And the difficulty is exit.** A chart that specialises in durable bonds is a
chart with no instinct for how to leave one, and these degrees supply no such
instinct at all. Not reluctance — *absence*. There is no procedure in you. When
an arrangement should end you do not weigh it and decide to stay; you find that
the question has no handle on it, and another decade goes by.

## The distinctions this row must hold

### 17 The Crown vs 18 The Heart — *both bonds*

**Nobody has drawn this line yet. It is being drawn here, and it governs 56
cells.**

- **The Crown** — the bond is **undertaken.** It began with somebody saying
  something to somebody, on purpose, as an equal. It holds because it was
  entered, and its difficulty is that a thing you entered has no exit built
  into it.
- **The Heart** — the bond is **occupied.** It is a position: seniority by a
  checkable date, nobody above you. Nobody entered it; you arrived at it and
  outlasted the people who might have ranked above you. Its difficulty is that
  precedence gives you nowhere to take a complaint.

**A promise versus a rank.** If your cell's bond could be described by a date
on a list rather than by something a person said, it is an 18 cell. Yours is
always traceable to a moment when it was undertaken.

### 17 The Crown vs 12 The Turning

Row 12 is written, and its second half is *what is owed is still owed* — an
obligation that survives a total break. **You are not that.** In 12 the thing
ends and the debt persists; in 17 **the thing does not end**, and that is the
whole problem. If your cell has the arrangement finishing, it is a 12 cell.

- **`17·17`** is the diagonal. Doubling the kept oath means **there is no

## ⚠ Banned tokens — including the words for what this mansion *is*

Read your packet's `banned` array before you draft. **Banned in all 28 cells:**

**`sworn`** · **`alliance`** · **`friendship`** · **`archway`** · `lotus` ·
`rabbit` · transliterations.

- **`sworn` is banned and this mansion is the sworn oath.** So is
  **`alliance`**, and so is **`friendship`** — three of the most natural words
  for the subject. Write *the oath*, *the undertaking*, *the agreement*, *what
  you said you would do*, *the promise*. `oath`, `promise`, `undertaking` and
  `agreement` are all permitted.

  friendship cell and cannot name it. Use *the friend*, *the two of you*, *what
  you are to each other*. `friend` and `friends` are separate tokens and pass.
- The ban is exact-token: `swear`, `swore`, `allied`, `friend` all pass. **The
  failure is one letter away and looks fine.**

`crown` and the keyword are permitted — the keyword is *the kept oath*.
