## What a sun in The Root *is*

A sun in **The Root** goes to the bottom of things, and **the mansion is honest
that this is a destructive act.** The talent is finding the actual cause — the
one underneath the three plausible ones, the one everybody had already agreed
to stop at. You are extremely good at this. When something is genuinely wrong
and nobody can say why, you are the person who finds it, and you find it below
the level anyone else was willing to go to.

**You cannot examine a root without pulling the plant up.** That is not a
metaphor for a risk; it is the mechanism. The examination and the damage are
the same motion. To see what a thing is standing on you take the standing away,
and you do this to marriages, to institutions, to your own account of your
childhood, to the story a friend has been telling themselves for nine years —
and you are right every time.

**And nothing here supplies a stopping signal.** These degrees give no
mechanism for deciding that a thing has been understood well enough already.
There is no point at which the search reports back that it is finished; there
is only the next layer, and the next, and the fact that you can still see
further down. Other people stop because they are satisfied. That feeling has never
been issued to you — you stop when you hit something that will not come apart,
or when nothing is left standing to work on.

## The distinctions this row must hold

### 19 The Root vs 16 The Claws — *both refuse the simple version*

**The distinction is direction and stopping.**

- **The Root** — goes **one way, straight down, all the way.** No fork, no
  stopping point. The cost is **destruction**: examining and dismantling are
  the same act. The failure mode is arriving at the bottom and finding the
  thing is dead.
- **The Claws** — goes **two ways at once, laterally, and both are real.** The
  doubleness is load-bearing, not indecision. The cost is **speed**. The
  failure mode is never arriving at one.

Row 16 is being written alongside this one. **Never write your subject as
carrying two things at once.** A 19 cell about holding both sides, working two
directions, or refusing to pick has walked into 16's row. Your subject goes one
way and the direction is *down*.

### 19 The Root vs 02 The Bearer — *both about what is underneath*

Row 02 is written and its subject holds one chosen thing through a term and
controls its release — a carried thing that comes out on a day. Yours is not
carried and there is no day. It is excavated, and the excavation is what breaks
the ground it was in.
