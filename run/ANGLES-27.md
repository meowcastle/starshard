## Batch A — 27·01 … 27·04

- **27·01** — a family sells the house, takes the job, gives the notice — none of that is yours. yours is the morning it all has to move, both ends of that day, and a hallway four hundred miles off.
- **27·02** — three of them got out of that house and you drove all three. the youngest tells it as a decision she made at twenty, and what she had to be walked past has been in you since you were fifteen.
- **27·03** — she had four thousand pounds she did not know was hers, and she believes an office found it. she had written twice and given up. the finding took nine months and happened in buildings she would not go into alone.

## Batch B — 27·05 … 27·08

## Batch C — 27·09 … 27·12

- **27·10** — eleven people hold posts here because you walked them the length of a process nobody explains, and each tells it as an application they made and won. the standing you spent doing it was carved before you were born.

## Batch D — 27·13 … 27·16

- **27·14** — the only object with your name on it took nine days and is free to anybody. the four hundred hours a year you spend walking people through the same thing have never been written down by anybody, including you.
- **27·15** — your own household cannot account for your thursdays. eleven years of them, and what was happening on each was a road, a person you never brought home, and the last part of something being done with them.
