## Batch A — 13·01 … 13·04

- **13·01** *work / trade.* The work is finished before anyone requests it. The
  fix, the jig, the form exists before the meeting that would have decided to
  want it. Nothing you made was ever chosen, so no process that runs on
  requests can record any of it, and the visible evidence is the handful of
  things nobody needed.
- **13·02** *family of origin.* The child who knew why the house was going and
  spent it on other people's exits — rooms, fares, applications that carried
  four siblings out. Each tells their leaving as their own. The fact was never
  told, and the two people it would have helped are dead.
- **13·03** *money.* A living made of seven separate things you started and
  handed off. One figure from any distance; seven small unrelated incomes at
  rates other people fixed. The single number no bank, landlord or form can be
  given is the one thing the life never produced.
- **13·04** *the body.* A regimen taken whole off one man at nineteen and
  propagated into other bodies by demonstration — the hour, the distance, the
  refusal at the table, unrevised for thirty years, now running in eleven
  people who each tell an honest story of how they got there.

## Batch B — 13·05 … 13·08

- **13·05** *friendship.* The appetite selects who gets built for: a person one
  conversation from a decision they have circled for years gets a concrete
  first piece inside a fortnight, and the hunger goes flat in the same
  fortnight they are most grateful. The tension is the **return leg** — they
  want to do something back and find no stuck person to be useful to.
- **13·06** *a public room.* A hall you started things in for twelve years,
  where every made thing left under someone else's arrangements and one
  four-minute scene fixed itself to you personally. The only thing that ever
  adhered to you is the thing you did not make on purpose.
- **13·07** *something resumed.* Back after eleven years to the Thursday night
  you started, told the origin story wrong to your face by a nineteen-year-old,
  warm and welcome and with no place to re-enter — because a place is what you
  built it not to need.
- **13·08** *grief.* The only way you love anyone is by making them things; the
  man dies and the apparatus swings onto everything still attached to him.
  Grief with nothing to brace against. The only account you could give of your
  mourning is a list of jobs, which would land as an invoice.

## Batch C — 13·09 … 13·12

- **13·09** *strangers.* A six-minute meeting converted into a push before the
  conversation ends. The made thing detaches instantly and unattributed while
  the read of the person is held whole for decades. They cannot read you back:
  there is no visible body of work, so first impressions run on manner alone.
- **13·10** *institutions / succession.* You occupy something carved before
  you, most of what the place runs on came out of your hands, and every start
  went in wearing the institution's clothes. There is a procedure for passing
  on the seat and none for passing on the record — and from a chair, listing
  what you started reads as exactly the unease the chair is meant to settle.
- **13·11** *display as size.* The width does the routing and deletes the
  request. Nobody has to ask, and the request was the only document these
  transactions were ever going to produce. Known enormously for a capacity,
  with no case anyone can name.
- **13·12** *an ending.* The molt is total and reaches nothing you made,
  because it left your hands years earlier. No equipment to sell, no successor
  to brief, no wake — so the leaving reads as restlessness. What crosses is
  the owing.

## Batch D — 13·13 … 13·16

- **13·13** *THE DIAGONAL — accounting.* Nothing you made is in the room you
  live in, and the second copy of you that would have kept the private ledger
  was never issued. Reaching and releasing are one movement, so a thing is
  never yours long enough to be felt as yours.
- **13·14** *the adjacent pair.* The one attributed object is the
  advertisement and the untraceable hand pays the bill: people arrive for the
  named piece and leave with an hour's unrecorded making. The named piece is
  the only exhibit, so when it is handled or copied there is no supporting body
  of work behind it.
- **13·15** *not being placed.* The form that wants a title and a place. What
  you started has no return address because you were never standing next to
  it; the composite reference exists nowhere and each referee holds one
  fragment.
- **13·16** *a fight.* You make with whoever is in front of you, including the
  opponent, so their best move left your hands months ago and cannot be
  reclaimed or proven. Your own side reads the continued contact as going soft.
