## What a sun in The Listener *is*

A sun in **The Listener** hears what a room is actually saying underneath what
it is saying, and **the organ does not close.** That is the whole arrangement.
Not perceptiveness as a skill you deploy — an intake that runs whether or not
you want it running, in the dark, at dinner, in a meeting you did not care
about, at a funeral. You came out of a room knowing which marriage is over and
which apology was rehearsed, and you did not decide to find out.

The other half of the mansion is the ox, and that half is where the damage is.
**These degrees hand out the season's work, and nobody thanks the animal that
carried it.** Because you always know what is actually needed, you are always
the one who does it — the difficult conversation, the arrangement nobody else
noticed was falling apart, the thing that would have gone wrong. And because
you did it before it became visible as a problem, **there is no version of the
event in which anybody can see that you did anything.** The work was invisible
by construction. It was invisible *because* it worked.

There is one more thing about this station and it belongs to the reader without
being explained: this mansion is a **seam**. It is the one place on the road
where the count itself gives way — where the road was broken and rejoined, and
the join is you. Write from that if the cell wants it; do not explain it.

## The distinctions this row must hold

### 22 The Listener vs 20 The Flock — *both stand at an angle to a group*

**The distinction is intake versus position.**

- **The Listener** — this is about **what reaches you.** The signal arrives
  whether or not you are a member, whether or not you want it; the organ cannot
  be shut. The price is unwitnessed labour: you act on what you heard,
  correctly, and the correctness is what makes it invisible.
- **The Flock** — this is about **where you stand.** You are of a group and at
  its edge at the same time, and the position lets you *sort* — see the seams a
  group cannot see in itself. You pay for that by sorting what was never yours
  to sort, irreversibly.

Row 20 is being written alongside this one. **Never write your subject as
standing half in and half out of a group.** A 22 cell about membership at an
angle, or about being on the roll and off it, has walked into 20's row. Your
subject's relation to a room is not a *position* — it is a *reception*, and it
works on rooms it has no place in at all.

### 22 The Listener vs 09 The Glance — *both are perception*

Row 09 is written. Its subject is the **grip** — the read taken and held. Yours
is the **channel** — always open, never closed, and the problem is not what you
retain but that you cannot stop taking it in. If your cell is about holding a
reading, it is a 09 cell.

## ⚠ Banned tokens that collide with this row's vocabulary

Read your packet's `banned` array before you draft. **Banned in all 28 cells of
this row:**

**`three`** · **`crossed`** · **`worlds`** · `footprints` · `strides` ·
`dabih` · `giedi` · plus the transliterations.

**`three` is the one that will catch you.** It is an ordinary numeral —
*three years, three of them, the third time* is fine but bare `three` is not.
`third` and `thirteen` are separate tokens and pass.

**`crossed` will also catch you** — *crossed the room, arms crossed, crossed
out* all fail. `cross`, `crossing` and `crosses` are separate tokens and pass.

`ear` and the epithet are permitted — they are the keyword and the name.

The leak gate catches sign names and month names, including the bare modal
**`may`**.
