## 2. What failed and why

Nothing in the gate. Four things to know:

**The cast screen was not screenshotted.** The smoke makes its accounts through `api.js`, not through the game's cast, so the rewritten line is checked in the built file (the `mz4 static` line) and not on screen. The line keeps the step's own 9.5 px Karla and muted gold with a 300 px measure; the phone pass (MZ8) owns the game's type sizes.

**The overlay is Escape only.** `pauseOn` is set by the Escape handler and nowhere else, so a phone never sees it; in a match a phone has the player-chip menu, which carries "account" only when no match is running (a plain link would lose the board; the menu's own destinations keep it). Part 5 has the option.

**No delete form inside the game.** The packet's words are "in the pause menu"; what is in the pause menu is the door to the delete, one tap away, password-confirmed on the account page. An in-game form needs new bindings in the game's `renderVals` closure and a wipe of its own, a game edit for MZ1's copy. Part 5.

**No browser on the Mac's VM**, as before: the smoke ran in the sandbox against the byte-identical build (hashes above). The Mac ran the build, the 230 and the 28.

Choices made rather than asked:

- **New tab from the overlay, same tab from the lobby.** In a match, leaving the page would lose the board without the overlay's own "forfeit the climb" question; in the lobby nothing is lost and the shell is the natural next screen.
- **Labels by what they do.** "a copy of your data" and "delete the account" are the account page's own words for its two sections, so the traveler reads the same thing on both sides of the door. "account", "privacy" and "terms" are already in the shell's nav and the signup line. No new noun.
- **The sweep after a delete** also clears `manzil-settings` (the sound preference) because it sits under the game's prefix; a deleted account's sound setting is not worth a second rule.
- **The report is named for the schedule's firing minute** (15:12 New York), the lock holds the exact time (20:13:10 UTC).
