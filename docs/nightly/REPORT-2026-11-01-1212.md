## 2. What failed and why

**The lint's prose rules on the corpus.** The gate says "writing lint clean". `tools/writing-lint.mjs` has two mechanical rules (no em dashes; "station", never "mansion") and three prose rules (the fourth-wall constructions, the throat-clearing connectives, "you will"). The corpus in `reading-copy.js` was generated from `research/` before `WRITING.md` fixed those rules: 602 em dashes and 220 "mansion"s, 15 "you will"s and 11 "which means" / "that's why"s. The packet reuses `reading.js` unchanged and the corpus files are outside the write list, so the two mechanical rules are applied at render by `app/src/house.js` (the fix the lint itself prescribes: an em dash becomes a comma pair, a spaced en dash between degrees becomes "to", the word becomes station), and the test holds that pass clean over every slot. The prose rules are a writer's job, not a regular expression's; they are left in the corpus and listed in part 5 with their slot ids. Two of the 28 corpus findings are the verb "sounding" ("dogs sounding in the dark", "constantly sounding it"), which the lint's retired-noun rule cannot tell from the noun; they are false positives, which is also a decision (a word-boundary rule in the lint, or two rewrites).
