## 2. What failed and why

Nothing in the gate failed. What could not be done the straight way:

**No browser on the Mac's VM**, as in the SS4 and SS5 runs (`npx playwright install chromium` was tried once more; the download host is outside the allowlist). The smoke ran in the sandbox against a byte-identical build, hashes above. `npx playwright install chromium` once on the Mac itself and `node test/smoke-app.mjs` runs strict there.

**The device commit tool wrote a stale copy twice** (an edit to a file it had already written once came through as the earlier version). Both times the file was patched in place on the Mac with the same edit and its hash checked against the sandbox copy; every file listed in part 4 was verified by hash or by grep after writing.

Choices made rather than asked:

- **The planets beyond the Moon come from astronomy-engine**, not from the Schlyter table the device sky uses for tonight. A natal chart is a document a visitor compares with other sites; Schlyter is good to a degree, astronomy-engine to arcseconds (part 3). It is the same library `transits.js` already trusts, loaded on demand in its own chunk so the first load does not carry it.
- **All ten bodies, not seven.** Tonight shows the seven that move nightly; the chart is the whole sky at one minute, and Uranus, Neptune and Pluto are on every printed chart. Their definitions say they mark a generation more than a person.
- **Aspects between every pair** of the ten bodies, plus the ascendant and midheaven when the time is known, with `transits.js`'s orbs (PRODUCT.md section 3's table, written for the four trusted points when there were only four). The ascendant and midheaven are never aspected to each other (they are a fixed right angle near the equator and the aspect would mean nothing). Tightest first, with the plain names from the table (stacked, facing, easy, grinding, available).
- **The wheel's orientation** is the printed chart's: ascendant at the left, zodiac counter-clockwise, house 1 below the ascendant. A noon chart turns 0° aries to the left.
- **The stations as the outer ring** are the game's codex ring in the game's quarter colours, the natal moon's station lit the way the game lights the moon's station tonight (full alpha, amber shadow, breathing). The brief calls the 28 "the thing no other chart shows"; the page says it in one line under the aspects.
- **Birth entry lives on the chart page**, as its empty state, since SS7 (the first run) and SS8 (accounts) are not built. The record is kept on the device under one localStorage key and, when signed in, sent to `PUT /api/me/birth` and read back from it, the game's own one-time chart grab contract. The form is three fields: date, time (or "the time is not known"), place; the place lookup is `api.geocode()`, Open-Meteo, the same lookup the game's cast uses. The error lines are the game's ("the sky can't read that date").
- **A place typed but not picked from the list** casts a chart without houses and says so; the alternative, refusing to cast, would stop a visitor with a village Open-Meteo does not know.
- **The glyphs carry U+FE0E.** The zodiac signs, Venus and Mars default to colour emoji on phones; the variation selector asks for the text form so the wheel is drawn in one ink (the sandbox's first screenshots showed the emoji; fixed and re-shot).
- **The term tooltip** is CSS on hover and focus and a toggle on tap, measured and shifted back inside the viewport when it would spill, flipped below the term when it would go above the top. On a touch screen hover is ignored so a tap owns it.
- **The polar sentence** from PRODUCT.md section 2 ("your birthplace is far enough north...") is on the page, worded for south as well, shown when `astro.js` falls back to Porphyry.
