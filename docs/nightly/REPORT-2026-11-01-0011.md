## 2. What failed and why

Nothing in the gate failed. What could not be done the straight way:

**No browser on the Mac's VM**, as in the SS4 run (the Playwright CDN is outside the allowlist). The smoke ran in the sandbox against a byte-identical build, hashes above. `npx playwright install chromium` once on the Mac and `node test/smoke-app.mjs` runs strict there.

**The design canvas is still not in the repo.** The board "Home · the lobby" exists only in the Design tool. The lobby was built to STARSHARD-LAUNCH §2.3's row for Home (V2's horizon scene, tonight's station as the headline, walk / read / codex, the chart and shard cards, the player chip) and to the game file itself, which §0 ranks above the boards.

Choices made rather than asked:

- **The scene is lifted, not drawn.** `app/scene-extract.mjs` reads the game file and writes `app/src/scene.html` and `scene.css`: the lobby stage's own layers at street level on a generic night, with the game's `{{ xStyle }}` bindings resolved the way its `renderVals` resolves them at elevation 0. Every SVG byte is the game's; the script resolves bindings and drops the layers whose opacity is 0 at street level on every night (the tower, the bridge, the high clouds, the aurora, the milky way, the climb's stars). The per-mansion set pieces (the chamber, the well, the kiln and so on) are the game's levels, not the street, and are not in the fragment.
- **The hour.** In the game the lobby's four lighting sets (dusk, full dark, the small hours, the hour before dawn) follow the level, not the clock. STARSHARD-LAUNCH §2.2 describes the scene as changing with the real hour, so the shell maps the clock onto the same four sets: 17 to 21 dusk, 21 to 01 full dark, 01 to 04 the small hours, 04 to 07 the hour before dawn, and daylight (07 to 17) the first set, the game's own default. If Justin would rather daylight had its own look, the game has none to lift and it would be a decision. The hour re-reads every minute while the page is open, with the game's own 1.6 s cross-fade.
- **The ring on Tonight is the game's codex ring**, not `sigil.js`'s 112-segment geometry. The packet says "the sigil ring"; the game's shipped ring is 28 segments in the four quarter colours with the moon's station breathing in amber, and §0 rule 3 says the newest shipped surface is the reference. `sigil.js`'s `sigilRingData()` stays available for the natal marks and steps SS7 will add (the `marks` prop is the slot).
- **The home's headline is "the moon is in the glance"**, the station name in gold. The alternative, the station name alone as the `<h1>`, is what Tonight does; the home needs the sentence because a stranger has not met the word station yet.
- **The fallback planets are Schlyter's elements**, `ephemeris2.js`'s table copied verbatim into `app/src/sky-device.js` (the game's file is an IIFE on `window`, not an importable module). Their agreement with the server is in part 3. The Moon and the Sun are `astro.js` on both sides and agree exactly.
- **The two cards on the home are cards.** APP-FRAMEWORK §5 allows a card for a heterogeneous unit tappable as a whole; each is a title, a line and a door, and the whole panel is the link. They are drawn as the game's panel (the .05 gold fill, the .14 outline, the inner inset line, 7 px radius, from the shard screen's card).
- **"walk tonight's road"** is the door to the game. "Road" is the game's own word for the walk ("the road begins again", "walk the road"); COSMOLOGY's unspent "Moonroad" is not used.
- **Retrograde** is written ℞ beside the planet's name, with the four-word definition on first contact under the table ("℞ marks a planet moving backward against the stars"), per WRITING.md's rule to use the astrology term.
