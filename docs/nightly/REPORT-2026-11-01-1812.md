## 2. What failed and why

Nothing in the gate. Two things to know:

**No browser on the Mac's VM**, as in the SS4 to SS7 runs. The smoke ran in the sandbox against a byte-identical build, hashes above. The sources went across in `_to_delete/ss8-src-bundle.tgz` and came back in `_to_delete/ss8-out-bundle.tgz`; both carriers can be deleted. The sandbox's own Playwright had to be the preinstalled 1.63.0 (the root `package-lock` pins 1.62.1, whose browser is not there); nothing about that is in the repo.

**The server has no change-password route**, so "your password" on the account page sends the reset link (`POST /api/auth/forgot-password` with the account's own email) rather than taking a new password in place. A route would be a `starshard-api/` change with tests; queued in part 5.

Choices made rather than asked:

- **Who is signed in is one store** (`app/src/account.js`, read by `useMe()` through `useSyncExternalStore`), asked once at mount by `main.jsx` and written by the account pages. Before, `shell.jsx` asked `/api/me` once and nothing could change the chip without a reload. `apiReachable()` moved there with it; `shell.jsx` re-exports both so nothing else moved.
- **The age check is the birth date alone**, prefilled from the device's chart when there is one, and the rest opens only on an "ok" from `/api/auth/age-check`; the server re-checks at signup regardless, as `api.js` documents. The refusal is the game's own line ("the sky asks you to come back in a year or two") with the sky's free surfaces offered and a way back for a mistyped date. A `too_young` answer at signup itself (a date changed between the two calls) shows the same refusal.
- **The chart goes with the account when its date is the one checked.** The fields step says so in one line ("your chart goes with the account: ...") with "change it" to the chart page. A date typed differently from the device's record signs up with the date alone (`birthTimeKnown: false`), and the line says to cast the chart once the account exists. The birthplace zone is sent as `birthTz` (SS1's field, the answered decision); `tz` on the body stays the device's region signal, as `api.js` fills it.
- **Sign in takes an email or a name**: an `@` sends `email`, otherwise `username` lowercased; the server's login accepts either.
- **The mail's links keep their shape.** `server.js` sends `${APP_URL}/#resetToken=...` and `/#verifyEmail=...`; `main.jsx` opens `/reset` or `/verify` for either hash on any path, the router now keeps a hash on `navigate`, and the pages clear the token from the address bar once it is spent.
- **`/verify` without a token** is headed "confirm your email" (the link in the mail confirms the address), not the route table's "email confirmed", which it is headed only after the server says so; a bad or spent token is "that link has expired". `/reset` without a token is headed "forgot your password". The `<title>`s still come from the route table ("email confirmed", "reset your password"); one line each in `routes.mjs` if they should follow the state.
- **Sign out leaves the chart on the device** and says so ("signing out leaves the chart on this device and takes the account off it"). The record was the visitor's own before the account existed; the game's own sign-in handles a shared device by pulling the incoming account's record unconditionally, which `birth.js` already does. A shared-device rule (clear the device on sign out) is one line in `account.jsx` if Justin wants it.
- **Delete clears the device**: the birth record and the burst flag, so the next visitor meets the arrival. The page then says "your account is gone" rather than bouncing home.
- **The export is downloaded, not shown**: `GET /api/me/export`'s JSON as `star-shard-data.json`, with one status line on the page. The server's own `Content-Disposition` does not survive a `fetch` with credentials, so the page builds the file.
- **"charts" in the packet is one chart**: the server holds one `birth_data` row per account, so the account page shows the one record, its words, and the doors to the chart, the shard and the game.
- **The copy** is the game's where the game has a line (sign in: "welcome back", "the road remembers you", "sign in ›"; signup: "the road needs a name to call you", "a way to reach you, for the nights that matter", "eight characters or more") and the shell's elsewhere; the hearts of `api.js`'s old v4 copy are not used. The server's codes map to the shell's words in `account.js`; an unknown code falls back per form.
- **The legal pages**: privacy "your rights" names the two buttons ("keep a copy of your data", "delete my account") and keeps the address for a correction, a restriction, an objection, or when the account page is out of reach; terms "ending it" says "on the account page, with your password". Both stamped 1 November 2026.
