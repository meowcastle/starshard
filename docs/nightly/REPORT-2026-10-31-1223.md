## 2. What failed and why

Nothing failed. Choices made rather than asked:

**Which engine serves which body.** The packet says "from `astro.js`". `astro.js` has only the
Sun and the Moon, and `astro.js` sits at the repo root, outside the night shift's write list, so
no planets were added to it. The planets Mercury to Saturn come from `transits.js`'s
`planetPositions()` (the vendored `astronomy-engine.js`, with the retrograde flag), which is
what `sky.js` and `transits.js` already declare canonical for everything `astro.js` cannot do.
Checked against the Swiss fixture on ten dates: maximum 0.0078 deg. `ephemeris2.js`'s own
Schlyter planets (0.1 to 1 deg) are not used by the server. The response names its engines
(`engine: { sunMoon: 'astro.js', planets: 'astronomy-engine.js' }`).

**The Moon is astro.js, per the packet, though the game's engine is the better Moon.** See
part 5. The packet's instruction and the codebase's "astro.js is canonical for the Moon" both
point at astro.js, so that is what ships; the data says the other engine is closer to the sky.
Nothing a traveler sees differs on more than one night in two years either way.

**The night rule and the zone.** A night runs 06:00 to 06:00 local and is read at 21:00 local
(`ephemeris2.js`'s `nightAnchor()`, unchanged). `tz` is an IANA zone; the wall clock becomes
an instant through `tz.js`'s `offsetFor()`, so DST on the night in question is honoured (test
2 covers EST, EDT and Tokyo). No `tz` means UTC and the response says so. An unknown zone or an
unreadable `at` is a 400 with `bad_tz` or `bad_at`.

**The next crossing is searched from the anchor, not from the request instant**, so the answer
belongs to the night and does not change between 21:00 and 06:00 (test 3). Ten-minute steps
then bisection to the second.

**Public, cached, rate limited.** The route needs no session (a signed-out stranger's home
page is its first caller), sends `Cache-Control: public, max-age=300`, and has a per-IP
ceiling of 60 a minute. No database access.

**Vocabulary.** The JSON uses `station` (1 to 28), the traveler-facing word and the one
`stations.js` uses, not `mansion`. Nothing new a traveler reads.

**The referee fixture was computed off the Mac.** The Mac's VM has no Swiss Ephemeris and the
mount cannot `pip install`; the sandbox could. The fixture is static data with its source stated
in `_source`; the tests never call out to anything.

`package.json`) and `BACKLOG.md` still uncommitted since 28 Oct, alongside tonight's files.
