# The twenty-eight, second draft

**26 August 2026.** Rewritten after playing the live build. Every card is checked against the four
board facts and carries a scenario. Faces unchanged.

---

## The four facts every card was checked against

1. **A taken card does not move.** It flips in place. No ability may assume a card relocates when
   it changes hands.
2. **The board fills.** Nine stations, ten cards. Nothing is empty at the count, so no scoring
   condition may depend on an empty station.
3. **Everything always stands.** A taken card is still on the board. Conditions must be about
   *ownership*, which changes, never about presence, which does not.
4. **Restriking the same target with the same numbers is a no-op.** A second strike must happen at a
   different moment, or reach a different station.

**And one thing that follows from fact 2:** cards that move only work while a station is open, which
is roughly the first four turns. Move cards are early-board cards by nature. That is a real timing
decision, not a flaw.

## The bar

A signature must beat **+1 on both faces**, which is the level-2 alternative. +1/+1 helps in every
fight the card is in, so a signature that fires once on one target is usually **under** the bar.
Persistent effects, count effects, and whole extra fights clear it.

## The frame

**L1** numbers · **L2** +1 both faces, or the signature · **L3** +1 both faces, or the quadrant grant
· **L4** full art, animation, a respec, and a respec again every time you beat the mansion after.
Two cards may be locked into every hand.

| quadrant | grant | permutations |
|---|---|---|
| **Byakko** the white tiger | its ground cannot be taken | deny a fight · lower a number |
| **Suzaku** the vermilion bird | once a board, tap to strike its neighbours again | add a fight · raise a number |
| **Seiryuu** the azure dragon | it may be lodged either way round | turn a card · move a card |
| **Genbu** the black tortoise | its station counts one more | change the worth · change the owner |

**Each quadrant gets one card that reaches outside its two.** Marked ✧ below. Four across
twenty-eight, so the identity holds.

# BYAKKO · the white tiger
*West. Autumn. Metal. What holds, and what wears down.*

### 1 · the gate — al-Sharaṭān — 6/6
*The two horns that open the year. The first one through.*
**deny · always.** The first strike against the gate does not land.

> You lodge the gate at station 4. She answers at station 5 with a 9 facing your 6 and would take it.
> The gate refuses that strike and holds. Her next attack lands normally. You bought one station and
> one turn.

### 2 · the bearer — al-Buṭayn — 6/5
*The little belly, where soft things are kept until they are ready.*
**lower · always.** Anything striking a card beside the bearer fights two lower.

> The bearer at 5, your 6/6 at 6. She lodges a 7 at station 7. It fights as a 5, loses to your 6, and
> your card holds. Without the bearer her 7 takes it outright. Note the bearer protects both sides
> and does nothing for itself.

### 3 · the gathered stars — al-Thurayyā — 7/7
*Six or seven little lights that the eye reads as one.*
**deny · always.** While a card beside it is yours, the gathered stars cannot be taken.

> Stars at 5, your card at 4. She cannot take the stars from station 6 at all. To open them she has
> to take your card at 4 first, from station 3, which costs her a turn and tells you exactly what she
> is doing.

### 4 · the follower ✧ — al-Dabarān — 8/7
*It follows what it loves across the whole sky and never loses sight of it.*
**add a fight · on claim.** When the card to the follower's left is taken, the follower strikes it
back at once.

> Your 6/6 at station 4, the follower at 5. She takes station 4 from the left. The follower
> immediately strikes station 4 with its own left face of 8 and takes it straight back. She has spent
> a card to gain nothing.

### 5 · the blaze — al-Haqʿa — 6/6
*The white mark on the brow. The one who leaves, learns, and comes home lit.*
**lower · on lodge.** The blaze marks one card beside it. That card fights two lower for the rest of
the board, whoever holds it.

> Her 8/7 sits at station 6. The blaze lands at 5 and marks it. It is a 6/5 now, for good. You take
> it next turn with a 7, and when she tries to take it back she is fighting a 6/5 too. The mark
> follows the card, not the owner.

### 6 · the storm — al-Hanʿa — 8/6
*Not doom. Voltage. It feels everything at full strength and then the air is clear.*
**deny · always.** No tie ever takes the storm. A bigger number still can.

> The storm at 5 showing 6. She lodges a 6 at station 6. Normally a tie flips and she takes it. The
> storm holds. She needs a 7 to move it, and on a board full of sixes and sevens that is most of her
> hand ruled out.

### 28 · the thread — Baṭn al-Ḥūt, al-Rishāʾ — 6/6
*Two names at once. The belly of the fish and the cord. It ties the year's end to its beginning.*
**lower · always.** Everything standing beside the thread fights one lower, yours and theirs alike.

> Thread at 5. Her 7 at 6 is a 6. Your 6 at 4 is a 5. It makes the ground around it soft for
> everyone, so you place it where her cards are strong and yours are not, and you accept the cost on
> your own side. It belongs to both and is counted by neither.

# SUZAKU · the vermilion bird
*South. Summer. Fire. What reaches further, and what burns hotter.*

### 7 · the return — al-Dhirāʿ — 7/7
*The forearm. The twins. Coming back counts as going forward.*
**add a fight · delayed.** At the end of your next turn the return strikes both its neighbours again
from where it stands.

> The return lands at 5 and takes nothing, both neighbours beat it. You spend your next turn lodging
> the blaze at 6 and marking station 4. The return's delayed strike then lands on a station 4 that is
> two lower than it was, and takes it. The second strike matters because the board changed.

### 8 · the ghost — al-Nathra — 7/6
*The soft glow in the lion's muzzle. The warmest possible haunting.*
**raise · on lodge.** One card beside the ghost fights two higher for the rest of the board, whoever
holds it.

> Ghost at 4, your 6/6 at 5. That card is an 8/8 now and she needs a nine to move it. If she does
> take it, she has an 8/8 and you have to answer it. Warming a card you might lose is the decision.

### 9 · the glance — al-Ṭarf — 5/6
*The lion's eye. One look and nothing gets past you.*
**add a fight · on lodge.** The glance also strikes the stations two along, past both its neighbours.

> Glance lands at 5. It fights 4 and 6 as any card would, and also 3 and 7. Four stations from one
> placement. Drop it into the middle of her line and it can unpick three of them at once.

### 10 · the throne — al-Jabha — 7/9
*The lion's forehead. Regulus. You meet things crown first.*
**raise · on claim.** Whatever the throne takes fights one higher for the rest of the board.

> The throne at 5 shows its 9 to the right and takes her 6/6 at station 6. That card is yours and it
> is a 7/7 now, so her counterattack from station 7 needs a seven where a six would have done. Take
> two cards with the throne and you have built a wall out of hers.

### 11 · the mane — al-Zubra — 6/6
*Presence as liberation. It makes everything near it feel it can be its actual size.*
**raise · always.** Both cards beside the mane fight one higher while it stands.

> Mane at 5 with your cards at 4 and 6. Both are a point stronger on every face for the rest of the
> board. But if she takes station 6, the mane is now raising her card too. It lifts whoever is
> standing there, so where you put it is the whole card.

### 12 · the turning — al-Ṣarfa — 7/6
*The turning of the heat. The hinge the season swings on.*
**add a fight · on lodge.** Whatever the turning takes strikes onward in the same direction.

> The turning lands at 5 and takes station 6. Station 6 then strikes station 7 with its own face. One
> placement, two stations. With the tie cascade gone this is the only card in the deck that chains,
> which is what a hinge should be.

### 13 · the hand — al-ʿAwwāʾ, Skt Hasta — 7/5
*Making as a form of kindness.*
**raise · always.** Every card you lodge beside the hand lands one higher, for good.

> The hand at 5. You lodge a 6/6 at 4 and it is a 7/7 permanently. Next turn you lodge a 7/5 at 6 and
> it is an 8/6. The hand builds the line around itself and the cards keep the gift even if the hand
> falls.

# SEIRYUU · the azure dragon
*East. Spring. Growth. What changes its face, and what changes its place.*

### 14 · the jewel — al-Simāk al-Aʿzal — 8/7
*The unarmed high one. Spica. Brilliance that never needed to win.*
**turn · on claim.** Whenever a card lands beside the jewel, the jewel turns to face it with its
stronger number.

> The jewel sits at 5 with its 7 facing right. She lodges at 6. The jewel turns and meets her with
> its 8. She lodges at 4 instead next time, and the jewel turns again. It never attacks and it is
> never caught the wrong way round.

### 15 · the veil — al-Ghafr — 8/3
*The cover. Twelve layers of inner life behind a calm face.*
**turn · on claim.** The first card that lands beside the veil is turned, so its strong number ends
up facing away.

> She lodges her 9/3 at station 6 with the 9 pointed at your veil. The veil turns it as it lands, so
> her 3 faces you. Your 6 at station 7 takes it on your next turn. The veil is a trap you set and she
> walks into.

### 16 · the claws — al-Zubānā — 7/6
*They are not for grabbing. They are for holding the line, and for opening.*
**move · on lodge.** The claws push one card beside them into the next open station along.

> Her 9/3 at station 6 is threatening your cluster at 4 and 5. The claws land at 5 and shove her card
> to station 7, out of contact with everything of yours. Only works while a station is open, so this
> is a card you play in the first half.

### 17 · the crown ✧ — al-Iklīl — 7/6
*Love of long duration. Not the spark. The keeping.*
**deny · on claim.** Any card the crown takes cannot be taken back.

> The crown at 5 takes her 6/6 at station 6. Station 6 is yours for the rest of the board and nothing
> she has will move it. She can still take the crown itself, and it will not give station 6 back.
> One card, one permanent station.

### 18 · the heart — al-Qalb — 8/7
*Antares, red in the scorpion's chest. Aim the heart and it lands.*
**move · on tap.** Once a board, move the heart to any open station. It strikes as it arrives.

> The heart is sitting at station 2 doing nothing while she builds a line at 6, 7 and 8. Station 9
> opens. Tap: the heart crosses the road, lands at 9 and strikes station 8 with its 8. One tap turns
> a wasted card into an attack in the place that matters.

### 19 · the root — al-Shawla, Skt Mūla — 7/7
*Where others skim, you dig to the cause. The truth at the root can sting.*
**turn · on lodge.** As the root lands, both cards beside it turn to show their other face.

> The root lands at 5 between her 9/3 showing 9 at station 6, and your 6/8 showing 8 at station 4.
> Both flip. Her 3 now faces you, which is what you wanted. Your 6 now faces her, which is what it
> cost. It upends everything around it and you have to want that.

### 20 · the flock — al-Naʿāʾim — 7/6
*Four ostriches walking to the river and four walking back. Winter is survived together.*
**move · on lodge.** As the flock lands, it and one card beside it trade places.

> Her strong card at 4. Your weak 5/6 at 5, about to be taken. You lodge the flock at 6 and swap it
> with your card at 5, so the flock now faces her and your weak card is safe behind it. They move as
> a group and the weak one ends up on the inside.

# GENBU · the black tortoise
*North. Winter. Water. What is worth more, and what belongs to whom.*

### 21 · the empty district — al-Balda — 3/8
*The station the old astronomers left blank. The centre of the galaxy is inside it.*
**worth · at the count.** The empty district counts two, and both stations beside it count for
nobody.

> District at 5, her strong cards at 4 and 6. At the count she scores nothing for either. You have
> deleted two stations from her total without fighting for them. Place it beside your own cards and
> you delete your own instead. It is the strongest card in the deck and the easiest to misplay.

### 22 · the listener — Saʿd al-Dhābiḥ, Skt Śravaṇa — 7/5
*To listen until the pattern shows itself. The listener is rarely the one heard.*
**worth · at the count.** The listener counts one more for every card standing beside it.

> The listener in the middle of the road counts three. The listener at station 1 or 9 has only one
> neighbour and counts two. The board always fills, so this is a reliable two or three, and the
> decision is simply never to strand it on an end.

### 23 · the drum — Saʿd Bulaʿ — 5/7
*You set the tempo of the room you are in. A drummer does not get to be off beat.*
**owner · at the count.** The station to the drum's right counts for the drum's side, whoever holds
the card.

> Drum at 5, your card at 6. She takes station 6 on turn three and thinks she has gained a point. At
> the count it is still yours, because your drum is beside it. To break it she has to come for the
> drum, which is exactly where you want her looking.

### 24 · the void — Saʿd al-Suʿūd — 9/3
*Luck of lucks. An opening is not a wound. Extremely good at holding space.*
**worth · at the count.** The void counts two, and both stations beside it count one less for
whoever holds them.

> Void at 5 with her card at 6 and your card at 4. You gain two from the void, lose one on your own
> neighbour, cost her one. Net plus two. Put it at station 1 or 9 and it has one neighbour instead of
> two, which changes the arithmetic entirely.

### 25 · the hideaway — Saʿd al-Akhbiya — 6/6
*Keeper of safe places. Everyone's shelter, nobody's guest.*
**owner · at the count.** The hideaway counts nothing for itself. Both stations beside it count one
more for whoever holds them.

> Hideaway at 5 with both neighbours yours: you gain two and lose the hideaway's own point, so net
> plus one. If she takes station 6 you are now paying to shelter her card. It helps whoever is
> standing there, which is the card, and the reason it goes at the edge of your line rather than the
> middle of hers.

### 26 · the chamber — al-Fargh al-Muqaddam — 7/6
*Go still and deep enough to hold weight. Foundations are under the house.*
**worth · at the count.** The chamber counts two while it has never been taken.

> Lodge the chamber at station 9. It has one neighbour, so there is only one direction it can be
> attacked from, and you can cover that. It holds all board and counts two. Lodge it at station 5 in
> the middle of the fight and it will change hands by turn four and count one.

### 27 · the guide — al-Fargh al-Muʾakhkhar — 6/6
*You want to see everyone home. You stopped choosing a destination a while ago.*
**worth · at the count.** While the guide stands, every one of your cards on its own mansion counts
one more.

> Tonight's road runs through the homes of two cards you kept from the twelve. Each already counts
> two for dominion. With the guide out they count three each. The guide is worth nothing if you drew
> no home cards, and it is the best card in your hand if you drafted for the road.

## What to check before building

**Measure all twenty-eight against the bar.** The ones I expect to sit under +1/+1 and need another
pass: the gate, the hideaway, and possibly the jewel. The ones I expect to be over the line and need
watching: the empty district, the glance, and the crown.

**The empty district silencing both sides may be too strong.** The engine's measured version silences
both. If it runs away, silence one side, the one facing away from tonight's mansion.

**Three cards need open stations** — the claws, the heart and the flock. Confirm they are still
playable at the point in a board where a hand actually holds them.

### Two engine changes this assumes

1. **The tie cascade is deleted.** The turning is now the only card that chains, which is the point.
2. **The Genbu grant is "counts one more,"** not the old return-to-hand, which measured as inert.
