# The twenty-eight, rebuilt

**26 August 2026.** Every card built from its own lore, inside the seven permutations, assigned to
its quadrant's two. Nothing here needs a rule the engine does not already have.

**Faces are unchanged.** They are balanced and tested and I have not touched them. Everything new is
in the signature.

---

## The frame

**Level 1** — the numbers. Nothing else.
**Level 2** — choose: **+1 to both faces**, or **this card's signature**.
**Level 3** — choose: **+1 to both faces**, or **your quadrant's grant**.
**Level 4** — the full artwork, the animation, and a respec. Beat the mansion again after that and
you may respec again, as often as you like. Two cards may be locked into every hand.

### The four grants

| quadrant | grant |
|---|---|
| **Byakko**, the white tiger | **its ground cannot be taken** |
| **Suzaku**, the vermilion bird | **once a board, tap it to strike its neighbours again** |
| **Seiryuu**, the azure dragon | **it may be lodged either way round** |
| **Genbu**, the black tortoise | **its station counts one more** |

### The seven permutations, and who owns them

| | |
|---|---|
| **Byakko** 1-6, 28 | deny a fight · lower a number |
| **Suzaku** 7-13 | add a fight · raise a number |
| **Seiryuu** 14-20 | turn a card · move a card |
| **Genbu** 21-27 | change the worth · change the owner |

# BYAKKO · the white tiger
*West. Autumn. Metal. What holds, and what wears down.*

### 1 · the gate — al-Sharaṭān — 6/6
*The two horns that open the year. The first one through.*
**Signature · deny · always.** The first strike against the gate does not land. After that it fights
like anything else.

### 2 · the bearer — al-Buṭayn — 6/5
*The little belly, where soft things are kept until they are ready.*
**Signature · lower · always.** Anything striking a card beside the bearer fights one lower.

### 3 · the gathered stars — al-Thurayyā — 7/7
*Six or seven little lights that the eye reads as one.*
**Signature · deny · always.** While another of your cards stands beside it, the gathered stars
cannot be taken.

### 4 · the follower — al-Dabarān — 8/7
*It follows what it loves across the whole sky and never loses sight of it.*
**Signature · deny · always.** While the card to its left still stands, the follower cannot be taken.

### 5 · the blaze — al-Haqʿa — 6/6
*The white mark on the brow. The one who leaves, learns, and comes home lit.*
**Signature · lower · on lodge.** The blaze marks one card beside it. That card fights two lower for
the rest of the board, whoever holds it.

### 6 · the storm — al-Hanʿa — 8/6
*Not doom. Voltage. It feels everything at full strength and then the air is clear.*
**Signature · deny · always.** No tie ever takes the storm. A bigger number still can.

### 28 · the thread — Baṭn al-Ḥūt, al-Rishāʾ — 6/6
*Two names at once. The belly of the fish and the cord. It ties the year's end to its beginning.*
**Signature · lower · always.** Everything standing beside the thread fights one lower, yours and
theirs alike. It belongs to both sides and is counted by neither.

# SUZAKU · the vermilion bird
*South. Summer. Fire. What reaches further, and what burns hotter.*

### 7 · the return — al-Dhirāʿ — 7/7
*The forearm. The twins. Coming back counts as going forward.*
**Signature · add a fight · on lodge.** The return strikes twice as it lands: once, then once again.

### 8 · the ghost — al-Nathra — 7/6
*The soft glow in the lion's muzzle. The warmest possible haunting.*
**Signature · raise · on lodge.** One card beside the ghost fights two higher for the rest of the
board, whoever holds it.

### 9 · the glance — al-Ṭarf — 5/6
*The lion's eye. One look and nothing gets past you.*
**Signature · add a fight · on lodge.** The glance also strikes the station two along, past its own
neighbour.

### 10 · the throne — al-Jabha — 7/9
*The lion's forehead. Regulus. You meet things crown first.*
**Signature · raise · always.** The throne fights one higher on the side facing tonight's mansion.

### 11 · the mane — al-Zubra — 6/6
*Presence as liberation. It makes everything near it feel it can be its actual size.*
**Signature · raise · always.** Both cards beside the mane fight one higher while it stands.

### 12 · the turning — al-Ṣarfa — 7/6
*The turning of the heat. The hinge the season swings on.*
**Signature · add a fight · on lodge.** The turning strikes both ways at once with its higher face,
whichever way it was lodged.

### 13 · the hand — al-ʿAwwāʾ, Skt Hasta — 7/5
*Making as a form of kindness.*
**Signature · add a fight · on claim.** When a card beside the hand is taken, the hand strikes it
back at once.

# SEIRYUU · the azure dragon
*East. Spring. Growth. What changes its face, and what changes its place.*

### 14 · the jewel — al-Simāk al-Aʿzal — 8/7
*The unarmed high one. Spica. Brilliance that never needed to win.*
**Signature · turn · on lodge.** The jewel lands showing whichever of its faces is stronger against
what stands beside it.

### 15 · the veil — al-Ghafr — 8/3
*The cover. Twelve layers of inner life behind a calm face.*
**Signature · turn · on claim.** The first card that lands beside the veil is turned, so its strong
number ends up facing away.

### 16 · the claws — al-Zubānā — 7/6
*They are not for grabbing. They are for holding the line, and for opening.*
**Signature · move · on lodge.** The claws push one card beside them along the road, into the next
open station.

### 17 · the crown — al-Iklīl — 7/6
*Love of long duration. Not the spark. The keeping.*
**Signature · move · on claim.** If the card the crown stands beside is taken, the crown steps to
stay beside it.

### 18 · the heart — al-Qalb — 8/7
*Antares, red in the scorpion's chest. Aim the heart and it lands.*
**Signature · move · on tap.** Once a board, move the heart to any open station on the road.

### 19 · the root — al-Shawla, Skt Mūla — 7/7
*Where others skim, you dig to the cause. The truth at the root can sting.*
**Signature · turn · on lodge.** The root turns one card beside it to show its other face, whichever
face that is.

### 20 · the flock — al-Naʿāʾim — 7/6
*Four ostriches walking to the river and four walking back. Winter is survived together.*
**Signature · move · on lodge.** The flock and the card beside it swap stations as it lands.

# GENBU · the black tortoise
*North. Winter. Water. What is worth more, and what belongs to whom.*

### 21 · the empty district — al-Balda — 3/8
*The station the old astronomers left blank. The centre of the galaxy is inside it.*
**Signature · worth · always.** The empty district counts two, and counts three while a station
beside it is empty.

### 22 · the listener — Saʿd al-Dhābiḥ, Skt Śravaṇa — 7/5
*To listen until the pattern shows itself. The listener is rarely the one heard.*
**Signature · worth · at the count.** The listener counts one more for every card standing beside it.

### 23 · the drum — Saʿd Bulaʿ — 5/7
*You set the tempo of the room you are in. A drummer does not get to be off beat.*
**Signature · owner · at the count.** One card beside the drum counts for the drum's side, whoever
is holding it.

### 24 · the void — Saʿd al-Suʿūd — 9/3
*Luck of lucks. An opening is not a wound. Extremely good at holding space.*
**Signature · worth · at the count.** The void counts three while nothing stands beside it, and one
otherwise.

### 25 · the hideaway — Saʿd al-Akhbiya — 6/6
*Keeper of safe places. Everyone's shelter, nobody's guest.*
**Signature · owner · at the count.** The hideaway counts nothing for itself. Both stations beside it
count one more for whoever holds them.

### 26 · the chamber — al-Fargh al-Muqaddam — 7/6
*Go still and deep enough to hold weight. Foundations are under the house.*
**Signature · worth · at the count.** The chamber counts two while it has never changed hands.

### 27 · the guide — al-Fargh al-Muʾakhkhar — 6/6
*You want to see everyone home. You stopped choosing a destination a while ago.*
**Signature · owner · at the count.** While the guide stands, every card on its own mansion counts
for the side that lodged it, whoever holds it now.

## What this fixes

**Every card is one class and one trigger.** Two words describe any of them. Nothing needs a clause.

**The redundancy is gone.** The three identical counters are now the empty district (counts by
emptiness), the listener (counts by company) and the chamber (counts by never having fallen). Three
different questions. The ghost and the glance were the same card and are now a raise and a strike.
The turning and the veil were the same card and are now a strike and a turn.

**Four ownership rules became one class with three members**, all in Genbu, all read at the count,
all announced by the tortoise on the frame. Silence is gone. The mane no longer reassigns anything.
The thread no longer takes the ends.

**The quadrant is legible in play.** Tiger cards refuse. Bird cards reach. Dragon cards shift. Tortoise
cards are read at the end. By level three every card you own is doing its animal's work, so the frame
colour stops being decoration.

## What still needs doing

**Measure all twenty-eight.** The bar is +1 on both faces. Anything under it is a card whose level-2
choice is fake, and the target is that most of them sit near the bar rather than far above it.

**The three I would watch.** The listener counting by company could run away on a crowded road. The
guide's ownership rule is the hardest to read on the board and may be the one that has to go. The
heart's tap is the only signature that is a tap rather than a trigger, which makes it the strongest
and possibly the wrong shape.

**Level-1 numbers are unchanged and should be re-checked once the signatures land**, because the old
numbers were balanced against the old abilities.

### Two changes to the engine this needs

1. **Delete the tie cascade.** Measured: better on every axis, and several of these signatures are
   built assuming a calmer board.
2. **The Genbu grant** is now "counts one more" rather than "returns to hand." The old one measured
   as inert.
